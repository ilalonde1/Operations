==============================================================================================
1. WHAT THIS TOOL IS SUPPOSED TO BE
==============================================================================================

THE SITUATION IT EXISTS FOR

  A structural engineer is designing a concrete tower. To analyse the lateral system she needs the
  geometry in ETABS: every shear wall, every column, every slab, on every level. On a 45-storey
  building that is roughly 1,750 walls and 1,487 columns.

  Today a person builds that by hand — opens ETABS, sets up the storeys, and draws it. Days to
  weeks of mouse work.

  None of it is engineering. It is transcription. The building already exists, fully drawn, in the
  structural plans drafting has already produced. Somebody is retyping a document that already
  exists, into a different program, by clicking.

  This tool removes the retyping. That is its entire function.

WHAT IT IS GIVEN

  1. The folder of plan DXFs drafting already exports for that job — one per level.
  2. Any ETABS file from the same job, even an empty shell. That is where the storey names,
     elevations, units, materials and grid come from.

  Nothing made specially for it. No per-job configuration files.

  IMPORTANT AND EASY TO GET WRONG: the input is not "DXF files" generically. It is the specific
  artifact drafting already produces for that job. This tool sits inside an existing workflow
  between drafting and engineering; it does not get to redefine what drafting hands over.

WHAT IT RETURNS

  - An ETABS .e2k carrying: walls at their true drawn thickness, on their centrelines, and
    CONNECTED so they transfer force; columns sized and oriented as drawn; floor plates; headers
    over openings; shaft and stair openings cut; a pier label on every wall.
  - A report stating, location by location, everything it could not do.
  - A workbook of the judgement calls it had to make, each answerable.

WHAT IT MUST NEVER DO

  - Loads, load assignment, diaphragms, stiffness modifiers, section properties, meshing, design.
    That half is the engineering and she wants to keep it.
  - Overwrite or duplicate geometry the engineer has already modelled. On 31138 it recognised 306
    of her walls and 316 of her columns and left every one alone.

THE THREE PROPERTIES THAT MAKE IT A TOOL RATHER THAN A RESCUE

  AGNOSTIC   Nothing in it knows about a particular building, office or drawing set. No job
             numbers, no per-project branches, no value tuned so one model comes out right.

  HONEST     Anything read and not modelled, discarded, skipped, deduplicated, substituted or
             fallen back on appears in the report. A correct decision made silently is
             indistinguishable from a bug.

  LEARNING   An engineer's answer becomes a rule applied to every job afterwards, with no code
             change and without her being asked twice. Rules and their evidence live in
             KorStandards; the code is the machine that applies them.

WHAT SUCCESS ACTUALLY MEANS — AND THE MEASURE THAT IS EASY TO MISS

  The goal is not "produce geometry". It is HOW MUCH OF HER CHECKING SHE CAN SKIP.

  A tool that is 95% right and quiet about the other 5% is worse than useless: she must verify
  every member to find the bad ones, which is the same work as drawing them, so she would be
  faster starting from nothing. The number that matters is not how many members were placed. It
  is whether the report can be trusted when it says "this is what I could not do."

  Judge everything below against that. An unreported approximation is a more serious defect than a
  reported failure.

4. ALREADY KNOWN — DO NOT SPEND TIME REDISCOVERING
==============================================================================================

THE FOUR FAULT CLASSES

  Geometry can be DROPPED, DOUBLED, MISPLACED or MISCLASSIFIED. Totals catch the first two and are
  blind to the last two: a member built on the wrong storey leaves every count healthy. 31168 once
  shipped with the ground floor of both towers empty and their 45 walls and 67 columns standing on
  the mezzanine, with no number wrong anywhere.

  Never verify output against the classifier that produced it.

ETABS .e2k FORMAT, LEARNED THE HARD WAY

  - Joints in POINT COORDINATES are PLAN ONLY.
  - A wall is  AREA "x" PANEL 4 "a" "b" "b" "a" n n 0 0  — the trailing integers are the storey
    span and offsets. The assigned storey is the TOP of the member; span n reaches n storeys down.
  - 0 0 0 0 is a spandrel/header.
  - 136 panels in the portfolio are 1 0 0 1 — skewed, corners at different storeys. The reader
    handles these; there is a test. Do not re-investigate.
  - Coincident joints are NORMAL, not a defect.
  - Site models interleave towers, so 2-inch and 8-inch sliver storeys are REAL.
  - The third value on a POINT line is an elevation above the storey base, NOT a member depth.
    The 88" opening height rests on that bad derivation and is a known open gap (L3).

KOR DRAFTING CONVENTIONS THE READER DEPENDS ON

  - A perimeter wall is drawn as two concentric rings and must be paired into one wall.
  - Drafting issues both a range sheet and a per-level sheet, so a floor arrives twice.
  - "P1" and "LEVEL P1" are the same storey; MEZZ reads as the level below.
  - Round columns are recognised only from arc provenance — a chamfered square passes every shape
    test a circle does.
  - Layer families: X and X-1 are one family and must be deduplicated per family, or the same
    edges build members twice.
  - Block INSERTs must be expanded or every block on the drawing is invisible.

THINGS ALREADY MEASURED AGAINST THE PORTFOLIO (1,126 engineer models, 145 projects)

  - Units: 936 models in inches, 159 in feet, 29 in millimetres, 2 in metres. Unit declaration is
    NOT a gap; do not re-scan for it.
  - Wall thickness cap was 36" and rejected 4,681 real wall sections; corrected to 60".
  - Column aspect <= 3.0 rejects 17.9% of 7,538 real column sections. The extremes are blade
    columns drawn deliberately across 15 projects. This is a KNOWN OPEN GAP and it is delicate:
    the engineer explicitly ruled 3.0, and her own model's most slender column is exactly 3:1. Do
    not silently overrule an engineer with a statistic.

THE REVIT SIDE

  - A raw Revit view export carries the LINKED ARCHITECTURAL MODEL. Concrete walls land on A-WALL
    among partitions, curtain wall and doors, where no layer pattern can separate them. The link
    category must be hidden in the view before exporting. This was discovered tonight and is why
    the third building is not yet testable.
  - Layer patterns are per-JOB, not global. Moving them into the database made them visible, not
    agnostic — setting a global rule for one job breaks the others. Hence the CLI overrides.

ENVIRONMENT

  - Get-ChildItem -Recurse over SMB is unusably slow. Corpus work runs ON the file server.
    \\Kor-fs01\Projects\Projects is E:\Projects\Projects there.
  - FS01 has no .NET runtime. A self-contained single-file publish runs there without installing.
  - WinRM is blocked on KOR-302N. It works on Kor-fs01, where Invoke-CimMethod also works.
  - The full suite takes 10-14 minutes because ~20 tests rebuild both reference buildings from real
    drawings over SMB. Do not start one and then keep editing — it locks the output directory and
    leaves test hosts wedged in SMB I/O.
  - Edge silently caches file:// URLs, so a rebuilt PDF can carry the old text at a plausible new
    size. Format-BdWebPdf.ps1 now renders through a fresh filename.
  - sqlcmd input files need a UTF-8 BOM.
  - A missing rule stops a production run by design; there is no fallback value.
  - analysis.vw_RuleSetting must have ONE active row per key. Importing an engineer's answer
    retires the matching convention in the same transaction.

==============================================================================================

  "less than 48 in length should be a column"
  "some walls are thicker than 24 inches"
  "the wall should stop at the opening; a header may be over the opening"
  "all walls should be assigned a pier label"
  "we can't have a wall go from here to here and then another one from here to here — we need a
   connection"                                    (connectivity is the whole point)
  "let's have a full model with both towers modelled; I will separate the towers later"
  loads, diaphragms, stiffness modifiers and section properties are hers

  These are banked in KorStandards at engineer-confirmed, the highest confidence there is. If the
  portfolio disagrees with one, that is a finding to report, not a value to change.

7. RULES OF EVIDENCE
==============================================================================================

  1. Measure the artifact. The report, the workbook, the documents and the suite are outputs of
     the thing under audit, not evidence about it.
  2. Read shipped PDFs as text, not the HTML behind them.
  3. Objects named "K" plus a letter and digits were written by this tool, and some files in the
     job folders are its own output round-tripped through ETABS. Establish provenance.
  4. A check is proved by failing on the defect it exists for, not by passing.
  5. Two buildings are not evidence about a third.
  6. State the file and the numbers behind every finding. A defect read off code is not a finding
     until you say what it does to a model an engineer opens.
  7. Where you cannot determine something, say so and say what would settle it.
