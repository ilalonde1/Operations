WHAT THE CHANGE DOES

  A wall is read by pairing its two faces. Closed outlines go to WallOutlineDecomposer, and open
  chains are handed to it ONE CHAIN AT A TIME, so a wall is only found where a single chain traced
  both of its faces.

  Drafting does not promise that. On 31065's ground floor the exterior wall arrives as 19 open
  chains on JBP_WALL_EXTERIOR, none of which close, and the two faces of one wall sit in DIFFERENT
  chains. Eight panels were recovered from seventeen outlines and the storey came out with 56% of
  the wall length the engineer's own model has -- the worst level in that building.

  Pooled across chains, those faces pair immediately and believably: fifteen pairs at 9.8" and
  11.8" (her own walls there are 10 to 16), the largest running 85 feet. The patch adds
  PairOpenFaces, which does that pairing. L1 goes from 56% to 82% of her wall length.

THE PROBLEM

  ModelCoverageTests.EveryDrawnMemberIsModelledOrAlreadyThere is a ratchet: it counts drawn
  members that were read and then neither modelled nor already present in the engineer's model,
  and that count MAY ONLY COME DOWN. With the patch applied:

      31138 2170 W 1st    0 lost   (ceiling 0)   -- fine
      31168 YMCA Langara  10 lost  (ceiling 7)   -- FAILS

  All ten are on ONE sheet: "S2-22-1_3_LEVEL 35 PLAN - CONCRETE OUTLINE - BLDG A".

MEASURED ALREADY -- do not spend the night re-deriving this

  - Without the patch, 31168 loses 7, and those 7 are the SAME sheet. The patch adds 3 more of
    what is already there rather than a new kind of fault.
  - That sheet classifies 8 walls and 2 columns and places on exactly 1 storey. ALL TEN are
    dropped by the composer. Meanwhile 7 unrelated member assignments sit on A-LEVEL 35 from
    other sheets. Run it yourself with scratch script run31168-full.ps1 (see below) -- the sheet
    table line is "--Structural Plan - S2-22-1_3_LEVEL 35 PLAN - CON...  1  1  8  2  0".
  - A code comment in DxfToEtabsService already records this fault from before the patch: "On
    31168 the tower A level 35 plan reported five walls and two columns against its name in the
    sheet table and put none of them in the model, because a sheet read earlier had already
    claimed those places." Five walls plus two columns is exactly the 7.

  THREE THINGS TRIED THAT DID NOT MOVE THE NUMBER. Do not repeat them:

    1. Dedupe the pooled wall against walls already in result.Walls by both endpoints rounded to
       the inch, in either order, thickness excluded -- the same key E2kGeometryComposer uses.
       31138 went 35 -> 30; 31168 stayed at 10.
    2. Also skip a pooled wall whose midpoint lies within MinWallThickness of an existing result
       wall's segment, to catch a pair lying ALONG a panel rather than exactly on it. No change.
    3. Pool only the chains the per-chain decomposition got nothing from, so a chain already read
       is never offered twice. No change: still 10.

  So the three extra are not duplicates of anything the per-chain pass produced. They are three
  more walls on a sheet whose members are all dropped for a reason that predates this change.
