How a pursuit lives

1 · GRAB

Claim it

Grab Opportunities → double-click to scout the buyer → Grab selected. It becomes your Drafting pursuit.

2 · WORK

Nudge it along

Log the odd call, send a fee proposal. The buyer's history, warmth and plan-takers are already on the screen.

3 · CLOSE

Call the outcome

Set stage → Won (link the project) or Lost (who beat us). The one thing only you know.
