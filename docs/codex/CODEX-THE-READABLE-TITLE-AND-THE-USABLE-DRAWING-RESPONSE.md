# Response — the readable title and the usable drawing

There is no safe automatic pairing rule in these five files. They establish where a title and geometry came from within a sheet, but do not establish that an `S0.00` view refers to a particular `S2.xx.y` drawing.

**1. What is available at line 689?** The composer has filenames, access to each file’s raw DXF lines, and `PlanSheetInfo` parsed from the filename. Its in-memory input is also reduced to names and DXF lines. There is no explicit source-to-destination drawing relationship in that handoff. See `Kor.Operations.EngineeringTools.Core/Dxf/DxfToEtabsService.cs:678` and `Kor.Operations.EngineeringTools.Core/Dxf/PlanSheetNaming.cs:6`.

The filename carries a sheet number, not a PDF page number; its view index is local to that sheet. `SheetViews` orders titles left to right and assigns `k + 1`, so `_3_` cannot identify a corresponding drawing elsewhere. Source-page context exists while splitting, but the resulting `Part` holds only the view title/position, filename, and geometry—no destination sheet reference. See `Kor.Operations.EngineeringTools.Core/Intake/SheetDxfName.cs:94`, `Kor.Operations.EngineeringTools.Core/Intake/SheetViews.cs:110`, `Kor.Operations.EngineeringTools.Core/Intake/SheetViews.cs:373`, and `Kor.Operations.EngineeringTools.Core/Intake/SheetViews.cs:33`.

Geometry extents are calculated locally to distribute grid axes among views; they are not cross-sheet identifiers. At line 689, geometry, layers, and named axes have not yet been decoded into the later working collections: segments arrive at line 1165, positioned tags and named axes at lines 1195–1196. Sheet subdivisions also defeat one-entry-per-file assumptions: the existing code documents `.1` and `.2` as two areas of one drawing. See `Kor.Operations.EngineeringTools.Core/Dxf/LevelsFromSheetOrder.cs:21`.

None is established by this handoff. Any future rule still needs the brief’s 48-set comparison before adoption.
