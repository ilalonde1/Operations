PART A — WHAT ELSE WILL ETABS REFUSE?

A1. GEOMETRY THAT IS LEGAL TO A SUMMARY AND ILLEGAL TO A SOLVER. KF54 was one shape. Enumerate the
    others and CHECK FOR EACH in both files:
      - a ring that touches itself at a point (a figure-eight) without properly crossing
      - three or more collinear joints where the path reverses (the KF54 shape) — now removed for
        FLOOR and opening AREA objects; is anything else written as a ring?
      - a ring wound clockwise where ETABS wants anticlockwise, or mixed winding between a plate
        and the opening cut from it
      - an opening whose ring is not entirely inside its plate
      - joints repeated non-adjacently in one ring
      - a ring whose joints are not coplanar once storey elevations are applied
      - an AREA whose joint COUNT field disagrees with the joints listed
      - a joint referenced by an object and never defined, or defined twice
      - a section, pier or spandrel referenced by an assign and never defined
      - degenerate area: three joints that are collinear, or an area below a square foot

A2. THE STOREY STACK. Read STORY heights and elevations in both files and against the reference.
      - any storey of zero or negative height, or one shorter than a person
      - the base elevation, and whether the lowest storey absorbs an implausible distance
      - a member assigned to a storey the file does not define
      - the same object assigned twice to one storey

A3. WALL PANELS specifically. A wall is four joints, two at the bottom of a plan line and two at
    the top. Verify that is true of every KW in both files: two distinct plan points, the right
    repetition, and a section that exists. A panel written some other way is one ETABS will drop.
