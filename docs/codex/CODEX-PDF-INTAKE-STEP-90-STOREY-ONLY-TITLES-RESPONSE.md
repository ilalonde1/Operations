# Codex response — step 90 review (2026-09-16 09:10), and what was done with it

**Codex's answer**: a wall-elevation sheet titled only "LEVEL 2 - 5" is typed plan — LEVEL a joining word, 2 and 5
storey tokens, "-" a joining word — and "the title alone cannot distinguish this elevation sheet from a plan";
"LEVEL 3 LOADS" is refused (LOADS is neither).

**Decision: NOT adopted — the limit is stated, and measured.** The construction needs an elevation sheet titled by
a level range and nothing else. On run 25's ledger (8,650 pages, 296 sets) the rule typed 116 pages on 26 sets plan by
a storey-only title, listed in §107: "LEVEL 2" … "LEVEL 38", "PARKING LEVEL P1", "LEVEL 13 - 21", "LEVEL 15 / ROOF",
"LEVEL 1 MEZZANINE", "MECHANICAL ROOF LEVEL", "GROUND FLOOR" — every one a plan (they are run 25's storey gains:
30807 8 → 38, 30823 9 → 26 plates, 30878 5 → 17, 50046 14 → 18). Not one elevation sheet: on these sets an elevation
sheet says ELEVATION in its title, and SECTION|ELEVATION is asked before this rule. A sheet that names an elevation
by a level range alone is a title the office has not written in 296 sets; if one appears, its geometry (no ring, no
columns) is the composer's refusal, and the title rule's limit is written here.
