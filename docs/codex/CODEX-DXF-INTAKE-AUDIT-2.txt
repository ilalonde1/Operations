PART A — ARE THE FIXES REAL?

A1. TAG THICKNESS was rewritten from plate-first to tag-first
    (StructuralPlanClassifier, the block beginning "THE THICKNESS THE DRAWING PRINTS INSIDE THE
    PLATE"). Claims: a tag belongs to the SMALLEST plate containing it; a tag inside an opening
    prices nothing; two different values in one plate are refused, not guessed between.
    ASK: nested plates three deep. A tag on the boundary of two plates. A plate that contains a
    tag but is later dropped (flood-fill "same floor read twice") — does its thickness leak?
    Openings are consulted via result.Openings — is that list complete at that point in Classify?

A2. THE ORPHANED-OPENING FIX removes openings whose centroid lies in a dropped plate
    (StructuralPlanClassifier, "AN OPENING GOES WITH THE PLATE IT WAS CUT FROM").
    ASK: centroid-in-polygon is not containment. What real opening sits with its centroid inside a
    dropped ring but belongs to the survivor? Is the reverse case handled — the survivor dropped
    and the other kept?

A3. NON-STRUCTURAL SHEET PATTERNS (dxf.non-structural-sheet-patterns, migration 053) filters the
    folder by substring on the FILE NAME, applied in DxfToEtabsService after ApplyRules.
    ASK: the list is REINFORC;REBAR;KEY PLAN;CORE WALL;MODEL SETTING;DESIGN LOAD;LOAD PLAN;
    SHORING;DEMO. What real structural plan does each pattern kill? "DEMO" inside "DEMOUNTABLE".
    A sheet legitimately called "LEVEL 5 - CORE WALL AND SLAB PLAN". Check it is applied
    everywhere a folder is read, not only in the main path — JobPublisher.ReachByStorey reads the
    same folder.

A4. REACH now loads the ruled options (JobPublisher.PlanRulesFor). It swallows every exception and
    falls back to defaults.
    ASK: is silent fallback right here? A wrong-but-present connection, a retired setting, a
    permissions change — reach quietly runs on built-ins and the published split is wrong with
    nothing said. Argue both sides and land somewhere.

A5. InUnitOf now converts four more lengths, and RulesTravelBetweenUnitsTests checks by reflection.
    ASK: what does the reflection test NOT see? Non-double length fields, lengths inside nested
    records or collections, and the NotALength allow-list — is every entry on it genuinely unitless?

A6. ShippedModelsAgreeWithEachOtherTests compares the two published .e2k artifacts.
    ASK: it compares area and thickness per shared storey. What disagreement does that MISS —
    member counts, wall thicknesses, opening counts, storey elevations, plate POSITION? Two models
    can agree on total area per storey and be different buildings.
