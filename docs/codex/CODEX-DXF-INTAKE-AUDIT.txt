WHAT WAS CLAIMED. Test each claim against the code, not against the commit message.

1. SLAB VERSUS HOLE. Three new rules decide whether a ring inside a floor is an opening:
   - a "doubled edge": parallel within dxf.doubled-edge-parallel-ratio AND covering more than
     dxf.doubled-edge-coverage of the plate  (StructuralPlanClassifier.SplitSlabsAndOpenings)
   - a ring whose edge lies ON the plate's edge for >= 50% of its length (FractionOnBoundary)
   - an opening not cut where a plate on the SAME STOREY from a DIFFERENT SHEET stands on the
     same ground: area within 2%, centre within 24 in  (E2kGeometryComposer, PlateFillingThisOpening)
   ASK: what real opening does each of these delete? Construct the drawing that defeats it. A
   lift core running the full height of a small floor plate; a light well; a stair opening in a
   narrow podium; two genuinely different floors that happen to have equal area. The engineer
   REJECTED a model on 25 August for inverting slab and opening -- the failure mode here is
   deleting a hole she drew, and it is invisible in every count.

2. PER-PLATE THICKNESS. PlanLoop.ThicknessInchesFromTag, set in the classifier from a call-out
   inside the ring, preferred by the composer over the per-sheet stick-file value.
   ASK: the bounds are 4..120 inches and the parser requires the word SLAB -- what real call-out
   is refused, and what non-slab text is accepted? Check the UNIT path especially: the tag is in
   inches, ComposeOptions.ModelUnitInInches divides it, and ComposeOptions.InUnitOf() scales other
   lengths. Is ModelUnitInInches ever scaled by InUnitOf, or set before it? A units error here is
   silent and ships a 12 inch slab as 1 foot thick or 144.
   Also: "smallest enclosing plate wins" -- what happens when two plates of EQUAL area both
   contain the tag?

3. THE REQUIRED-RULE LIST. RequiredRuleKeys grew from 35 to 43 keys, and RequiredRuleCoverageTests
   claims to prove that every rule the code READS is one the run REQUIRES, using a recording
   dictionary passed to ApplyRules.
   ASK: does the recorder actually observe every read? Are there settings read anywhere OTHER
   than the two ApplyRules methods -- in the classifier, the composer, the publisher, the CLI?
   Those would be invisible to this test. Name any you find.

4. THE QUESTION GATE. RulingCoverageTests.NoQuestionIsAskedOnATopicSheHasAlreadyRuledOn builds a
   report carrying every flag and fails if a NEEDS YOU row's topic is a banked ruling.
   ASK: is the synthetic report REALISTIC -- do those flag strings match what the composer
   actually emits today, string for string? If a flag's wording changed, the question it triggers
   would silently stop being generated and this test would still pass. Check each flag literal in
   the test against its producer.

5. THE BRIDGE EXPORT. exportdxf now takes "setup" and "viewcontains", forces SharedCoords,
   Polymesh and TextTreatment.Exact over the setup, and names files for the VIEW.
   ASK: forcing three options over a saved setup -- what does Polymesh do to a wall or a curved
   slab edge that the setup's own solid mode would not? Is SafeName(view.Name) safe against a view
   name containing a path separator, a leading dot, or a name longer than MAX_PATH once joined?
   What happens when two views differ only by characters SafeName replaces?
