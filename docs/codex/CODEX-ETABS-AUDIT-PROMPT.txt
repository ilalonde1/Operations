ETABS model (.e2k) for an engineer to import. Find what is wrong with it.

THE STANDARD

  A structural engineer imports the .e2k into ETABS and looks at it. Anything that would make
  her distrust the model is a finding. She is a real reviewer with limited patience and this is
  not her first look — a fault handed back twice costs more than a fault found late.

  Judge the OUTPUT against the DRAWINGS. Both are on the share and both are readable as text.

RULES OF EVIDENCE — these matter more than coverage

  1. Do not treat the tool's own report, dossier, one-pager or test suite as evidence that
     anything is true. They are claims by the thing under audit. Check them.

  2. Do not treat a file in those folders as an engineer's work because of where it sits or what
     it is called. Some are this tool's own output round-tripped through ETABS. Establish
     provenance before you use anything as ground truth. A model full of names beginning K
     followed by digits was written by this tool.

  3. Where you assert a defect, give the file and the numbers you measured. "This looks wrong"
     is not a finding. Neither is a defect derived only from reading code — say what it does to
     the model.

  4. If you cannot determine something, say so and say what would settle it. A confident wrong
     answer is the most expensive thing you can produce here.

WHAT I AM MOST AFRAID OF, in order

  1. That it is a one-off. This must be a general tool for any job with drawings, not a machine
     tuned until two specific buildings come out right. Look for rules, thresholds, layer names,
     storey names or shapes that only make sense for these two jobs, and for anything that would
     silently produce a wrong model on a third building rather than refusing.

  2. Geometry that is wrong in a way no count can see — a member on the wrong storey, at the
     wrong size, of the wrong kind, or in a place the drawings do not show it. Totals agreeing
     with each other proves nothing.

  3. Anything the tool discards, skips or fails to read WITHOUT SAYING SO. Silence is the fault.

  4. Statements in the shipped documents that are not true of the shipped models.

DO NOT

  - Do not build, run tests, or run the publish script. Read and measure only.
  - Do not modify any file on the \\Kor-fs01 share. Those are live project files.
  - Do not fix anything. Report.

OUTPUT

  A list of findings, worst first. For each: what is wrong, the evidence you measured, what it
  would cost the engineer, and how confident you are.

  Then, separately: go back over your own findings and try to knock each one down. Say which
  survive. I would rather have four findings that hold than twenty that read well.
