## 4 — Alberta

**108 unclaimed · 80 named (74%) · avg 29.5 — all of it new since 8 October**

Alberta had **zero** early-signal coverage until yesterday. Calgary's land-use redesignation
feed is now live: **864 rezonings, 82% naming the applicant** — the highest naming rate of any
feed we hold.

| Filed | What | Who filed it |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-07 | Land Use Amendment → **M-C1** | Anatolian Turkish Islamic Centre of Calgary |
| 2026-10-06 | Land Use Amendment → **M-H1** | **SPARKS PLANNING GROUP** |
| 2026-10-05 | Land Use Amendment → **R-CG** | **HORIZON LAND SURVEYS** |
| 2026-10-05 | Land Use Amendment → **MU-1** | **URBAN SYSTEMS** |
| 2026-10-05 | Land Use Amendment → **H-GO** | **HORIZON LAND SURVEYS** |

**Read the district code — it tells you the building.** `M-*` is multi-residential, `MU-*` is
mixed use, `H-GO` is high-density, `R-CG` is rowhouse infill. Those are the ones worth a call.

**Do this:** the planning firms are the relationship. **Horizon Land Surveys, Sparks Planning
Group, Urban Systems, O2 Planning and Design, CivicWorks** file repeatedly. One conversation
with a firm that files thirty rezonings a year is worth thirty cold calls.

⚠ This is **rezoning only** — earlier than a permit, which is why the applicant is named.
Calgary's development permits (193,000 of them) and Edmonton are not wired yet.
