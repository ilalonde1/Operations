# BC Design-Build Prequalification — What KOR Needs to Win Big-Fee Work

**Prepared 2026-06-21** · answers the meeting actionable §1 #5 (raised by Rory): what lists/quals does KOR need to play on big-fee design-build, and can a contact sponsor us in? All claims web-sourced (URLs inline).

## The core reality
KOR does **not** need its own prequalification with Infrastructure BC or any crown body to play on $500M+ design-build. **The SE rides the team** — the GC and lead architect are the named principals at shortlisting; the structural engineer is named/scored later in the RFP via key-personnel CVs. The whole game is **being inside the right team before the RFQ drops.** This confirms KOR's prime-consultant strategy: target the architects.

## How BC major design-build is procured
**Infrastructure BC** (ex-Partnerships BC) runs procurement for ministries/health authorities on capital projects >~$50M. Sequence: **RFQ** (GC + lead architect submit quals; ~3 teams shortlisted; SE not separately named) → **RFP** (key personnel incl. SE named/scored) → award. **Progressive Design-Build (PDB)** is the dominant model on current BC healthcare (Richmond Hospital Ph 2/3, Stuart Lake, Fort St. James); the **Alliance** model (Burnaby Hospital Ph 2) is even more relationship-driven. *(infrastructurebc.com)*

## Qualification rosters — ranked for an SE subconsultant
**Tier 1 (high, actionable now)**
- **PHSA Annual Notice of Intent (NOI)** — PHSA Supply Chain runs prequalified design-consultant lists for **all 5 BC health authorities**. Once/year it posts an NOI on BC Bid. **Single highest-leverage registration.** Monitor BC Bid year-round; apply the moment it posts.
- **Fraser Health Prequalified Engineers List** — mandatory for acute-care capital >$5M; refreshed via BC Bid/Bonfire. **Action: email FMP@fraserhealth.ca** about current roster + refresh cycle + how to apply; register on Bonfire.
- **BC Bid keyword alerts** — school districts & municipalities issue 3-year structural RFPQ rosters constantly (e.g., SD38 Richmond, seismic-heavy, 5 firms, $100K–$50M). Each win is a reference multiplier. Set alerts: "structural engineering RFPQ," "seismic assessment prequalification," "structural engineer qualified supplier."

**Tier 2 (moderate):** MOTI issues project-specific RFQs (no Ontario-style central registry); Infrastructure BC MULRFQs are advisory-only (not SE).
**Tier 3 (low/NA):** BC Housing (no SE prequal), BC Ferries (naval/mech/elec only — not building structural), TransLink (no standing SE roster).

## Does KOR need to register with Graham/PCL/EllisDon?
**No formal GC SE panels exist.** SE selection sits with the **lead architect**, not the GC. The path onto Graham's Richmond Hospital team was **through HDR**, not Graham's procurement office. Cold-approaching a GC for an "SE panel" isn't a real mechanism.

## Can a ministry/client contact sponsor KOR in?
**Informal influence, not a formal bypass.** A program manager can't unilaterally add a firm mid-cycle, but a warm health-authority capital-projects relationship gives **early warning** (when to apply) and **scoring influence** on the evaluation panel. Worth cultivating; not an access shortcut.

## Realistic path for a challenger SE
Fraser Health / PHSA list → reference projects on $5M–$50M health work → principal-level intros to **Parkin / HDR / ZGF** → ride onto a PDB RFP team → win a mid-scale PDB in **Interior/Northern Health** (lower competition) → named reference → eligible for Metro Van $500M+ teams. RJC's dominance took 20+ years; the door is mid-scale PDB in the regions.
