KOR Structural · Standard Details · for drafters

Quick Start

Start every job from the master. Pull details and parts from the palette. Everything you can place has been approved — nothing else shows up.

How a job starts

1 · START

Copy the master

Open the master from Drafting → KOR-Standards → template → MASTER and Save As into your job folder. Then delete what the job doesn't need — a concrete job drops the wood framing. Never work in the master itself; it's read-only.

2 · PLACE

Pull a detail

In Revit: KOR Tools tab → Insert panel → KOR Details. Search or pick a discipline, double-click — the detail view is copied into your job.

3 · PARTS

Drop a part

Same panel → Quick Insert. The families you place come from the same approved list, so the part in your job is the part everyone else uses.

Cheat sheet — I want to…

Start a new jobOpen KOR-Standards-Master-R25.rvt → Save As into the job → strip the disciplines you don't need. Don't start from an old job.
Place a detailKOR Tools → Insert → KOR Details → type a number or title, or click a discipline → double-click the row. It reports the view it placed.
Place a partKOR Tools → Insert → Quick Insert → pick a category → pick the type.
Find its numberEvery standard detail is KOR-D-#####, on the view's View Prefix. Quote that number when you ask about one.
The detail I need isn't listedIt isn't approved yet. Draw it in your job as you would today, then send the number and the job to the gatekeeper — approved details get brought into the master, and then everyone has it.
A detail is wrongSay so to the gatekeeper with the KOR-D number. It gets fixed once, in the master, and the fix reaches the whole office. Don't fix it locally and leave it.
The palette is empty or errorsIt reads the standards database live. If it can't reach it, it says so — try again, and tell IT if it keeps happening. Your job file is untouched either way.
I want a PDF of detailsThat's the Operations app, not Revit — engineers use it to review and mark up. Ask if you need a set.

What everything means

Master — the Revit template every job starts from. Approved content only.
KOR Details — the palette that copies one approved detail into your job.
Quick Insert — the approved families (replaces the old QuickPick).
KOR-D-##### — a detail's permanent number. Use it when you talk about one.
Gatekeeper — the one person who edits the standards and publishes the master.

The master is read-only to everyone but the gatekeeper. Copying it is a read — that always works.

The bridge — gatekeeper only

Publishing the master needs Revit. That happens through a small add-in — the bridge — installed on the gatekeeper's own machine. The Operations app hands it the job; their Revit does the work.

It isn't tied to a person. To move it, run install\install-bridge.ps1 on the new machine with Revit closed, then point the app's StandardDetails.BridgeRoot at it. Run the same script with -Uninstall on the old one.

Per-user, nothing machine-wide, no ribbon button, no share. It only works while that person's Revit is open — which is the only time it's needed.
