## 1. STRATEGY — greenfield, position 2–3 years out
KOR has **no interior track record** — this is a relationship-build market, not a fee market (warm fee ~$15M, mostly civic). **Don't spend pursuit budget on interior bids this quarter.** Instead, seed two anchor relationships now so KOR is on the team when the real seats procure in 2027–28:
- **UBC Okanagan** (Aaron Mogerman) — 3 open buildings ($120M housing + $32M FIRE + $33M MPT-MOT). Treat as one campus account; the FIRE facility's complexity (combustion chamber, wind tunnel) is where early structural value is highest and a generalist incumbent is weakest.
- **City of Kelowna** (Mac Logan) — the only existing KOR tie; the Performing Arts Centre ($220M) is the flagship, and Revery (concept architect) is the inside track to get on the design team.

## 2. DISPLACEMENT — the wall is RJC
**RJC Engineers runs a Kelowna office and owns the interior institutional incumbency** (ONE Water Street, The Exchange mass-timber, Okanagan College, UBCO Nechako, multiple K-12). They are the firm to get around. The route is **architects, not owners**: target Kelowna-active architects *not* already defaulted to RJC or ROV — Revery (Performing Arts), Diamond Schmitt (large institutional), Meiklejohn (local civic). ROV is the local high-rise/mass-timber specialist but is thinner institutionally — that's the gap. Concede nothing yet; there's no KOR position to defend, only ground to take.

## 3. CREDENTIAL HONESTY
KOR has no Okanagan reference. Any interior institutional pursuit needs a teaming arrangement with a Kelowna architect for a first credit. Manage the founder (Bryson) EGBC-seismic disclosure proactively if seismic comes up — though the interior is lower-seismic than the coast, so it's less central here than on the Island.

## 4. NEW MONACO — the long game
The dormant $1B Peachland marquee (AMC Developments). It cleared its highway-access blocker (Feb 2025) but has no permits or design team; SE engagement is 12–24 months out *if* the Preliminary Layout Review completes. **Build the AMC relationship now, while it's quiet and uncontested** (Paul Tsang, Chairman; David Burnside, Chief Dev & Investment Officer) — when it moves, it's a multi-year flagship and the early relationship wins it. Monitor for the PLR/development-permit milestone as the trigger to escalate.
