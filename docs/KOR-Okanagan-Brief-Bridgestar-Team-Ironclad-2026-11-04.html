2 · Team Construction: useful, but a channel

What it is. Kelowna construction manager, founded 1981, about 16 staff (Apollo). Offers design-build, construction management, GMP and stipulated sum across multi-family, commercial, institutional and wineries. Apollo reports it has its own framing and concrete crews.

Why it ranks second. It doesn't own the buildings, so on most jobs the owner chooses the engineer. Design-build and development management are the exceptions, where Team could bring its consultants (my read). Its "latest venture" is a six-storey building near Kelowna General Hospital: 40 rentals and about 6,500 sq ft of medical office, status and team unstated.

3 · Ironclad: assume the seat is closed

Why. It runs land, design, engineering, construction and rentals itself, and says its buildings are designed by its "technicians and engineers". Apollo lists Andrew Meads as Lead Structural Engineer since 2022 in Winnipeg, about 6 people in engineering, and about 130 staff. Founder Ryan Van Damme and VP Steve Van Mil both came from Seymour Pacific.

What could change that. Apollo also lists Meads as current at DGH Engineering, and I could not confirm who seals BC buildings. If it is an outside BC engineer, there may be an opening. That is the one question worth asking, and Janet Sokolowski, COO and a P.Eng, formerly its VP of Pre-Construction and Design, is the person to ask.

Scale and local work. About 5,000 units across five provinces (press, October 2025). Okanagan projects on its site: 2241 Springfield Rd (401 units, six storeys) and The Aurora in Kelowna; Lakeview Pointe in West Kelowna; Rockwood Landing and The Highland in Vernon. Sources conflict on status, so confirm before citing.

Be aware. A worker died at 2241 Springfield Rd on 14 June 2024. RCMP and WorkSafeBC investigated; I found no published outcome.
