# What the map says to fix — 2026-08-29

Read off `architecture.json`, then **verified** before being written down. The map reports 45 type
names declared in more than one project; a shared name is where to *look*, not a finding. Every item
below has had the two declarations pulled out of their files and compared as text.

Of 38 non-boilerplate duplicate names: **21 are 90%+ identical, 8 are 55–90%, 9 share only the
name.** 547 lines of verified duplication in the 90%+ group.

The similarity measure is now **in the tool** (`ArchDuplicate.Similarity`), not a script that was run
once, so every number here is reproducible and will move as the work is done. It began as a
throwaway Python check; the C# it became disagreed with that check on ten of thirty-eight types
until the comparison changed from lines to tokens — a one-line record differing by a field name
scores zero on lines and 96% on tokens, and 96% is the answer a person would give.

## 1. One systemic cause, not thirty separate mistakes

**A tool in `tools/` is written as a prototype, graduates into a `Kor.Opportunities.Worker` job, and
the prototype is never retired.** That single habit produces most of the duplication on the map.

| prototype | its Worker twin | shared type names | invoked by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `tools/BdDeltekLink` | `Services/BdDeltekLinkDryRunJob.cs` | **9** at 95–100% | **nothing** |
| `tools/BdSeedImport` | (Deltek path, via Worker) | 4 | **nothing** |
| `tools/ApcInterestBackfill` | `Services/ApcInterestEnrichmentJob.cs` | 2 at 96% | **nothing** |
| `tools/BdIntelExtract` | `Jobs/IntelExtractionCatchUpJob.cs` | 2 at 100% | `monitor-drains.ps1` |

`tools/BdDeltekLink` is mentioned in 17 files. **Thirteen are audit documents**; the other four are
the Worker's own twin job and its registration. No script, no config and no scheduled task runs it.

**The fix is not thirty extractions.** It is a rule: when a prototype graduates, it is deleted or it
becomes a thin caller of the shared code. Then the individual duplicates below stop reappearing.

> ⚠ `tools/BdIntelExtract` is the exception — it IS live, called from `monitor-drains.ps1`. Do not
> delete it. Its 6-line `EnrichmentRow` is the one to share.

The map counted only C# until now, which let it claim this system is 62 projects of one language.
It is not: **289 scripts live outside every project** — 190 PowerShell, 72 Python, 24 SQL — and
**179 of them are named by no other file in the repository.**

| where | unreferenced |
|---|---|
| `output/` | 73 |
| `tools/` | 66 |
| `_Scripts Rebuild/` | 19 |
| `docs/` | 12 |

Biggest: `tools/BdIntelGraphAudit.sql` (463 lines), `tools/extract_a233_14_equations.py` (363),
`tools/BdCanonicalDedup/Generate-FamilyPlan.ps1` (278).

Unreferenced does not mean dead — it means **dead, or run by a person from memory**, and those are
the two cases worth knowing about. `output/` is where to look first: 73 scripts in a folder whose
name says it holds output.

Two things are deliberately NOT counted, because counting them produced false findings:

- **294 numbered SQL migrations** under `Schema/`. A runner applies them in ordinal order and nothing
  names them; counting them reported 252 dead files that are the live BD schema.
- **Vendored PowerShell** (`PowerShellGet`, `PackageManagement`). Sixteen thousand lines of
  Microsoft's own code made the largest "unreferenced script in this repository" a file nobody here
  has ever opened.
