Business Development — Quick Start

Find it. Grab it. Work it. Close it. Everything below is in the KOR Operations app.

Two kinds of opportunity

A signal: somebody applying to build

A permit, rezoning or project announced months or years before any tender. The structural engineer is usually not chosen yet. Find who filed it and phone them.

A bid: somebody asking for prices

A tender with a deadline. The engineer is often chosen already, so decide quickly whether to bid. The score on each row is a hint, not a verdict.

The steps

1 · OPEN IT

Home › Business Development › left menu Opportunities. Three tabs: Signals, Bids, Mine. Newest first; work down from the top.

2 · GRAB IT

Tick To grab, select the row, press Grab selected. It is now yours, so nobody else phones the same person, and it moves to My Pursuits. Not for KOR? Not for us…

3 · LOG EVERY CONTACT

In My Pursuits, open it. In Activity log pick Call, Meeting or Email out, type one line on what happened, press Log. This is how the weekly list knows you have been in touch.

4 · NEXT STEP AND FEE

Next step…: what you will do and by when. Edit… › Proposed fee (CAD): a rough number is fine; without one the opportunity cannot be counted in the work ahead.

5 · THE DOCUMENTS

For Bids&Tenders tenders, the bid documents appear in Files by themselves, and new addenda are added daily until it closes. Add your own by dragging them onto Files or Browse….

6 · WRITING THE PROPOSAL

More ▾ › Checklist…: every requirement quoted from the RFP, checked against your draft. Empty? Ask Ian to load the RFP, or press Add the generic checklist.

7 · SUBMIT

Set stage › Submitted, then Browse… to the proposal PDF you sent; it is stored with the opportunity. The follow-ups start from that date: day 14, then day 45.

8 · CLOSE IT, WON OR LOST

Set stage › Won or Lost, with one line on why. For a loss, say who won (start typing the firm). This is how KOR finally learns its real win rate.

Every week: Ian's to-do list

An email from Ian with a numbered list: who to call, what to send, by when. Reply with one line per number; a new date moves your next step, the same as Next step….
