Where the cover sheets are

Nelson's master sheets are now in the catalogue: 57 general-notes and typical-detail sheets, in five collections, each with its drawing.

For Monday with Jim
Sheets tab, S1.03 selected. Left: the sheets under their collection. Right: the sheet as Nelson's master prints it, and the 30 details on it.

1Find a cover sheet

  1. Open KOR Operations → Standard Details.
  2. Click Sheets (top left, beside Details and Parts).
  3. Pick a collection: Concrete tower E, E1, D or Wood frame E1, D.

2Look at it

  1. Click the sheet. Its drawing shows on the right.
  2. Click the drawing to zoom, or Open PDF for the full sheet.

3Go to a detail on it

  1. Under the sheet's title: Details on this sheet.
  2. Click one. The app opens it in Details with its own drawing.

4Before the demo

  1. This is the new build. The app installed on staff PCs today shows an empty Sheets tab.
  2. Releasing the new build is your call.
