The standard details drafters place from Revit now come from your masters: your CAD typical-detail blocks and your Concrete Tower R24 template, in one file. Where a sheet of yours held a Revit copy of a CAD detail, it now holds the detail drawn from your CAD block, so a change you make in CAD is the one everybody gets. Wood framing: the one wood question

Ian has settled how wood framing is drawn. His words, 25 October:

"I think the issue is that until recently we were told woodframe couldn't be done in revit" · "If we CAN then 2D cad is redundant"

So framing plans show modelled members (beams, joists, posts, hold-downs, walls), with 2D for annotation only. Standard details, the typical sections and connections, stay 2D and are not affected. What it does not settle is which version of the "Level N showing Level N+1 framing over" sheet becomes the standard. Today there are three:
