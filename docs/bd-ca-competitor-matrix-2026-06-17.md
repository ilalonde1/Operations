## The LA read
LA's SE market is **tiered**: starchitect/supertall (Saiful Bouquet, TT, Arup, NYA), civic/institutional (JAMA, KPFF), seismic-heritage (Brandow & Johnston, NYA), and developer-multifamily (Englekirk/WSP, DCI, LFA). **KOR's lane is developer-residential/mixed-use multifamily** — won on seismic credibility + principal-led responsiveness against Englekirk/WSP, DCI, and LFA, not against the starchitect tier. The seismic-retrofit pool (see the seismic SE-demand map) is where KOR competes *directly* against Brandow & Johnston, NYA, and Miyamoto — and where KOR's PBSD depth shines.
