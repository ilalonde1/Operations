Business Development Quick Start

Find it. Grab it. Work it. Close it. Everything below is in the KOR Operations app.

New in this version

It opens on Today: what needs you, your list with dates, and pursuits waiting for a next step. One Grab button for tenders and for projects years out. The documents arrive by themselves, and Claude's review of the RFP lands on the pursuit. People by name everywhere. My Pursuits shows yours (turn off Mine for everyone's), each marked No RFP yet, Out for bid, Submitted, Won or Lost.

Two kinds of opportunity

Early: a project years out, or a permit

A project announced, a rezoning or a permit, long before any tender. The structural engineer is usually not chosen yet. Find who is behind it and phone them.

A bid: somebody asking for prices

A tender with a deadline. The engineer is often chosen already, so decide quickly whether to bid.

The steps

1 · OPEN IT

Home › Business Development. It opens on Today. Everything else is in the left menu; More ▾ holds the rest.

2 · FIND IT

Opportunities: Signals (permits, rezonings) and Bids (tenders, structural work only). Future Projects: projects years out, KOR's kind of work only.

3 · GRAB IT

Select the row and press Grab; the same button on Future Projects. It is now yours and moves to My Pursuits. Ian sees what is grabbed and may hand one on; you get an email when one is given to you. Not for KOR? Not for us…

4 · WHEN THE RFP POSTS

If you hold a future project and its tender is posted, the tender joins your pursuit, whoever pressed Grab. Today asks once: Yes, it's this project or Not this project (then it is theirs).

5 · LOG IT, SET THE NEXT STEP

In Activity log pick Call, Meeting or Email out, one line, Log. Then Next step…: what and by when. A future project with no next step and nothing done for 14 days goes back to the pool; the morning email warns you four days before.

6 · DOCUMENTS AND THE REVIEW

For Bids&Tenders and Alberta Purchasing tenders the bid documents appear in Files minutes after you grab, and addenda daily until it closes. When Claude has read the RFP, Open review shows bid or not and why, the questions for the buyer, and the checklist.

7 · CHECK IT AND PUT A FEE ON IT

Write it as you do now; tick it off against More ▾ › Checklist… (every RFP requirement). Put one rough number in Edit… › Proposed fee. Coming: one standard KOR proposal, built in the app.

8 · SUBMIT, THEN WIN OR LOSE

Set stage › Submitted, then Browse… to the PDF you sent. When it is decided: Won or Lost, one line on why, and for a loss who won.

Every week: Ian's to-do list

An email from Ian with a numbered list: who to call, what to send, by when. Reply with one line per number; a new date moves your next step, the same as Next step….
