## 7. The skeptic's section — risks, weaknesses, and what we could not verify
**Adverse findings on the record [V]:**
- **CIRB (two orders):** the Board unanimously found **LTS Solutions — Ledcor's telecom-services subsidiary, not the Building division KOR would contract with** — bargained in bad faith with IBEW Local 213 (finding dated **Nov 10, 2022**: "makes a mockery of the collective bargaining process"), then imposed a two-year first collective agreement by a **separate order, June 22, 2023** — the first successful Canada Labour Code first-agreement imposition since 1986. The dispute ran from the union's Aug 2017 certification. A formal federal adverse finding against a group company, disclosed here for completeness and scoped accurately.
- **DMS v. Ledcor Construction, 2025 ONSC 5100:** Ledcor's termination of a subcontractor was **upheld** (chronic understaffing, repeated warnings), but the court cut Ledcor's back-charges from $5.55M claimed to $2.45M awarded and rejected its delay claim entirely for lack of critical-path analysis. The judicial criticism ran in both directions — read it as a caution on documentation discipline, not a scandal. A companion ruling, **2026 ONSC 188**, later awarded Ledcor **$415K in costs** against DMS — on balance the litigation record favours Ledcor.
**Soft numbers [I]:** revenue/headcount/ranking figures are aggregator estimates — Ledcor publishes no audited financials. Nothing in this dossier hangs on them.
**Thin spots [V]:** healthcare-buildings record beyond the PHSA campus is thin (Section 4); no data-centre building work found despite marketing; the "Telus Living — no SE named" finding is absence-of-evidence across public coverage (the rezoning booklets themselves returned errors on direct fetch).
**Could not be checked by machine (open items):** WorkSafeBC and US OSHA penalty databases (require manual queries); BC Bid / Alberta Purchasing Connection postings (not indexable — a live pursuit could exist there without appearing in this document); confirmation of who leads Ledcor's Edmonton buildings office; the structural engineer of record on six of the seven US projects (city permit-portal lookups would resolve); the US portfolio's total value (the "8 projects / $2B+" figure dates from a 2020 release — treat as directional only).
