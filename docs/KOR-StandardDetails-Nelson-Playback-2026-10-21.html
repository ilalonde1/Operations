Why there's a CAD set and a Revit set

Turning Revit into CAD — export, linking it in, or a PDF — gets the line weights and colours wrong, and engineers change cover sheet notes on most jobs anyway. With both masters, a change is made twice and that's the end of it. Making one from the other would mean doing it again on every job. I'll build around the two sets, not against them — the thing worth fixing is never knowing which one is right, not having two.
