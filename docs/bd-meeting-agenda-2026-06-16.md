## 1 · The problem (4 min)
- Engineers lose billable hours researching and monitoring for work — bid sites, rumours, rebuilding the same contact lists.
- No shared memory between pursuits: intel leaves with people, and two of us can chase the same job.

## 2 · The BD Brain — what we built (10 min)
- Runs continuously: pulls live project + award feeds → de-duplicates → ties every project, owner, architect, GC and competitor back to KOR's Deltek history → verifies before anything surfaces.
- Produces intel on demand on **any company, project, competitor, or key person** — relationship depth, win/loss, the decision-maker, how to reach them.
- Built on KOR's own data (our 177-win / 79-loss record, our project history), not a generic market database.
- Every figure reconciles to a live query; built, locked, or out-of-lane projects can't reach a call list.

## 4 · How opportunities move (8 min)
**Surfaced → Triaged** (in-lane? early enough? warm path?) **→ Rejected, or Advanced to CRM → Tracked.**

- Triage runs on the funnel rules, in the open — every rejection carries a reason.
- The CRM is basic today and grows from use: one shared record — owner, stage, next action, won/lost.
- Result: no re-research, no collisions, no intel walking out the door.
