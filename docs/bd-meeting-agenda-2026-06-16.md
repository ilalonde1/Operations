## RUN SHEET (50 min)

### 0 · Open — why we're here (2 min)
- One sentence (above). Frame yourself as **support, not oversight**: you're removing a chore, not adding one.
- Set the frame: *"This is a working session, not a status update. We'll end with specific calls owned by name."*

### 1 · The problem we're solving (4 min)
- Engineers are KOR's most expensive, most billable resource — and too much of that time leaks into **researching and monitoring for work**: scanning BC Bid, chasing rumours, rebuilding the same contact lists.
- That work is real, but it's **not engineering**. It's also done blind — no memory between pursuits, no shared record of who-knows-whom.
- *"What if every engineer started from a verified call sheet instead of a blank page?"* — that's the pitch. Pause here. Let it land.

### 2 · What we built — the BD Brain (10 min)
- A single system that runs continuously in the background: **pulls live project + award feeds → de-duplicates → ties every project, owner, architect, GC and competitor back to KOR's own Deltek history → verifies before anything surfaces.**
- It can produce **intel on demand on any company, any project, any competitor, any key person** — relationship depth, win/loss history, who the decision-maker is, how to reach them. *This is the part to emphasize: it's not a list, it's a brain that answers questions.*
- **Why this beats anything off-the-shelf:** a bought database knows the market. **This one knows KOR** — our 177-win/79-loss record, our 78-project history with Wesgroup, which architects route work to us vs. to Fast+Epp. *No vendor can sell you that — it's our data.*
- The discipline that makes it trustworthy: **every number reconciles to a live query, and nothing built, locked, or out-of-lane can reach a call list.** (One line on the integrity gate — this is your credibility anchor when the numbers get questioned.)

### 3 · The proof — Lower Mainland, today (12 min)
Walk the Field Guide, fast, hitting only these:
- **The funnel, honestly:** 1,154 active projects → **not** 1,154 opportunities. Strip construction/built/on-hold and unknowns → **581 genuinely early** → **247 where KOR already has a warm path.** *The honesty is the point — we don't inflate.*
- **The number:** ~$340M of structural fee sits behind relationships **we already hold**; ~$183M is the realistic expected capture. *(Don't quote the $950M upper bound as if it's real — call it context.)*
- **The shortlist (this is what they came for):** the ~20 verified-open or warm plays — Surrey Memorial (pre-RFP, the one true open marquee seat), Wesgroup & Anthem (70–78 projects of trust, just cross-sell), the warmest Primes (Dikeakos, Arcadis IBI).
- **The "do NOT chase" list** — show it deliberately. *"The system is as valuable for what it tells us to skip as what it tells us to chase."* (Richmond/Burnaby hospitals, JCC — locked or cancelled.) This pre-empts the "is this just a wish list?" objection.
- **Who to call Monday:** every top seat now has a named, verified buyer + Prime + GC. Hold up the call sheet. *"This used to be a week of research. It's now a phone number."*

### 4 · How it works going forward — the opportunity lifecycle (8 min)
The process, drawn on the board in four boxes:

> **Surfaced** (the Brain finds it) → **Triaged** (in-lane? early enough? warm path?) → **Rejected** *or* **Advanced to CRM** → **Tracked & managed**

- The CRM is **basic today, and that's deliberate** — start simple, prove it, grow it. It gives us one shared, honest record: who's on it, what stage, next action, won/lost.
- **The win for engineers:** no more re-researching the same client, no more two people unknowingly chasing the same job, no more intel walking out the door when someone's on vacation.
- Triage is transparent — opportunities are accepted/rejected on the **funnel rules** (in-lane, early-stage, warm path), not on a hunch. Everyone sees why.

### 5 · What I need from you (5 min) — make this concrete, by name
- **Claim your warm accounts.** If you have the relationship, you take the call — the Brain hands you the opening, you do what you're already good at.
- **Feed it back.** Heard something at a site meeting? A competitor's move? A contact change? Tell the Brain — it gets smarter, and it remembers so you don't have to.
- **The ask is small:** ~20 warm calls this quarter, not 1,100 cold ones. *"I'm not asking you to become salespeople. I'm asking you to make the warm calls the system already teed up."*

- Coming: **one-page pursuit briefs** generated on demand, and deeper Deltek fusion so win/loss feeds back automatically.
