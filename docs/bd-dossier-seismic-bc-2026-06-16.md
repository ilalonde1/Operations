## 1. The thesis (why this is KOR's single best lane)
1. **It's KOR's specialization** — seismic is KOR's stated core competency.
2. **BC is Canada's seismic frontier** — the Cascadia subduction zone puts the Lower Mainland and Vancouver Island at the country's highest earthquake risk; seismic capacity is a hard code/insurance/operational requirement, not a preference.
3. **Seismic work is structural-LED** — on a seismic retrofit or replacement, the structural engineer is *the* lead consultant, not a sub behind an architect. **This is the one lane where KOR drives the team rather than waiting for an architect's invitation.**
4. **It's a funded, recurring government program with a huge open backlog** — predictable, multi-year, public pipeline.
5. **The seats are open.** Of **349 BC seismic projects (~$9.9B)** in KOR's graph, **346 (99%) have no structural engineer committed.**

The program has shifted over time from *retrofits* toward full **seismic replacements** (new schools), which are larger-value structural projects — favourable for an SE-led firm.
