## 38926 Glotman Simpson
The dominant BC high-rise concrete residential SE, ~90 staff with an LA office. Carrying THREE concurrent mega-programs (Senakw, Amazing Brentwood, City of Lougheed) plus a 10+ project California pipeline run from one LA office.

Where KOR contests:
- Institutional / K-12 / healthcare - confirmed GS gap; no schools or hospitals in their pipeline. BC SPIR school-seismic work sits in a lane they do not pursue.
- Seismic / heritage retrofit - GS portfolio is almost entirely new construction; KOR's heritage/seismic credentials are a genuine differentiator.
- Mid-market residential (6-20 storey, sub-$100M) - principals absorbed by mega-projects; mid-scale developers get junior attention or get declined. Target architects/developers who historically used GS.
- California - GS is the SoCal incumbent (LA office, US-licensed SEs); KOR is challenger via the Lindley/JWDA beachhead, winning on responsiveness where GS is over-committed.
- Watch (not contest): mass timber - GS is building a moat with Canada's Earth Tower.

## 69014 RJC Engineers
Canada's largest independent employee-owned buildings-SE firm, 18 offices, ~742 staff, employee-owned. Strong in K-12 seismic, civic/institutional, post-secondary, heritage. No California office and no visible CA-licensed SE.

Where KOR contests:
- BC K-12 seismic - RJC holds VSB relationships but the Vancouver structural bench is thin; KOR's principal-led key-person commitment competes on RFP scoring. Target mid-size districts (Burnaby, Richmond, Surrey, Abbotsford) where national overhead reduces agility.
- Heritage seismic and mass-timber mid-rise - RJC's lists are not saturated; KOR competes on delivered experience vs. a firm scaling capability via research.
- Developer-residential Metro Vancouver - under-served by RJC (skews civic/Toronto-tall); KOR's core lane.
- California - RJC has no CA presence (SD work is pure NHERI research); KOR's beachhead is a real entry RJC lacks.
- Avoid: large public civic (fire halls, courthouses) where RJC's QBS track record is insurmountable at KOR's size.

## 7345 Fast + Epp
The undisputed leader in large-scale civic mass timber, ~130 staff across 6+ offices in 4 countries. Just delivered Walmart HQ (largest NA mass timber campus) - senior bandwidth is releasing into the market now.

Where KOR contests:
- Developer residential wood-frame and 6-storey ICI - F+E does not compete here; KOR owns this lane uncontested.
- Seismic retrofit / heritage - no visible F+E work in this segment; direct positive differentiator for KOR.
- Mid-scale civic / cultural / Indigenous (sub-$20M) - F+E chases trophy aquatic/arena projects; smaller architect-referred work favors an accessible principal-led SE.
- Post-Walmart window (now through late 2026) - architects who relied on F+E availability are receptive to alternatives.
- California (with a CA stamp) - thin F+E footprint; contest timber-lite/concrete/woodframe in SD/Sacramento they won't prioritize.
- Avoid: trophy mass-timber civic head-to-head.

## 75868 Bush, Bohlman & Partners
Healthcare-and-K-12 structural boutique, ~32 staff, Vancouver-only. STRETCHED across three concurrent mega-hospitals (Royal Columbian opened May 2026; Cowichan 2027; Surrey 2030) plus a 12+ K-12 portfolio.

Where KOR contests:
- Mid-size healthcare additions/renovations (Delta/Langley scale, not mega) while partners are tied up on the big three.
- K-12 seismic upgrades - KOR's seismic credentialing competes directly with Tim White's seismic specialty.
- Developer residential - BB is nearly absent; KOR's bread-and-butter.
- California healthcare - BB has zero presence.
- Architect-relationship play - BB runs with Parkin/HH Angus/ZGF; target architects outside that consortium (HCMA, KPMB, mid-market). The Cowichan timber win shows owners are open to timber-fluent SEs - KOR's woodframe+seismic is credible adjacent territory.

## 38934 Stantec
Global 32,000+ multidiscipline A/E where structural is one internal service line. Absorbing 4,000+ acquired staff (Morrison Hershfield, Hydrock, Page) and tied to long BC infrastructure MSAs. Building CA presence in museums/water/transit.

Where KOR contests:
- Dedicated-SE positioning - structural competes internally for attention; KOR wins on dedicated senior attention, responsiveness, seismic depth.
- California gap - Stantec CA focus is infra/museums/water, NOT developer-residential or sub-SE on private buildings; KOR's SD beachhead sits in that gap.
- K-12 seismic retrofit - do NOT contest Stantec's architecture-led school work (11-yr #1); instead pursue seismic-upgrade work on existing schools where the SE relationship drives selection.
- Morrison Hershfield integration friction - a window to win mid-market clients experiencing transition service degradation.
- Mid-size healthcare additions where owners prefer a dedicated SE over a large-firm PM chain.

## 6154 DIALOG
Architect-led integrated firm (~1,200 staff) where structural is an in-house SUPPORT function, not the signature credential. Healthcare leadership is locked into UHNBC ($1.579B through 2031); lost Richmond Hospital Phase 2 to Graham/HDR.

Where KOR contests (partner + displacement):
- Mass timber / woodframe seismic - DIALOG used Fast+Epp externally on The Hive, so it does not always self-perform complex seismic timber; KOR can be the specialist SE partner on similar procurement.
- Healthcare RFP displacement - DIALOG lost Richmond Phase 2; the Graham/HDR winner needs a structural partner. Monitor HDR/Graham BC healthcare pursuits.
- Indigenous / affordable housing (Heather Lands, Squamish) - structural is subcontracted; KOR's project type.
- California transit/civic (BART/San Rafael) - requires a CA-licensed SE stamp; KOR's licensing gap blocks a direct bid but enables a partnering play.

## 75664 Entuitive
KOR's most direct structural-only rival in BC. ~290-355 firm-wide, but the Vancouver office runs on ~2 principals covering BOTH structural and building envelope, and is actively hiring (a thin-bench/surge signal). National mega-projects pull principal attention east to Calgary/Toronto.

Where KOR contests:
- Principal-led attention - with two principals covering two disciplines, neither is fully present on any single Vancouver file; KOR's named-engineer-in-the-room is a credible differentiator.
- Surrey affordable / social housing - Entuitive running Surrey Shelter + Baird Blackstone; BC Housing/Fraser Health relationships are contestable if KOR builds them now.
- Seismic / woodframe niche - Entuitive's seismic work is real but not its brand identity; KOR's seismic heritage + woodframe fluency differentiates on mid-rise.
- Restoration / heritage Vancouver - direct overlap; KOR's EGBC seismic narrative competes.
- California commercial (with a CA stamp) - thin, unverified SD presence is contestable.
- Avoid: P3 healthcare (Royal Inland), major infrastructure, tall-building optimization.

## 76560 Associated Engineering
A civil/environmental multidisciplinary firm (~1,099 staff) whose structural strength is transportation/bridges and emergency bridge response - building structural is a thin layer inside a civil firm. Stable and growing; not a stretch target. Canada-only.

Where KOR contests (indirect):
- AE will not be shortlisted for dedicated building SE (residential, institutional, seismic retrofit, woodframe). The angle is bundle disruption: when an owner/architect using AE for civil site work is tempted to bundle structural, KOR positions against diluted, non-principal-led, non-seismic-fluent in-house AE structural. Specifically relevant if AE bids bundled civil+structural on BC school seismic, healthcare, or woodframe residential.

## 70543 Kasian (architect, structural-adjacent)
An architecture/ID/planning firm with NO in-house SE - it outsources structural, so this is a partnering/seat-capture target rather than a head-to-head rival. Vancouver is stretched across Senakw + BC Hydro West End ($1B) + a new Defence stream, which means SE selection on new pursuits may be decided faster and on relationship.

SE partners are NOT locked in BC: WSP (mega-infra only), Glotman Simpson (civic/institutional, e.g. James Cowan Theatre - KOR's direct rival is the current incumbent). Alberta uses Entuitive + RJC. Victoria/Island and Defence are OPEN.

Where KOR contests for the SE seat:
- Defence - Kasian is building a Vancouver defence stream; defence SE is a thin market no firm dominates. Engage the team before Glotman locks it (the 5+ yr requirement implies an imminent award).
- Mass timber / woodframe residential - Kasian does timber resi (Basswood) but its BC partner is unconfirmed; KOR's woodframe/timber fluency is a direct entry.
- Senakw-adjacent - Capilano Reserve is next and the SE is not locked; be in the room.
- Victoria / Island - the office and Island Health work are growing with an unidentified SE partner; engage before RJC/Glotman occupy it.
- General pitch - KOR is principal-led, seismic-fluent, woodframe-experienced, and unconflicted by multidiscipline bundling: the relationship-forward alternative to Glotman on civic/institutional.

## Cross-cutting themes

1. California is open for almost everyone except Glotman Simpson. RJC, Bush Bohlman, Associated Engineering, and Kasian have NO CA presence; Fast+Epp and Entuitive are thin and unverified for CA licensure; Stantec is infra/museum-focused, not building-SE. KOR's binding constraint in CA is its own SE stamping/licensure gate, not competitor density - the Lindley/JWDA beachhead is genuinely contestable space.

2. Seismic retrofit and heritage is a consistent gap. Glotman, Fast+Epp, and RJC are new-construction-weighted; KOR's seismic/heritage identity is a differentiator against the whole field (Bush Bohlman's Tim White is the one strong direct counterpart).

3. Developer-residential and mid-market is under-served by the majors. Glotman (mega-only focus), RJC (civic/Toronto-tall), Fast+Epp (deliberately vacated), and Bush Bohlman (nearly absent) all leave the BC developer mid-rise lane open - KOR's core.

4. The architect-led firms (DIALOG, Kasian) are partner/seat-capture targets, not rivals. DIALOG outsources complex seismic timber; Kasian has no in-house SE and unlocked BC seats. The recurring incumbent to displace on both is Glotman Simpson.

5. Capacity is genuinely stretched at the most overlapping rivals. Glotman (3 mega-programs), Bush Bohlman (3 mega-hospitals on ~32 staff), and Entuitive (2-principal Vancouver office) are all over-committed right now - the best timing window for KOR to take mid-market and mid-size institutional work they cannot prioritize.
