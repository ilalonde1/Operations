## How to read this
"Open seat" means no SE firm is named in any public filing AND the project is early enough that the structural consultant has plausibly not yet been retained. Per saved KOR rule: a seat is genuinely open only if the project is EARLY-stage; design-dev/permitting/construction typically means the SE is already locked even when "unknown" in the data. Under-construction and completed projects are excluded from the rankings below.
