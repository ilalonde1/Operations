**Why pursue:** Project is actively in early construction (foundation/parkade complete, framing underway mid-2026). SE has not been publicly named; with framing beginning, a late-stage SE appointment window may still exist.

**Action:** Contact Zeidler Architects directly and ask if SE has been appointed for Fernie 692. Frame as Kootenays-capable SE with affordable housing experience. If open, submit qualifications immediately.

**Contact path:** Zeidler Architects (Vancouver/Calgary/Toronto offices); New Commons Development (dev mgr, non-profit); North Mountain Construction (GC, Nelson BC).

## Tier C: Low Probability / Small Scale

### 6-9. RHI Rd3 First Nations Projects (mpiIds 7416, 7415, 7417, 7420)

**Lantzville (Nanoose, 7416), North Saanich (Pauquachin, 7415), Port Renfrew (Pacheedaht, 7417), Alert Bay (Namgis, 7420)**

These four projects are listed in the MPI as Round 3 but no public procurement, announcement, or team has been found for any of them. The prior RHI projects for these same nations (all completed 2021-2023) were small (5-12 units, single-family or low-rise modular). New Round 3 projects are likely similar in scale.

**Key factors:**
- Small budgets (likely under $5M each) - below KOR's typical threshold for ROI on a pursuit
- Remote communities (Alert Bay, Port Renfrew are barge/ferry access)
- SE selection is owner-controlled; delivery often through modular suppliers who bring their own SE
- Alert Bay (Namgis) is the exception - $46M multi-year program is larger, but vote postponed

**Action for Alert Bay only:** Monitor Namgis First Nation announcements for membership vote outcome. If borrowing approved, a housing SE opportunity could emerge at meaningful scale. Contact Namgis housing department if vote passes.
