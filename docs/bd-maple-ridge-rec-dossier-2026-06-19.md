# Maple Ridge "Recreation Ready" — BD Dossier & Deep Analysis (2026-06-19)

**Trigger:** City of Maple Ridge LinkedIn "Recreation Ready" post. **Scope:** the City's 3-project rec-infrastructure program, led by the **$227M Hammond Aquatics & Recreation Centre**. **KOR fit:** a credible, well-timed SE pursuit in KOR's Lower Mainland backyard — *if* it positions through the architect (HCMA) and the Oct-2026 referendum before the post-referendum design-team selection.

## Bottom line
This is a **$393M, referendum-gated municipal program** — not a live RFP. HCMA is the prime architect, and their go-to aquatic-centre SE is **Fast + Epp** (KOR's main civic/timber rival), so the seat is contestable but defended. **KOR's hand is stronger than it looks**: Deltek shows a real rec/aquatic/arena resume (POCO Rec Centre, Parkinson Kelowna, Burnaby Lake Aquatic, Nelson Arena) — a genuine counter to Fast + Epp. The play is **relationship-led positioning over the next 12–18 months** — get on HCMA's team (we already hold 23 HCMA contacts incl. the Recreation director) and watch the **October 2026 referendum** that gates the whole thing. Detailed-design SE selection is ~2027; **there is time, and entry is the constraint, not capability.**

## 2. Hammond Aquatics — the target (deep detail)
- **122,000 sq ft over two storeys**; aquatic hall ~65,000 sq ft. Site: Larry Walker / Hammond Stadium ball fields at Hammond Community Park (after the golf-course→ballpark land swap).
- **Aquatics:** 37.5 m, 8-lane lap pool (movable bulkhead → 25 m / 12.5 m), climbing wall + diving platforms; leisure pool w/ lazy river, play features, waterslide; 2 hot pools, cold plunge, steam, sauna.
- **Community:** full gymnasium, fitness/cardio/weights, fitness studios, multipurpose + arts/culture rooms, children's play, café; **above- and below-ground parking** (structurally significant — below-grade + pool tanks + long-span aquatic roof).
- **Structural character:** long-span aquatic roof over a corrosive natatorium environment + below-grade parking + pool-tank structures. This is exactly the specialized aquatic-SE problem set — and where the incumbent's typology advantage lives.

## 3. Competitive reality — HCMA + Fast + Epp
- **Architect: HCMA Architecture + Design** (Vancouver) — Canada's premier aquatic/rec architect (Grandview Heights Surrey, təməsew̓txʷ New West, Rosemary Brown, Maple Ridge Leisure Centre expansion). They own this typology.
- **Incumbent SE: Fast + Epp.** HCMA's signature aquatic centres (təməsew̓txʷ, Rosemary Brown) are **Fast + Epp** structural, with **mass-timber/CLT hybrid roofs** (Kalesnikoff CLT, steel+CLT sawtooth). Per our competitor map, **Fast + Epp = the BC timber/civic+K12 dominator** — this is their home turf.
- **Implication:** Fast + Epp is the presumptive structural partner on HCMA's feasibility/schematic work now. Displacing them on their flagship typology is hard — but the *formal* detailed-design SE selection is post-referendum (~2027), and the City (not just HCMA) will have procurement say on a $227M public project.

## 5. Honest gaps & risks
- **No existing City of Maple Ridge or HCMA Deltek relationship** — KOR is cold to both (mitigated: 23 HCMA contacts already in our graph).
- **Fast + Epp's HCMA lock + timber-aquatic typology ownership** — the hardest barrier. KOR must either (a) match the mass-timber aquatic story or (b) win on availability/responsiveness + municipal-rec track record + cost.
- **Referendum risk (Oct 2026):** the entire $393M program is contingent on the public vote. Tax-increase optics ($90→$385/yr) are a real failure risk. Don't over-invest before the vote signals.
- **Long horizon:** Hammond construction 2029 → detailed-design SE selection ~2027. This is a *positioning* pursuit, not near-term revenue.
