ETABS column ANG is the long face's bearing - every oblong column was a quarter turn off since 8333a93e

ETABS turns a column's local axis 2 by ANG from global X, and a rectangle's D - our long face - lies along local 2, so
a column long north-south is ANG 90. 8333a93e (2026-08-06) wrote AxisAngle - 90 on the belief that local 2 lies along
global Y. Found by the Virtual Drafter session (operations-ee) drawing Halifax from our e2k; the engineer's 31170-01
carries 234 of its 274 12x30s, drawn long north-south, at ANG 90.

Measured against the engineer's models, oblong columns (long/short >= 1.3) at the same place and storey (model-yardstick
registration, within 100 mm), long-face bearings compared:
- before: 1,506 of 1,506 pairs 90 degrees off (31065 380, 31138 196, 31130 270, 31202 660);
- after: 0 off by 90. 39 sit on a skewed grid the reader takes square to the page (31065 34 at 8 degrees, 31130 5 at
  17 degrees) - a separate reading limit, not this fault.

Differential, same code with and without this line, the six banked sets rebuilt both ways: the only lines that changed
are column LINEASSIGN ANG values, each by exactly +90 (6,668 lines; 0 other changes). The six baselines are re-banked
with exactly that change and nothing else: byte for byte the same files once ANG values are blanked, CRLF kept. They
were already behind the current reader for other reasons (six-set column losses, reported 10-23); those are not banked here.

Every place that writes or reads a column angle (grep AxisAngleDegrees and ANG over Core, TakeoffCli and the App):
E2kGeometryComposer writes it (fixed); ColumnFootprint documents AxisAngleDegrees as the long face's bearing from
global X (unchanged, now agreed with); PlanGeometryTransform rotates it with its frame (unchanged); the App's
EtabsE2kExporter writes beams only, ANG 0; nothing in Core, the CLI or the App reads ANG back from an e2k - model-diff
and model-yardstick compare positions and sizes, not angles.

New test AColumnsAngIsTheBearingOfItsLongFace: 12x30s long north-south, east-west and at 30 degrees give ANG 90, 0, 30
(8333a93e gives 0, 270, 300 and fails it). Core fast 1,651 of 1,651. Previously delivered models: not listed, not
touched (Ian).
