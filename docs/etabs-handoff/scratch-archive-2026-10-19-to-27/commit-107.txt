db 107: dxf.nested-ring-tile-share (0.25) - a ring that tiles a hole already cut is part of it (intake step 177)

One KorStandards rule for the Operations reader's step 177: a ring drawn inside an opening already cut, running along
its edge (dxf.ring-on-plate-edge-fraction, 054) and at least this share of its area, is part of that hole - a cab in
its shaft - and is not cut again. Measured on the six banked sets: cabs are 29-100% of their shafts; real holes drawn
along a larger ring's edge 4-6%. Without it 31138's shaft was cut three times on every storey.
