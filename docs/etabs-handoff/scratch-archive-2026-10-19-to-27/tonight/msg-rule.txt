A ruling written for people (units 'rule') is not a setting: rules-required builds no longer stop on the engineer's prose rulings

KOR.Drafter migrations 131, 132 and 134 (and one row of 119) put the engineer's and the drafter's rulings into analysis.Ruling with
units 'rule' and prose values ("X = opening", "top-to-bottom; columns right-to-left"). vw_RuleSetting serves every row, and
RuleSettings.TryLoad refused each as an unreadable number, so LoadRequired threw "KorStandards rule settings could not be read"
and EVERY rules-required DXF-to-ETABS build stopped - production runs included, keys the build never reads included. Found by the
full suite at d92e50b1 (started 12:40, after 131 at 12:50): ShippedModelsAgreeWithEachOther, StackMergeChangesNothingButLabels
x2 and AnAnsweredQuestionChangesTheNextModel failed in under a second each with that message. Operations-ee (Ian's proxy):
the rows stay as they are ('rule' is a ruling for people and the drafter; relabelling 20 rows would hide the class) - fix the
loader; and every ruling migration now gets a read-back counting the rows this loader refuses, expecting 0.

- RuleSettings.KindOf(value, units): the loader's per-row decision, now one testable function - a 'rule' row is ForPeople (left
  out and counted), a list is a setting (an empty one stops the run), anything else must parse or it stops the run, as before.
- LoadRequired(conn, keys, out rulingsForPeopleLeftOut); DxfToEtabsService's report says how many were left out.
- RulingCoverageTests: 134's 8 rulings (pit edge, red outline, X = opening, columns centred, core door holes, header depth,
  levels from the section, wall on the marked face) declared NotYetObeyed - they govern the markup reader (the drafter's lane);
  measured today on Halifax L1/P1, each fault Jim found was already in the DXF this converter was given.

and the LIVE store: LoadRequired over the real view succeeds and leaves out exactly the 'rule' rows it serves. The 4 tests that
