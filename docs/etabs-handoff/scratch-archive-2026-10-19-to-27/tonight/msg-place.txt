sheets.csv records where each view was put in the model - the placement the build computed and threw away, proved on 3 sets

The build places each sheet's view by a frame (named-axis fit, the set alignment, or none) and the composer adds an offset;
neither was kept, so a member could be traced from the model back to its sheet only by voting - and the votes failed twice:
10-26's untraced WRONG TWIN columns (31065 L2, 31170-arch L7), 10-27's outline-column measure (both registration designs).

SheetOutcome.Placement = "rot=R;dx=X;dy=Y;scale=S;ox=OX;oy=OY" (invariant culture): a DXF point is scaled by S, turned R deg,
shifted (X, Y) - the view's frame - then (OX, OY) added - the composer's offset. Taken from the frame the geometry is actually
transformed by (frameOfSheet, set where PlanGeometryTransform.Apply is called) and the offset the composer is given. Carried into
sheets.csv as a new last column "placement", one per DXF view joined by " | " as dxf_files is; a ledger written before it (19
fields) reads back with a null placement.

Proved: tools/CorpusGateBisect/placement_proof.py carries every placed view's own KOR_V_COL columns through its recorded
placement onto the written model - corpus-gate --jobs 31130-01,31183-01,31048-01 (31183's sheets set by fit, 31048's quadrants by
members): 382 of 382 (100%), 343 of 355 (97%), 695 of 775 (90%) land within 50 mm of a model column; no view with 5+ columns under
half, exit 0. Matched on ANY storey, measured: on the view's own storeys it reads 60-64%, because a member carries the storey at
its top (a foundation plan's columns are written on the storey above) - the header says so.
