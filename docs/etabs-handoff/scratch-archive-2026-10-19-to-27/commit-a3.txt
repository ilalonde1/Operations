One markup DXF from the app and the CLI: markup labels read in Core, layer-by-colour a choice in both, any named markup page exported

Briefs CODEX-ENGINEERING-FIX-A3, -A3B and -A3C, by Codex; verified on the dev box 2026-10-19.

Found on a real request (143 E 3rd, sheet A105, a KOR Bluebeam markup):
- The app's DXF carried the 18 markup labels; the CLI's carried 0. The text reader lived only in the app.
- The by-colour layering, which says what the engineer drew rather than guessing slab/beam/column, was hardcoded
  off in the app and absent from the CLI.

Now:
- MarkupText (Core) reads markup labels for both. DrawingIntake adds them only in markup mode.
- `takeoff pdf-takeoff --layer-by-colour` (refused together with --kor-layers).
- An app checkbox, "Layers by markup colour", saved in FirmDefaults.
- In markup mode the CLI writes the page the user names whatever its sheet type (Ian's decision). The stick-file
  route keeps the plan-sheet rule.

Measured on this build:
- Core fast suite: 1,626 of 1,626.
- App PdfToSafe tests: 430 of 430, including TheMarkupDxfIsTheSameFromTheAppAndTheCliTests, which compares shapes on
  Regent and shapes and labels on a synthetic labelled page, in both layer modes.
- Six-set gate (on A3): 2 of 2, so all six banked models are byte-identical and the stick-file route is untouched.

Known and NOT caused here: the App test host does not exit after its tests headless. The blame timeout ends it at
120 s and dotnet test reports exit 1 with every test passed. The unfiltered run hung the same way this morning,
before A3.

Not done: the app's scale reader still misses a title block that stacks its SCALE value under the label (fix A2).
