# The Workload Meeting screen - a one-page guide

**For Jim DesRoches. KOR Operations app, PM Tools. 27 November 2026.**

## In short

The Workload Meeting screen now runs the meeting the way you asked on 9 November: **what is due in the next few weeks
comes first, in date order**, PMs are ordered by the work they have actually prioritised, the drafters get their own
pass, and at the end **one button emails everyone what was agreed**. Everything reads live from Deltek. Nothing needs
re-typing.

## Running a meeting - five steps

1. **Open it.** KOR Operations > PM Tools > **Workload Meeting**.
2. **Start the meeting.** Press **+ New Meeting**. It carries forward last meeting's priorities and notes.
3. **Look ahead.** The top list shows the **prioritised jobs due in the next 2 weeks** (change it to 3, 4 or 6). Walk it
   top to bottom.
4. **Go PM by PM, then drafters.** Below, each PM's jobs. Set a priority (P1-P5) and type a note on any job. Then switch
   **Group: Drafters** (or Drafting Mgrs) to give the drafters their turn.
5. **Close it out.** Press **Email the report**. Outlook opens a finished email to every PM and drafting manager with a prioritised job. Read it,
   press Send.

## What each part shows

| Part | What you see | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **Due in the next N weeks** | Prioritised jobs in date order: due date, priority, job, PM, the deliverable (e.g. "BP - BP review") and notes | The job's next deadline in Deltek (Projects > Deadlines) |
| **No deadline in Deltek** | Prioritised jobs with no future date entered - listed in amber so the gap shows | Same; the PM enters the date in Deltek to move the job up |
| **PM lists** | One list per PM, highest **prioritised unbilled fee** first. Under each name: "Prioritised: N - Fee - Unbilled" | Prioritised jobs only, so long unprioritised lists do not distort the order |
| **Each job** | Phase, **CAD or Revit**, % billed, **drafting manager**, engineering and drafting % used, delivery risk | Deltek project fields and timesheets |
| **Group switch** | **PMs** / **Drafting Mgrs** / **Drafters** - the same jobs regrouped | Drafters = whoever booked drafting hours to the job in the last 4 weeks, so it works even where Deltek has no drafting manager |
| **Underwater** | A quiet amber "under" beside a job whose cost has passed what has been billed | Hours at salary rate vs. fee billed; a job not yet invoiced is flagged only once cost passes 10% of fee |
| **Show $ Detail** | Adds Fee, **Original fee**, **Extra fee**, **% of original billed**, **Earned** (hours x charge-out), **Cost** (hours x salary), **Billed/Earned**, **Billed/Cost** | Extras = Deltek's "X - Extra" phases. Billed/Earned at 1.00 or more: making money. Billed/Cost under 1.00: working for free |
| **Email the report** | One Outlook draft: due list, no-deadline list, every prioritised job by PM with drafting lead, next due date and notes, plus meeting notes | Addressed from the staff directory; nothing is sent until you press Send |
| **Export to Excel** | The meeting or the PM lists as a spreadsheet | Same data as the screen |
| **Demos** (PM Tools home) | Opens the shared folder of recorded demos, one folder per topic | `\\KOR-FS01\Library\11 IT\KOR Operations Demos` |

Hover any column heading for its definition.

## Three things that make it work

- **PMs keep Deltek current.** Today 48 of 63 prioritised jobs have no future deadline and 21 hot-list jobs have no
  drafting manager. The screen is only as good as those two fields.
- **Set priorities before the meeting.** Only P1-P5 jobs feed the top list and the totals.
- **Press + New Meeting each time.** The last meeting saved in the app is 19 October.
