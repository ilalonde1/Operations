## 1. Market & policy drivers
- **BC Housing Rapid Response to Homelessness (RRH):** ~$291M capital + $170M services → **2,000+ modular supportive units** in 22 communities (2017→2021). 3-storey wood-frame, ~250 sq.ft. studios, relocatable. ([BC Housing RRH](https://www.bchousing.org/projects-partners/Building-BC/RRH))
- **CMHC Rapid Housing Initiative (RHI):** Rounds 1+2 ($2.5B national); BC modular: Aster Place/Harbour House (Richmond), The Juniper (Victoria), North Saanich. ([MBI](https://www.modular.org/2022/09/26/the-rapid-rise-of-affordable-modular-housing-in-canada/))
- **BC Builds (Feb 2024):** low-cost construction loans for middle-income rental; modular explicitly encouraged; ~4,000 units broken ground. e.g. **Estrella, Prince Rupert** (40 units, 5 storeys). ([BC Builds](https://www.bchousing.org/projects-partners/funding-programs/bc-builds))
- **DASH — Digitally Accelerated Standardized Housing:** BC Housing/BC Builds open-source tool for 3–6 storey prefab multifamily; structural collaborator **Timber Engineering Inc.**; manufacturer collaborators ROC Modular, Winton, Paradigm, TAG. 31 firms committed. ([DASH](https://www.acceleratedhousing.ca/))
- **BC Modular Schools Program:** **$475M+ for 37 prefab school additions, ~7,900 seats across 17 districts** (since fall 2023). NRB/ATCO historically the classroom provider. ([gov.bc.ca Feb 2025](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025INF0010-000083)) — *this is the program behind KOR's 16 tracked projects.*
- **Mayors' Task Force on Standardized Modular Homes (Nov 2024):** chaired by Burnaby Mayor Hurley w/ Modular BC; 8 standardized templates debuted May 2025. (Notable: Prince George Mayor Simon Yu is a structural engineer.)
- **Build Canada Homes (federal, Sept 2025):** $25B debt financing aimed at prefab builders (BC sites not yet named).

## 4. The structural-engineering angle (the KOR opening)
**Two SE roles in modular:** (1) **Specialty/Factory Engineer (SSE)** retained by the manufacturer, designs modules to CSA A277; (2) **SE of Record (SEOR)** retained by owner/architect, designs **foundation, podium, lateral system, site assembly** + BCBC compliance. Low-rise wood-frame = compact foundation/podium scope; high-rise steel volumetric (King Edward) = complex seismic/connection engineering.

**Who currently does it in BC:**
- **Design Works Engineering** (Kelowna) — primary SE for the NRB / S2 Architecture / BC Housing cluster (Grand Forks, Mission). A Vancouver firm (KOR) is better placed for Lower Mainland/Island site work.
- **WSP** — UBC high-rise steel-modular seismic research; likely King Edward SEOR.
- **Timber Engineering Inc.** — embedded in the DASH platform.
- **Aspect** [LIKELY] — modular mass-timber via Perkins+Will system.
- **RJC** — modular SE seen in AB, not yet BC.
