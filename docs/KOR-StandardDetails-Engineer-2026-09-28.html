Standard Details — Engineer

Everything you can do with the standard details, in the order you'd do it. Five jobs, start to finish.

For engineers

1Look at a detail

  1. Open KOR Operations → Standard Details.
  2. Type a number or a few words into Search detail # or title…
  3. Click the detail in the list. Its drawing appears on the right.
  4. Click Open PDF to open that one in Bluebeam.

2Review a whole set

  1. Click a discipline chip — Concrete, Steel, Wood Frame, General.
  2. Click Open list as PDF.
  3. You get one PDF containing every detail in that set.
  4. Mark it up in Bluebeam exactly as you'd redline a drawing.
  5. Email it to the gatekeeper.

You never edit a detail yourself — you say what's wrong and the gatekeeper fixes it once, for everyone.

3Say a detail is good or bad

  1. Click the detail.
  2. ✓ Approve — it becomes placeable and goes into the master at the next publish.
  3. ✕ Reject — it drops out of the master and stays out until it is fixed.

Nothing is ever deleted. A rejected detail keeps its number and its history.

4Get a brand-new detail in

  1. Draw it, or mark up what you want changed.
  2. Send it to the gatekeeper and say what it's for.
  3. They draw it in the standards model and add it.
  4. It gets a permanent KOR-D-##### that never changes.
  5. Once they publish, every drafter has it.

There is no “submit a detail” button, on purpose — one person decides what becomes a standard.

5Build your own sheet

  1. Click Tools ▾ → Compose Sheet…
  2. Double-click details in the left list to add them.
  3. Give the sheet a name, then create the PDF.

It is stamped UNCONTROLLED WORKING COPY, so it can never be mistaken for the standard.

What the words mean

KOR-D-##### a detail’s permanent numberPlaceable approved, so drafters can use itThe master the Revit template every job starts fromGatekeeper the one person who changes the master

The one habit

If a detail is wrong, say so with its number. It gets fixed once, in the master — never patched inside a job.
