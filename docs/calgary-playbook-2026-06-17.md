## 1. STRATEGY — win a first reference through a warm architect, then build
Calgary is greenfield (no KOR track record) and a stated growth market. **Don't chase owners or bid cold.** The fastest realistic win is the SE sub-seat on a project led by an architect KOR *already* knows:
- **GGA Architecture — Airdrie SW Rec Centre ($90M).** SE sub selecting now (Council reviews schematic July 7). This is the single highest-probability Calgary-area win — a warm architect + an open seat + a public-sector reference. **Move on it immediately.**
- **GEC Architecture — NE Athletic Complex ($162M).** Also KOR-tied; design underway, so the SE may already be moving — a fast call to confirm status is worth it.
Land either and KOR has its first Calgary reference, which unlocks the institutional pipeline.
