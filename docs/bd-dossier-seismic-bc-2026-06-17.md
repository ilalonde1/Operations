## 1. The thesis (why this is KOR's best structural-led lane)
1. **It's KOR's specialization** — seismic is the stated core competency.
2. **BC is Canada's seismic frontier** — the Cascadia subduction zone puts the Lower Mainland and Vancouver Island at the country's highest earthquake risk; seismic capacity is a hard code/insurance requirement, not a preference.
3. **Seismic work is structural-LED** — on a retrofit or replacement the structural engineer is *the* lead consultant, not a sub behind an architect. **This is the one lane where KOR drives the team rather than waiting for an architect's invitation.**
4. **It's a funded, recurring government program with a large open backlog** — predictable, multi-year, public.
