The Workload Meeting screen - a one-page guide

For Jim DesRoches. KOR Operations app, PM Tools. 27 November 2026.

In short

The Workload Meeting screen now runs the meeting the way you asked on 9 November: what is due in the next few weeks comes first, in date order, PMs are ordered by the work they have actually prioritised, the drafters get their own pass, and at the end one button emails everyone what was agreed. Everything reads live from Deltek. Nothing needs re-typing.

Running a meeting - five steps

  1. Open it. KOR Operations > PM Tools > Workload Meeting.
  2. Start the meeting. Press + New Meeting. It carries forward last meeting's priorities and notes.
  3. Look ahead. The top list shows the prioritised jobs due in the next 2 weeks (change it to 3, 4 or 6). Walk it top to bottom.
  4. Go PM by PM, then drafters. Below, each PM's jobs. Set a priority (P1-P5) and type a note on any job. Then switch Group: Drafters (or Drafting Mgrs) to give the drafters their turn.
  5. Close it out. Press Email the report. Outlook opens a finished email to every PM and drafting manager with a prioritised job. Read it, press Send.

Three things that make it work

  • PMs keep Deltek current. Today 48 of 63 prioritised jobs have no future deadline and 21 hot-list jobs have no drafting manager. The screen is only as good as those two fields.
  • Set priorities before the meeting. Only P1-P5 jobs feed the top list and the totals.
  • Press + New Meeting each time. The last meeting saved in the app is 19 October.
