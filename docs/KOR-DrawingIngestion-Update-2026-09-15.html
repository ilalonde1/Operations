What it does, in plain terms

Drop in a structural drawing set — the PDF we already send the client — and out comes an ETABS model with the building typed in: every storey at its elevation, the grid, the columns sized and turned as drawn, the walls at their true thickness, the floor plates, the openings. No Revit, no re-drawing, no typing. The engineer opens it and starts engineering — the part only an engineer can do. When Andrea first saw it: "if I have that, it's going to take me like no time to rebuild it."

BREADTH

253 of our 296 jobs

Every current structural set in our archive goes through the tool after every change — 8,650 pages in under an hour. 253 build a model from the PDF alone; the 43 that don't say exactly why (17 are just copies of another job's file).

ACCURACY

Where the engineer put it

Checked against the engineers' own ETABS models: on jobs with a current model, about two of every three columns land within 100 mm of where the engineer put theirs — and often it is their model that has moved on. Storeys, grids and walls read straight off the sheet.

TRUST

It says what it assumed

Every reading rule is code with a test, or a row in our standards database (86 of them). Anything the tool had to assume is listed in the report beside the model. An independent AI reviewer has audited the logic four times; the last audit's findings were fixed the same day.
