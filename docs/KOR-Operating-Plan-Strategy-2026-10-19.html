Recommendation · you decide

Strategy

Business development

Win more of the right work, earlier

Where to play

  • Grow the developers who already choose KOR. Westland, Northland, Cressey, Domus, Kerkhoff and Polygon: 40 won, 1 lost.Each gets a named partner and a standing check-in, not a proposal-by-proposal relationship.
  • Metro Vancouver and South of Fraser are the base. 220 of 281 wins since 2020; South of Fraser converts 81% of decided proposals.
  • The US is the growth market. 22 wins at 65% decided, and 17 of 110 live pursuits, all Jim's.
  • Qualify hard before writing a fee for the clients who rarely convert. NSDA, Wesgroup, Strand, Starlight and Porte: 12 won, 15 lost, 35 never decided.A go/no-go before the proposal, not after.

How KOR wins

  • Get in as the architect's engineer before the RFP. The structural engineer is usually chosen at the start of schematic design.
  • Mid-size fees convert best. $25–100k wins 80% of decided; $250k and up wins 58%. Big bids are deliberate bets.
  • Close every proposal. Follow up at 14 days, chase the decision at 45, and record who won and why. Glotman Simpson is the competitor named most often (6 of the stated losses).
  • Spread the selling. One principal leads 345 of 701 pursuits (49%). Six owners now hold the 110 live pursuits.

Goals to 31 March 2027 · numbers yours to set

  • Every live pursuit has a fee estimate and an owner's next step (today 0 of 110 have a fee).
  • No proposal sits "submitted" past 60 days without an outcome.
  • Submitted-to-won from 43% toward 50%.
  • Invoicing back above $400k a month.

Operations

Turn work into cash, and stop giving hours away

Where it hurts

  • Cash. $2.26M owed, $1.41M of it over 90 days, and $805k on projects no longer on the active list.
  • Overruns. 272 of 814 active projects are over their hours budget; 72 are critical; 94 are burning hours faster than they bill.
  • Capacity. Utilization is 70.8%.

Priorities

  • Collect. A partner owns every invoice over 90 days and reports at the quickie. Decide the $805k on inactive projects: collect, settle or write off.
  • Charge for changes before the hours are spent. Extra-service letters on the worst ten over-budget projects first.
  • Bill on time. Close the gap on the 94 projects where billing lags the hours.
  • Fix the process before January. The rush should land on a working cadence, not a backlog.

Goals to 31 March 2027 · from the scoreboard

  • DSO from 174 to 120 days: about $760k of cash without billing a dollar more.
  • Owed over 90 days under $700k.
  • The $805k inactive AR decided, with the reason recorded.
  • Over-budget projects down from 272, the worst ten with change orders.
