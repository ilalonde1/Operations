One chain, two halves

Signal to cash

1 Signal→2 Pursuit→3 Proposal→4 Decision→5 Won or lost→6 Delivery→7 Invoice→8 Cash

Orange is business development, slate is Operations. The BD half decides how much work comes in; the Operations half decides how much of it becomes cash. Part 1's goals sit on this chain: invoicing back above $400k a month needs the left half, $760k of cash from faster collection needs the right.

Where it breaks today

Pursuit → ProposalNone of the 110 live pursuits carries a fee estimate, and 61 have not been touched in 14 days or more, so nobody can say what the pipeline is worth.
Proposal → Decision266 proposals since 2020 were sent and never closed. Win rate is 72% when a client decides, 43% of everything sent.
Invoice → Cash174 days to collect. $1.41M is over 90 days, $805k of it on projects no longer active.

Owner · measure · what the system does

The eight stages

1Signal

What
Permits, rezonings, tenders and news about the people KOR works with.
Owner
The radar finds; the regional lead picks.
Today
1,731 new in 30 days, from 128 of 140 sources. Only you see them so far.
System
Nightly ingestion, the relevance gate, client dossiers in the Director.
Next step
Release the app to the leads (the pilot for Omar, Islam and Jim, promised on 9 October), so each lead sees the signals in their region and makes the good ones pursuits.

2Pursuit

What
A named project or client with an owner and a next step.
Owner
Regional lead: Omar Vancouver, Islam Alberta, Conor Okanagan, Rory Island, Jim US; JM.
Today
110 live; 0 carry a fee; 61 untouched for 14 days or more.
System
The CRM, and a weekly roll-up per owner: first touch by day 7, keep warm every 21 days, next step when due.
Weak point
Calls and meetings are rarely logged, so most items read "first touch".

3Proposal

What
Go or no-go, then the fee proposal (filed in Project Opportunities\Pending).
Owner
The lead, with the engineer who will run the job.
Today
None of the 110 live pursuits is a tender, so no go/no-go is on record.
System
For tenders: go/no-go 2 days after it opens, draft review a week before the deadline, submission check 2 days before.
Weak point
Questions deadlines and addenda are not captured, so the system cannot warn on them.

4Decision

What
Chasing the client until they decide.
Owner
The lead.
Today
43% of sent proposals won; 266 never closed. Leduc was closed as a no-bid on 16 October because its submission was never recorded.
System
Follow-up at 14 days, chase the decision at 45, both in the owner's roll-up.
Weak point
The win/loss review found no standing close-out step before this month.

5Won or lost

What
Won: hand the job to delivery. Lost: who won, and why.
Owner
The lead, handing to the project manager.
Today
Of 291 lost or unanswered proposals with a comment, fewer than 30 say why.
System
Hand-off and loss-reason asks within 30 days, in the roll-up.
Weak point
Loss reasons are what Part 1's "how KOR wins" should learn from, and they are mostly missing.

6Delivery

What
Designing the job inside its fee.
Owner
The project manager (12 PMs, 814 active projects); JM and Jim in the COO seat.
Today
272 over their hours budget; 72 critical; utilization 70.8%.
System
The Financials engine, the COO monthly, the COO moves in your morning hour.
Weak point
Proposed: an extra-service letter before a project passes its hours budget, tracked as a step, not found afterwards.

7Invoice

What
Billing what has been earned, on time.
Owner
The project manager.
Today
$367k a month, April to September; 94 projects bill slower than they burn hours.
System
The billing-lag list from the Financials engine, in the morning brief.
Weak point
Proposed: the billing-lag list is reviewed at each PM's quickie, not only read by you.

8Cash

What
Collecting what has been billed.
Owner
Proposed: a named partner for every invoice over 90 days.
Today
174 days to collect; $2.26M owed; $1.41M over 90 days.
System
The scoreboard records it every morning; the COO monthly pre-read lists the largest.
Weak point
The collections module is barely built, so chasing is by hand.
