## 2. Business & Footprint

Pinnacle International is a family-controlled, owner-operated developer focused exclusively on luxury high-rise condominium residences, hotels, and master-planned mixed-use communities. Michael De Cotiis has run the company since founding. They do not flip or sell assets — they develop, manage, and sell units directly under the Pinnacle brand.

**Product type:** Cast-in-place concrete high-rises, 30–90 storeys. Hotels integrated into residential towers (Le Méridien San Diego is their first US hotel flag; Vancouver Marriott Pinnacle is their Vancouver hotel). Master-planned multi-tower campuses (Pinnacle Centre Toronto = 4 towers; Pinnacle Lougheed Burnaby = 5 towers planned).

**Geographic presence:**
- **Greater Vancouver / BC:** 25+ completed projects — Yaletown (939 Homer "The Pinnacle," 1997), Coal Harbour (Venus, Classico, Ritz), Burnaby (Carrigan Court, Lougheed supertalls)
- **Greater Toronto Area:** Bellagio on Bloor, Pinnacle Centre (4-tower, 2,000 units waterfront community), Pinnacle on Adelaide
- **San Diego:** Four completed high-rises since 2005; two more in active pipeline

**Company character:** Tight-knit team (~100 people), high repeat with same consultants, long-term relationships are the norm. Pascal Yammine (VP Construction) is the day-to-day decision-maker for structural. Michael De Cotiis stays involved on major decisions.

**KOR's displacement angle:** Glotman Simpson is a larger generalist firm. KOR can offer the same Vancouver-to-SD continuity with deeper personal principal involvement (Jim himself on the file) and a higher-touch relationship. On a repeat developer with tightly managed projects, that matters.

## 6. KOR Angle & How to Win

### Why KOR

- **Dual-market presence, proven.** KOR has 10 delivered projects in San Diego (800 Broadway, 6th & Olive, Simone at 1401 Union, Bayside, The Grande, and others) and 70+ in Greater Vancouver. Pinnacle is the only major developer operating simultaneously in both cities who does not already have KOR. That asymmetry is the pitch.
- **No listed SE on the live SD towers.** Pacific Heights is in preconstruction with no structural engineer on record. This is not a "we hope to displace someone" situation — it is an open seat.
- **High-rise concrete is KOR's core.** Pinnacle builds concrete supertalls. KOR built One Burrard Place, The Mondrian, The Smithe, International Hotel & Tower in Vancouver. The product type is an exact match.
- **Same continuity story Glotman Simpson has sold, but KOR can offer it at principal level.** Jim DesRoches can be the named structural principal on the Pacific Heights file and on Lougheed. That is a credible differentiator over a 90-person firm.

### Concrete Asks for Jim (San Diego Trip)

1. **Request a 20-minute intro with Pascal Yammine** while you are in San Diego. Reason: you are completing work nearby and want to introduce KOR. Don't cold-call as a pitch — frame it as a peer introduction between professionals working in the same two cities. Reference your Vancouver practice and your SD projects. Ask about their SD pipeline timeline for Pacific Heights specifically.

2. **Bring one SD project reference sheet** (single page): pick the biggest/tallest SD project in KOR's completed portfolio. A rendered photo, brief stats (storeys, units, concrete system type, completion year), and a client quote. Leave it with Pascal or email it following the meeting.

3. **If you get in front of Michael De Cotiis:** lead with Lougheed. He is personally invested in those supertalls — it will be the most significant structural engineering scope in Western Canada in a decade. Offering BC code expertise + SD continuity on one relationship is the reason to move from Glotman Simpson to KOR. Don't undersell the supertall opportunity.

4. **Follow-up move after the trip:** Have someone in KOR's Vancouver office search the Burnaby/Vancouver permit systems for the Lougheed structural permit application (it should file within 12–18 months of the September 2024 council approval). When it files and names the SE of record, you know whether there is still a window on Lougheed or whether to focus exclusively on Pacific Heights and the 601 Beach rezoning.
