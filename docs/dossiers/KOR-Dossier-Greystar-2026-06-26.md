## 2. Business & Footprint

**Who they are.** Bob Faith founded Greystar in 1993 in Charleston, SC. Forty years later it is the largest apartment operator in the world — managing roughly 1.1 million units and beds across 17 countries — and a top-tier institutional real estate investment manager. Greystar's $79B total AUM spans Core, Core-Plus, Value-Add, and Opportunistic fund strategies.

**What makes Greystar structurally different from every other developer KOR pursues.** Greystar is fully vertically integrated: they are simultaneously the investment manager (capital), the developer (land + entitlements), the general contractor (via Greystar Construction), and the property manager. There is no third-party GC selection event. Structural engineer selection is made by Greystar's internal development team during preconstruction — typically 12–18 months before groundbreaking. KOR earns work here by having relationships with Greystar's development directors, not by chasing RFPs.

**Product focus.** Class A luxury concrete high-rise multifamily (15–25+ storeys), purpose-built student housing, and mixed-use residential. Large-scale concrete is their flagship product — identical to KOR's sweet spot. They do also build lower-density wood-frame suburban and some senior living, but the prestige pipeline in KOR's markets is all concrete.

**Geographic expansion.** Greystar entered Canada in 2022, starting in Victoria BC (University Heights). Their stated Canadian focus is BC, Alberta, and Ontario. Their two active Vancouver rezoning sites (both concrete, both DIALOG) represent the leading edge of a multi-decade Canadian concrete high-rise pipeline. Per John Wilbeck's public statements, Canada is a strategic expansion priority at the highest level of the organization.

## 5. Competitive Reality

### San Diego — KOR is the incumbent; protect and deepen

KOR has now performed structural engineering on two consecutive Greystar luxury concrete high-rises in San Diego's Bankers Hill:
- 525 Olive (2022) — won "Best Multifamily" from SDBJ
- 6th & Palm (2025–2028) — currently under construction, highest-profile SD rental tower

The gateway is the JWDA relationship. JWDA has been Greystar's architect of record for both projects. As long as KOR and JWDA's relationship is intact, KOR is effectively the go-to SE for Greystar SD concrete high-rise. **There is no confirmed incumbent SE for any future SD sites beyond 6th & Palm — the next opportunity is open.**

For student housing and lower-rise: VCA Structural is the confirmed SE on Accolade Campanile (6-story SDSU housing, also JWDA-designed). KOR is not being displaced from high-rise; VCA is being used for a different product type.

### Pacific Northwest — CT Engineers

On Birchway Angle Lake (SeaTac), Greystar used CT Engineers (Seattle-based) as structural. This is outside KOR's markets and consistent with using local SE firms per region. Not a displacement threat in SD or Vancouver.

### Vancouver — DIALOG structural is the primary barrier

DIALOG is an integrated firm. If they perform structural in-house on 1527 Main and 1770 W 12th, there is no SE slot for KOR. However:
1. Vancouver House (DIALOG architecture) used **Glotman Simpson** as external SE — proof that DIALOG does use external SEs on major concrete high-rise work.
2. 1527 Main at 22 + 18 storeys is complex high-rise work — there is a real question whether DIALOG's structural group will handle it solo.
3. KOR needs to ask — if DIALOG handles structural, KOR's path is a peer-review or special studies role; if DIALOG hires external, KOR is a strong candidate.

**Glotman Simpson** is the Vancouver concrete high-rise structural incumbent (Vancouver House, multiple others). They are KOR's primary displacement competitor if KOR pursues the Vancouver DIALOG channel.
