## 2. Business & Footprint

**Who TCC is.** Trammell Crow Company is CBRE's wholly-owned development subsidiary, founded in 1948 and acquired by CBRE in 2006. It is the largest commercial developer in the US by consecutive ranking — #1 on the Commercial Property Executive list for 12 straight years. From 2022 to 2024 alone, TCC completed more than 56 million square feet of projects with a combined value of $18.3 billion.

**How TCC operates.** TCC's competitive model is local-first with national backing. The firm's ~465 employees work in 20 US offices and 6 international offices. Regional business unit leaders hold real decision-making authority; CBRE provides access to institutional capital relationships (including CBRE Investment Management as a frequent JV partner), market data, and platform infrastructure. Structural engineer selection is made by the regional team's principals and managing directors — typically during preconstruction, 12–18 months before groundbreaking. There is no centralized procurement list; relationship is everything.

**CEO direction.** Adam Nims, who joined TCC as an intern in 2006, became CEO on January 1, 2026. He has stated no major strategic pivot — the plan is quality over quantity, talent retention, and deploying proprietary data for competitive advantage. He has publicly flagged that TCC is positioned on "the edge of the next supply wave," signalling confidence in their pipeline despite a tighter capital environment.

**Sectors.** TCC develops across commercial office (Class A/trophy), industrial, data centers (15 sites, 7,000+ acres as of end-2025), life science, and multifamily (via High Street Residential). In KOR's SoCal markets, the primary KOR-relevant sectors are **multifamily (HSR)** and **life science** in San Diego.

**High Street Residential (HSR).** HSR is TCC's dedicated multifamily arm — a separate brand within TCC, not a spinoff. Their SoCal product is Class A market-rate podium and wrap communities (5–8 stories, 100–350 units, ground-floor retail). HSR projects in the San Pedro Waterfront Arts District show a preference for infill, transit-adjacent urban sites with lifestyle positioning. HSR's SoCal pipeline is the highest-probability channel for KOR to enter the TCC ecosystem.

**Capital partners.** CBRE Investment Management (a separate CBRE entity managing $144B+) is a frequent co-investor on TCC life science projects. Other capital partners include institutional equity from life insurance companies, private RE funds, and commingled vehicles. For HSR, Haseko North America was a JV partner on Jules San Pedro. Sumitomo Mitsui Trust Bank provided the $80.8M construction loan for that project.

## 5. Competitive Reality

### LA — VCA Structural Engineers is the Incumbent

VCA Structural Engineers (Monterey Park/Orange, CA) is TCC/HSR's confirmed structural engineer on two consecutive multifamily projects in Los Angeles:
- **Vivo on Harbor** (137 units, San Pedro) — VCA SE, KFA Architecture, Snyder Langston GC
- **Northridge Fashion Center** (350 units) — VCA SE, TCA Architects

VCA is a Southern California-focused structural engineering firm founded more than 40 years ago. Their specialty is precisely HSR's product type: wrap and podium multifamily, Type III Modified, mixed-use. They have offices in Orange (OC) and Monterey Park (LA County), and a Denver office, but no San Diego office. On the Modera San Diego project, VCA was listed as the SE — showing they do cross into San Diego on projects despite no local office there.

**Displacement path in LA:** VCA is entrenched via the Greg Ames / LA HSR team. Direct displacement requires either (a) Greg Ames to actively reconsider his SE roster, or (b) a new HSR LA project where the architect selection opens an alternative SE path. KFA Architecture is a recurring HSR partner in San Pedro — getting on KFA's preferred SE list is the indirect path. KFA controls architect-of-record selection which in some projects influences or recommends the SE.

For San Diego HSR multifamily, there is no confirmed SE incumbent. Brett Montgomery's explicit mandate is to build the SD HSR pipeline from scratch — he is assembling his consultant roster now. This is the primary window for KOR.

## 6. KOR Angle & How to Win

**Core narrative for Jim.** TCC / High Street Residential is actively building LA and SD multifamily pipelines with a new SD team still forming its SE relationships. KOR has local offices in both cities and brings the seismic credibility, BIM collaboration tools, and life science structural specialization that TCC's SD team needs. The strongest opening is with Brett Montgomery and Chris Tipre in SD, where VCA's incumbent lock is thinner. In LA, the angle is through the architect layer — specifically KFA Architecture, which has now committed to TCC/HSR on two consecutive San Pedro waterfront projects.

**Talking points:**

1. **Dual-market local presence, one firm.** KOR has active offices in Los Angeles and San Diego — TCC's exact SoCal footprint. No freight charges, no "who's the local contact" problem. KOR's SD team knows the Sorrento Mesa permitting environment and the coastal seismic conditions.

2. **Life science structural is a specialty, not a commodity.** Vista Sorrento Labs required "upgraded structural engineering" per TCC's own announcement — the 32,000 SF column-free floorplates and lab-grade vibration criteria for clean rooms are not standard multifamily SE work. If TCC's next SD life science project materializes, KOR needs to be on Tipre's call list before the architect is selected.

3. **HSR multifamily expertise at scale.** KOR's ability to execute 5–8 story podium/wrap projects efficiently and within schedule is the primary value proposition for Greg Ames' team. Demonstrating familiarity with the HSR product type (ground-floor retail, above-grade parking, pool deck loads) shows a consultant who won't need hand-holding.

4. **Brett Montgomery has an open roster.** Brett joined TCC from Alliance Residential in June 2024. He is actively sourcing SD, OC, and Inland Empire sites. VCA's incumbent relationship is with the LA team, not specifically with Brett. A well-timed introduction while his roster is not locked in can get KOR into the first SD HSR project.
