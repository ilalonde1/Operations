Summary

Summary and what to do

Graham Construction and Gracorp are one group with two completely different procurement realities. Graham the contractor wins BC's largest healthcare work through alliance and progressive design-build, where the structural seat is locked at team selection — Richmond Hospital's is already Entuitive's. Gracorp the developer builds market rental high-rises and procures structural itself, project by project. The second is the lane KOR actually wins in, and it is won on relationship.

So the evening is a Gracorp evening. We have zero history with Graham — no projects, no contacts, nothing — but we are currently the structural engineer for the firms that three of Gracorp's Vancouver team just left, and for the architect on two of Gracorp's biggest BC projects.

Section 01

Two companies, two conversations

Graham began in 1926 as a family contractor working on the Saskatchewan railway. It restructured during the early-1980s downturn and offered employees shares; it is now 100% employee-owned — which is the thing to compliment, because almost nobody their size is. Today: more than 3,000 people, 29 permanent offices across Canada and the US, a project backlog approaching $6 billion, and 2024 revenue of CAD $4.3 billion, placing them among Canada's three or four largest contractors. President and CEO is Andy Trewick. They have been named one of Canada's Best Managed Companies for 26 consecutive years and a Top 100 Employer for 2026.

◆ Correcting our own file

Our BD record described Graham as a "family-owned GC". That is how it started and it is wrong now — they have been employee-owned for forty years, and a second record in the same system already said so. Calling them family-owned tonight would land badly. The record is being corrected.

The distinction that matters for structural work:

Graham Construction
General contractor. Its big BC work is healthcare and institutional, delivered by progressive design-build and competitive alliance. In that model the design-builder assembles the whole consultant team — architect and engineers — at team selection, and the structural seat is filled then. It is not re-tendered later, and alliances do not announce their subs. "No structural engineer is named publicly" is the normal state of a closed team, not evidence of an opening.
Gracorp
The group's real estate development arm, founded 2008, about 35 people, Vancouver head office shared with Graham BC at Suite 700, 700 West Pender. It develops purpose-built rental, mixed-use, office and industrial across BC, Alberta and Washington State, and it hires its own consultants per project. Market residential developer work won on relationship is precisely KOR's demonstrated lane.

Spend the evening on the second one.

✕ The wording trap

All seven were invoiced to the developer, never to MCMP. MCMP is a client record in our system but has never been the paying client. Say "we've been the structural engineer on seven projects with MCMP" or "we've worked alongside them". Section 06

Two landmines

✕ 1045 Burnaby Street is in litigation — do not mention it

Gracorp Properties sued Fiera Real Estate in the Supreme Court of British Columbia in August 2024 over the 16-storey West End rental project. Fiera held 90% and Gracorp 10% as development and construction manager. Gracorp entered a $200,000 crane-swing easement with a neighbour in April 2024; Fiera issued default notices in June, terminated both agreements in July, and invoked a buyout of Gracorp's stake at 90% of fair market value, with closing expected 7 November 2024. Gracorp alleges there was no valid basis for termination. The outcome is not verified. It is a live legal matter at a party — leave it entirely alone, including the address.

✕ Do not ask for the Richmond Hospital structural seat

ReNew Canada's project record for the $1.96B Richmond Hospital Redevelopment lists the Engineer as Entuitive, with HDR Architecture as architect and Graham Design Builders LP as Phase 2 alliance partner. The seat is filled. Asking for it would signal that we do not understand how alliance teams are assembled — the opposite of the impression we want on a first meeting.

⚠ Our own BD system still carries a February 2025 note saying the structural slot "may be open" and telling us to phone Graham BC immediately, and a June 2026 note telling us to ask Graham whether their structural position on Richmond is open. Both are wrong, both are being corrected, and neither should reach a conversation.
