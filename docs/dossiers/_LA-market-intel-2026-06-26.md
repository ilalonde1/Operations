## Executive Summary (Bottom Line Up Front)

Los Angeles is the largest US construction market KOR does not yet meaningfully occupy — and the one where KOR's identity is most structurally transferable. Four structural demand vectors converge in 2026–27: (1) a multifamily pipeline surge (15,735 units permitted in the 12 months to March 2026, +85% YoY, with Q1 2026 construction starts at a 3-year high), driven by SB 79 and developer catch-up; (2) a live, underserved NDC seismic-retrofit market with ~1,500 covered buildings in their Year-3-to-Year-10 compliance window and fees of $100–400K/building, where only ~6% have been retrofitted; (3) a Palisades/Eaton wildfire rebuild demand surge (14,834 units damaged/destroyed, all requiring full structural engineering under 2022 CBC, representing an estimated $150–300M in SE fees over 3–5 years); and (4) post-acquisition drift at Englekirk/WSP opening the boutique mid-market seismic developer lane in the 2026–2027 window. KOR's most actionable near-term entry is owner-direct NDC retrofit (no architect gate; principal-led SE earns the relationship), followed by riding Vancouver developer and architect relationships into open project seats. The constraint is LA market presence and brand, not licensure — Markulin holds the CA SE.

## 1. Market Structure & Segmentation

### 1.1 Overall LA Construction Economy [C]
- California is the largest construction economy in North America: ~$31B/yr private nonresidential + ~$55B/yr state/local put-in-place (US Census/AGC 2024). LA County represents ~35–40% of CA volume.
- Construction cost spike: Q1 2025 saw hard costs spike 6% — the steepest quarterly rise in over a decade; 10.5% year-over-year growth by early 2025. Lumber and some steel inputs retreated from 2022–23 peaks; contractor availability improving entering 2026.
- FIFA World Cup 2026 and 2028 Summer Olympics ("no build" strategy using existing venues) are generating hospitality and LAX ($14B upgrade) demand, but **not major structural new-build opportunity for KOR.** [C — Dodge; smartcitiesdive.com]

### 1.3 Woodframe / Podium Multifamily [C]
- The **dominant volume segment** — vast pipeline of 5-over-2 and 5-over-1 Type V/III podium across Westside, San Fernando Valley, South Bay, and TOC corridors.

- **LA woodframe SE market is fragmented** — no firm dominates the way Saiful Bouquet dominates trophy towers. T&S Structural Engineers, JN Engineering, LFA, and numerous boutiques compete. This fragmentation is genuine opportunity. [C — SE firms research agent]

### 1.9 Seismic Retrofit — The Structural-Led Lane [C]

This is the highest-confidence LA SE demand opportunity for KOR in 2026. Every building requires an owner-retained licensed SE — no architect Prime gate.

**Soft-Story Retrofit (Ordinance 183893):**
- **Total buildings: 12,347** (LADBS official Feb 2024 Compliance Report; contractor sites round to ~13,500 — use 12,347 as the verified figure). [C — LADBS Feb 2024 report]
- **Compliance as of Feb 1, 2024:** Plans submitted 95%; permits issued 89%; **Certificate of Compliance (complete): 76% — 2,970 buildings still outstanding.** Of these: ~1,593 have permits but unfinished construction; ~1,377 have no permit.
- **All 7-year compliance deadlines have expired** (orders were mailed 2016–2017 in waves; the last 7-year clock ran out by late 2024). All 2,970 non-compliant buildings are in active enforcement — misdemeanor charges, fines, liens, vacate orders.
- **Per-building costs:** small (2–4 units) $45–95K; medium (10–20 units) $100–250K; large (20+ units) $200–500K+.
- **Residual market rough estimate:** 2,970 buildings × $100–150K average = **$297M–$446M** in remaining soft-story construction value. [P — my estimate; no official total]
- **SE read: commoditized, skip as primary entry.** This market is owned by vertically-integrated contractor-engineer hybrids — **Optimum Seismic (~3,500 buildings retrofitted in SoCal)**, Alpha Structural, Partner ESI — who bundle design, fabrication, and construction with SE fees as loss leaders at $5–15K/building. A standalone SE cannot compete profitably here. Value only as a volume relationship-builder.

**Non-Ductile Concrete Retrofit (Ordinance 184081):**
- **Total buildings: ~1,500** (LADBS still finalizing the covered-buildings list; QuakeStrong 2024 cites 1,200+). Pre-Jan 13, 1977 concrete construction; SFH/duplex excluded. [C — LADBS; Q4 2021 Omgivning/MWest white paper]
- **Compliance as of Q4 2021** (the only public snapshot available — no 2025/2026 official update): 3-yr checklist submitted: 31%; 10-yr plans submitted: 11%; 25-yr construction complete: **3.7%**. QuakeStrong early-2024 cites ~6% construction-complete. No live LADBS dashboard exists.
- **Compliance deadlines (from order issuance, most ~Jan 2018):**
  - 3 years: Submit NDC Checklist → due ~2021 (lapsed; enforcement active)
  - **10 years: Submit retrofit plans or demolition plans → due ~2028** (the next major inflection point; building owners without SE engaged must hire now or face enforcement)
  - 25 years: Complete construction → due ~2043
- **Cost benchmarks:** Retrofit-only: $30–50/sqft; all-in: $50–100/sqft. A 68,000 sqft building = $2.1M–$6.8M.
- **Total NDC market rough estimate:** 1,400 remaining buildings × $1M–$4M average = **$1.4B–$5.6B** in structural work, running to ~2043. [P — my estimate]
- **Entry product for KOR:** A **$15–50K Phase 1 ASCE-41 Tier 1/2 feasibility study** provides the building owner a compliance path and scoping report. This earns KOR the Year-10 design contract ($150–400K) and a relationship the contractor-hybrids cannot replicate.
- **NDC SE competitors:** **Degenkolb** (most prominent — consults for City of LA on the ordinance program PLUS does private NDC work), **SGH** (Lane Building SEAOSC award, confirmed NDC of record), **Saiful Bouquet** (2025 SEAOSC retrofit award), **Thornton Tomasetti, ZFA** (self-described NDC specialist), **Structural Focus** (DTLA boutique, "hundreds" of existing buildings + nearly two dozen National Register properties). [C — SE firms research agent; seismic agent]

**LA County NDC Ordinance (NEW, 2025):** LA County (unincorporated areas + county-owned buildings — distinct from City of LA) introduced a high-rise NDC ordinance April 24, 2025 with a 20-year compliance window. A separate but related pool of buildings. [C — seismic research agent]

**Steel Moment Frame:** **No mandatory SMF ordinance in the City of Los Angeles.** Torrance adopted SMF requirements March 2023; LA has not followed. [C — seismic research agent]

**Competitive SE fact:** Degenkolb is the #1 NDC ordinance firm — they consult for the City of LA on the program design AND do private NDC work (Dignity Health, Loma Linda, multiple municipal programs). Any KOR NDC marketing must position against Degenkolb, not just LFA and Miyamoto.

### 1.10 Wildfire Rebuild — Palisades / Eaton (January 2025) [C]

An unplanned but immediately large SE demand event. **Every rebuild requires full structural engineering under the 2022 California Building Code** — no exemption for like-for-like replacement.

| Metric | Data |
|---|---|
| Total units damaged/destroyed | **14,834 housing units** (13,142 parcels) |
| Rebuild applications received | 6,116 |
| Permits issued | 2,894 (~47% of applications) |
| Projects under active construction | ~1,420 |
| Fully completed buildings | **28** — massive backlog, 3–5 year demand runway |

**SE opportunity estimate:** If 10,000 homes are rebuilt at average SE fee of $15–30K each, that equals **$150–300M in structural engineering fees** concentrated in LA's highest-cost West Side zip codes. Miyamoto launched a dedicated "Rebuild LA" program (January 2025) and is the most visible SE firm in this lane. KOR is not positioned here yet; it would require a local Los Angeles presence and fast-track permit experience. **This is a medium-term opportunity to monitor, not an immediate entry target.** [C — CalMatters; LA City Planning; GlobeSt]
