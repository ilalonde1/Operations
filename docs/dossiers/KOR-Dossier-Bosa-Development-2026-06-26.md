## 2. Business & Footprint

**Origin.** Nat Bosa started as a construction labourer in the 1960s, co-founded Bosa Brothers (1966) as a GC, then pivoted to development through the 1980s. He is credited with pioneering downtown San Diego's residential condo revival beginning in 1998, expanding Whistler into an international resort destination, and reshaping downtown Vancouver and Calgary. Inducted into the Urban Development Institute Hall of Fame (2016); 2013 Alonzo Award recipient.

**Ryan Bosa.** Nat's son. Grew up on construction sites, ran construction through his mid-20s, then led the California expansion. In 2003 he separately founded Embassy Development (master planning, Vancouver/Seattle/Calgary), which merged back into Bosa Development in 2017 when he assumed the presidency. The "Embassy BOSA" branding appears on US condo projects.

**What they build.** High-rise concrete residential condos (35–53 storeys), mixed-use podium towers, and multi-phase urban master plans. Bosa builds its own projects — they are their own general contractor, which means they control the entire design-engineering team selection process internally.

**Geographic footprint (active):** British Columbia (Vancouver, West Vancouver, New Westminster, Victoria), Alberta (Calgary), Washington (Bellevue/Seattle), California (San Diego). Also historical/complete projects in Oregon, Hawaii, Arizona, San Francisco.

**Family company separation.** In 2022 Bosa Development (Nat/Ryan) sued Bosa Properties (Colin — Nat's brother's side of the family) over US trademark use of the Bosa name, resulting in Bosa Properties renaming its US subsidiary. The two companies operate completely independently. KOR's contact data has historically included Bosa Properties personnel — those contacts (including any @townline.com emails) are not Bosa Development and have been excluded from this dossier.

## 6. KOR Angle & How to Win

### Why KOR's position is actually strong

1. **Warm relationship, not cold approach.** Ryan Bosa knows KOR. Jim is not walking in as a stranger — this is a re-engagement of a documented long-term client.
2. **Local presence = real advantage on First & Island.** GS services San Diego from LA. KOR has local engineers who can be on-site for concrete pours, RFI turnarounds, and pre-pour inspections without travel overhead. On a 35-floor tower with a tight budget and tariff risk, that matters.
3. **SD portfolio credibility.** Simone Little Italy (2024 ACI award) is a direct comparable to Bosa's product type — high-rise residential concrete podium tower in downtown SD.
4. **The window is First & Island.** Andia is fully underway with GS locked in. First & Island is the next SD tower — permit filed, no SE publicly announced. This is the entry point.
5. **Bosa is its own GC.** They don't go through a GC to select the SE — Paul Lamme and the development team choose structural directly. Jim can talk directly to the decision-maker.

### Talking points for Jim

- *"We've worked with Bosa Development for 20+ years — Ryan's testimonial is on our website. We know how you build. Let's talk about what's next in San Diego."*
- *"Our team is based in San Diego — when your concrete inspector needs us at 6am before a pour, we're 20 minutes away, not flying from Vancouver or driving from LA."*
- *"Simone Little Italy won the 2024 ACI San Diego Best Residential Structure award — same product type as your towers. We know how to do this work here."*
- *"First & Island is the obvious next step — we'd love to be part of your team for that."*

### Four concrete asks for the SD meeting

1. **Ask for a pre-design meeting on First & Island** — not to pitch, but to understand the program and offer preliminary thoughts. Structural engineers who engage pre-permit have a structural advantage in selection.
2. **Request an introduction to Nat Bosa** through Ryan or Paul Lamme — Nat is still active and his blessing matters for major consultant decisions.
3. **Offer a local site walk on Andia** (even if GS is the SE) — just to demonstrate SD presence and build the relationship for the next project. Ask to meet Paul Lamme at the Andia site.
4. **Leave a one-page KOR SD credentials sheet** tailored to Bosa: SD project photos (Simone, Grande, Bayside), ACI award mention, and the explicit note that KOR has been working with Bosa Development for 20+ years in BC and Alberta.
