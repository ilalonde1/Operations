## 2. Where to register — the "anything other than what we're doing" answer

We already *see* every public VCH tender via BC Bid — that is passive visibility. Being *invited* to formal/larger non-alliance work requires being on the roster, applied for separately:

- **Apply through BC Bid + Bonfire together.** Once a year a Notice of Intent (NOI) is posted on BC Bid inviting new suppliers to begin the pre-qualified application process; the RFPQ submission itself runs through the Fraser Health Bonfire portal at fraserhealth.bonfirehub.ca.
- Being prequalified gates invitations to formal/larger solicitations and raises the direct-award threshold (~$50k vs ~$25k for non-listed firms).
- It is not one-and-done: at each annual refresh, currently-listed firms must re-apply or be removed from the list.
- **Scope nuance (verified):** non-prequalified firms can still receive small direct awards (under ~$25k); and Bonfire is not the sole portal — it is BC Bid + Bonfire together, with the BC Bid NOI giving the application/submission details.

## 4. Why the list is irrelevant for the marquee towers

Confirmed 3-0 against Infrastructure BC primary sources: VCH's flagship acute-care projects are delivered via a project-specific Single Target Outturn Cost (TOC) Alliance Selection Process (RFQ then RFP), not a standing roster. Richmond Hospital Phases 2 & 3 ($1.96B, VCH-owned) lists its Alliance Partners as Vancouver Coastal Health, Infrastructure BC, Graham Design Builders LP and HDR Architecture — one integrated team. The RFQ mandates a Respondent team with at least one construction NOP and one design NOP as distinct legal entities. A structural firm cannot register directly with the owner for these; it participates only inside a proponent team. So for the towers, the lever is the HDR / Graham / Kasian relationship — exactly as KOR-VCH-Deep-Analysis-2026-06-23 already lays out — and being on any VCH/LMFM list does nothing for them.

## 5. Eligibility + the tier structure (how the roster actually works)

The live list is not a flat yes/no roster — firms are pre-qualified cell-by-cell across two tiers, based on demonstrated reference experience:

- **Care type:** Acute Care Projects vs Non-Acute Care Projects (separate columns).
- **Consulting-fee band:** under $150K | $150K–$1M | $1M–$5M | over $5M.
- **Hard gate:** EGBC licensure (KOR clears this as an EGBC Accredited Employer since Jul 2023). References are then checked to assign which cells a firm is qualified for.
- **Strategic read:** KOR does NOT need acute-care credit to get listed. Small structural firms appear in only 1–2 cells (Equilibrium Consulting, Sense Engineering, Wicke Herfst Maver, C.Y. Loh) — proof you get listed at the band your references support. KOR's realistic entry is Non-Acute + lower fee bands, which is exactly the "first healthcare credit" play; acute / >$5M cells come later.
