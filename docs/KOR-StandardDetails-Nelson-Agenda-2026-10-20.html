How the general notes and typical details are kept today

For Nelson Yu · from Ian Lalonde · 30 minutes, at your screen

When Michael left, a lot of how our standards are kept went with him, and the rest lives in a few people's heads. I'd like to write down how it actually works today — so it doesn't depend on anyone's memory, and so anything I build fits what you already do rather than the other way round. You know this better than anyone, so I'd rather hear it from you than guess.

What I think I understand — please correct anything that's wrong

  • The published set is the read-only PDFs in P:\Projects\00 Templates\000 Kor Master PDF Files — Concrete and Wood Frame, each file dated, older copies in OLD.
  • Changes come in as engineer markups in 03 Cover Sheet Markups, get drafted, go to Completed Ready for Backcheck, then Backchecked, then the PDF is republished.
  • In Revit, the general notes live in the project templates in I:\009_REVIT CUSTOMED\000_Project_Templates — Concrete Tower R24, Steel R24, and the Typical Details container R23. A new job copies the template, renames it and detaches it.
  • In CAD, the cover sheets in I:\Kor Structural\001 Cover Sheets are built from the G001–G006 note blocks XREF'd in from Cover Sheets Blocks; the typical details are KOR TYPICAL DETAILS.dwg in 002 Typical Details.
  • In July Jim made Revit the master, and Simon's Revit-vs-CAD review went to the Engineering Committee to bring the two sets back in line.

What only you can tell me

  1. Who does what today? For each one — the Revit templates, the wood-frame general notes, the CAD cover sheets, the typical details DWG and the published PDFs — who makes the change, who backchecks it, and who publishes it.
  2. Where is the current wood-frame general notes file in Revit? In the template folder I can only find wood-frame files marked Do Not Use or moved to Old, but the Revit wood-frame general notes PDF was updated on 6 October. Same question for the low-rise concrete general notes.
  3. With Revit as the master, how is CAD kept in step? By hand after each Revit change? Who checks that the two still match — and where did Simon's review end up?
  4. When a standard changes, how does everyone find out? Jobs that already copied the template keep the old notes — does anyone update live jobs, or do the changes only apply to new ones?
  5. Which Revit version are new jobs starting in now? The templates are R23 and R24.
  6. What takes up most of your time keeping all this current — and what would you fix first?
