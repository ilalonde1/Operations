# CA healthcare / OSHPD seismic — KOR entry strategy (research 2026-06-18)

## Forced-demand pipeline (KOR's seismic lane)
OSHPD/HCAI 2030 deadline (SB 1953): every CA general-acute-care building must be SPC-3/4/5 + NPC-5 by 1/1/2030. AB 869 (2024) extends to 2033/2035 only if a Seismic Compliance Plan was filed by 1/1/2026 + CDs by 3/1/2026.
- **251 of ~410 CA hospitals non-compliant; 674 non-compliant buildings.** LA County: 73 of 93 campuses. RAND est. $34B-$143B statewide; ~$92M avg/building. Only ~45% on track.
- **HCAI publishes PUBLIC building-level compliance datasets** = a forced-demand pursuit list (potential future funnel source):
  - data.chhs.ca.gov/dataset/seismic-compliance-data-by-county-for-general-acute-care-hospitals
  - data.chhs.ca.gov/dataset/seismic-deadline-extensions-granted-for-california-hospitals
- Systems: Sutter (closing Alta Bates Berkeley -> new Emeryville campus, open ~2033); Kaiser (portfolio program, hires SE owner-direct); Dignity/CommonSpirit (Swinerton MTCAP, 10 NorCal campuses); Providence (4 LA need work); Adventist (20 hospitals); UC Davis (California Tower).

## Retrofit market beyond hospitals
- LA non-ductile concrete (Ord 184081): ~1,500 buildings, deadlines 2027-28, ASCE-41 high-expertise = KOR's lane ($200K-$500K+/bldg). Incumbents Degenkolb/SGH/Saiful Bouquet. West Hollywood hired Degenkolb as program SE (similar city-SE roles may open in Beverly Hills/Burbank/Glendale).
- LA soft-story (Ord 183893): ~5,400 remaining, Apr 2026 deadline — but low-expertise/contractor-dominated (Optimum Seismic, Alpha Structural). Low ROI.
- SF (CBSP non-ductile concrete, active), Santa Monica, Oakland (Tier 3 Feb 2025).
