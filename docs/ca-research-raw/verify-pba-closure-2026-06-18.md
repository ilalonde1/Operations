## Part 2 — What ACTUALLY closed on December 31, 2025 (the real PBA)

The closure that happened was a **completely different firm**:

**PBA Engineering, P.C.** (formerly Paul Beck Associates, P.A.)  
12 Kulick Road, Fairfield, NJ 07004  
Website: [https://www.pbanj.com/](https://www.pbanj.com/)

This New Jersey structural engineering firm, founded by Paul C. Beck, PE in November 1978, **officially closed December 31, 2025** after 47 years when Paul Beck retired. Their announcement (live on pbanj.com as of this research pass) reads verbatim:

> "PBA Engineering, P.C. will officially close on December 31, 2025. Key members of the PBA team will be joining KSI Professional Engineers so they may continue the high-quality work and client service that PBA has been known for since 1978."

This is the only confirmed "PBA + KSI" closure. It is a New Jersey firm serving the Tri-State area — no California operations, no Orange County clients, no connection to Architects Orange / TCA / KTGY / JPI.

## The Fabrication Mechanism

The original research almost certainly performed a search for "PBA Engineering closed KSI 2025," found the genuine NJ closure announcement on pbanj.com, and then incorrectly attributed that closure to Patel Burica & Associates (same abbreviation, both structural engineering, similar web presence). The OC architect-transition claim then appears to have been extrapolated from the (false) CA PBA closure premise.

This is a **classic AI hallucination pattern**: accurate underlying fact (NJ PBA did close) + incorrect entity attribution (wrong firm) + logical extrapolation presented as fact (therefore OC architects need a new SE).
