## 4. Their Model & Positioning — How Glotman Wins Vancouver-Developer-in-CA Work

### The "Coattail" Model (Geoffrey Glotman's own words)
Glotman explicitly follows BC developers down the I-5 corridor. The model has four stages:

1. **Establish trusted relationship in Vancouver** — Become the default SE on a developer's home-market projects where relationship and reliability are proven over multiple cycles.
2. **Follow the developer when they enter a new market** — The developer brings their Vancouver "team" (architect + SE) because it removes procurement risk and replicates a process that worked.
3. **Build a local office to sustain it** — LA office opened 2016, anchored by Chris Smith (California SE license). The office handles local licensing, plan checks, and peer review, reducing friction with authorities.
4. **Extend into non-BC developer work organically** — Once credentialed and known in LA/SD, Glotman picks up local developers (Cypress Equity, Holland, Magnum) who value Canadian expertise in high-density seismic concrete.

### Competitive positioning statement (synthesized from BCBusiness):
> "Most of the highrise stuff [in San Diego], the large majority is Canadian developers, and they're all using Canadian architects."
> — Geoffrey Glotman [Confirmed]

Glotman's message: Canadian firms bring superior highrise density experience, seismic engineering sophistication, and a collaborative work style that reduces coordination overhead. This is explicitly positioned against local SE firms as differentiated.

### Key structural specializations they emphasize in CA:
- **Performance-based seismic design** for high-rise concrete (Chris Smith is certified for CA post-earthquake assessments)
- **Mass timber / CLT** — first-in-LA CLT diaphragm, first CLT floor system, now doing mass timber for Westbank in Vancouver + elsewhere
- **Adaptive reuse / seismic retrofit** — Western Pacific, 843 N Spring
- **Complex podium structures** — post-tensioned slabs over deep basements, sub-water-table parking (Ariel Suites)

KOR has:
- Bosa Halifax & Willingdon (Burnaby) — 43 and 60-storey towers [Confirmed — KOR website]
- Bosa Evolution (Calgary) [Confirmed — KOR memory]

Glotman has Bosa Pacific Gate, Broadway Block, Andia (all San Diego).

**The opening:** Bosa has explicitly paused SD (Nat Bosa's public statement). When Bosa re-enters SD or enters a new market (Sacramento? Bay Area? LA?), they will look for a structural engineer. KOR's Canadian track record on Bosa high-rises (Halifax & Willingdon is one of the most prominent Bosa projects) is a direct credential. KOR should position itself as the "next-market Bosa partner" — the SE that Glotman cannot service because of capacity and Vancouver mega-project load.

**The Grande** (KOR's prior SD work for Bosa) further demonstrates that KOR has done Bosa highrise in San Diego specifically — this is a direct competitive credential against Glotman on Bosa's home turf.

### 6b. Capacity Overload — Senakw + Brentwood Tie Up Vancouver Bandwidth

Glotman's 50 engineers are committed to projects that will not complete until the early 2030s (Senakw, Brentwood). Taking on a new 40-storey tower in Sacramento or Oakland simultaneously is structurally difficult for a 90-person firm. This is KOR's opening for any Vancouver developer entering a new CA market.

### 6c. Mass Timber Is Glotman's Lane — KOR Should Not Chase It

Glotman has earned first-mover advantage on CLT/mass timber in LA (first CLT diaphragm, first CLT floor). KOR's strength is seismic concrete high-rise and heritage retrofit. Do not try to compete on mass timber credentials.

### 6d. Seismic Heritage & Retrofit — Potential KOR Differentiator

KOR's heritage/seismic rehabilitation expertise (EGBC, SMP background) is a differentiator in CA where there is a massive retrofit market (soft-story apartments, URM buildings, historic structures). Glotman does some retrofit work (Western Pacific) but it is not their primary identity. KOR could build a CA retrofit niche separate from the developer-channel.

### 6e. Onni Group — Untested KOR Relationship

Glotman holds both confirmed LA Onni projects. Onni is a major BC developer with 10+ active North American markets. If KOR has any Onni relationship in Canada, that is a path to Onni USA work. [Unverified — KOR should check its own client list]

### 6f. MVE Architects Relationship — SoCal Entry Vector

MVE + Partners is the architect on three Glotman CA projects (Ritz-Carlton Newport, UCI Research Park, 9908 Santa Monica). MVE is Orange County / LA's largest multifamily architecture firm. If KOR could establish an MVE relationship independently, that bypasses Glotman's developer-channel lock-in entirely. MVE brings its own developer clients and assigns the SE.

### 6g. No NorCal / Bay Area Presence Beyond San Jose

Glotman's Bay Area presence is entirely Westbank-tied (San Jose campus). Oakland, Berkeley, the Peninsula, Sacramento — these are open markets where Glotman has no footprint. KOR's developing Sacramento and Bay Area streams (from prior research sessions) are targeting the right gap.

### 6h. Seattle — Bosa 3rd & Cherry Is Stalled

Bosa's 57-storey Seattle tower has been paused since July 2022 and appears unresolved as of 2026. When/if this project resumes, Glotman is the incumbent SE — but there may be a re-bid opportunity if Bosa restructures the project or changes partners. [Unverified]

## 7. KOR Strategic Recommendations

### Priority 1: Re-activate the Bosa CA channel via KOR's Canada-proven track record
- KOR should formally reach out to Bosa's project development team citing Halifax & Willingdon + The Grande SD as the credential
- Frame as: "We're the SE that knows Bosa's system, is not capacity-constrained by Vancouver mega-projects, and is licensed in CA"
- Target: Bosa's next CA market entry (likely LA or NorCal, given SD pause)

### Priority 2: Enter the Westbank San Jose campus as a second-choice SE
- Westbank's Fountain Alley (BIG-designed, 768 units) is still in planning. Glotman is the named SE. But Westbank's pipeline is enormous — they may split packages
- Longer-term: Westbank is beginning NorCal / Sacramento / Oakland work. KOR's existing research streams in those markets should flag Westbank-linked projects

### Priority 3: Pursue MVE + Partners as an architect relationship (not developer)
- MVE is the architect on Ritz-Carlton Newport, UCI Research Park, and 9908 Santa Monica — all Glotman projects
- An MVE relationship would put KOR on shortlists for SoCal luxury/resort residential independently of the Vancouver developer channel

### Priority 4: Build a CA seismic retrofit / heritage niche
- KOR's EGBC seismic heritage credentials are a genuine differentiator in the CA retrofit market
- No BC competitor (including Glotman) has prominently staked this ground in CA
- Target: California state-mandated soft-story retrofit programs, URM assessments, historic adaptive reuse (large volume in LA, SF, Oakland)

### Priority 5: Stay out of mass timber / CLT
- Glotman has first-mover advantage and industry recognition in this niche
- Market demand for CLT SE in CA is growing but Glotman is effectively the trusted expert
- KOR's time and CA marketing investment is better spent on seismic concrete high-rise and heritage

### Priority 6: Sacramento and Bay Area — exploit the gap before Glotman enters
- Glotman has no confirmed NorCal footprint outside the Westbank San Jose campus
- KOR's active research streams in Sacramento and the Bay Area (per existing ops data) are correctly targeted
- Speed to first project matters: establishing a reference project in Sacramento before Glotman arrives is strategically valuable
