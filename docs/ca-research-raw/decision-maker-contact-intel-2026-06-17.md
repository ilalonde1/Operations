# CA Decision-Maker Contact Intelligence Dossier
**Prepared:** 2026-06-17

**Research methodology:** Public-source web research (LinkedIn, company websites, press releases, email-pattern aggregators RocketReach/LeadIQ/ZoomInfo headers). No paid contact databases were accessed.  
**Anti-fabrication rule:** No individual email addresses or phone numbers are asserted as verified unless confirmed from a public authoritative source. Where a pattern is inferred it is marked [Unverified pattern]. Apollo.io / Hunter.io verification requirements are listed per person.

## Tooling Reality Statement

Web research reliably yields: name, title, company, LinkedIn URL, firm email pattern, public bio/background. It does NOT yield: verified individual email addresses, direct-dial phone numbers, or mobile numbers for private-company executives. **Verified contact data for these individuals requires Apollo.io (US) or Hunter.io (domain-level) with API or UI access.** Each person's entry below flags exactly what Apollo/Hunter would fill.

**How KOR should stand up Apollo/Hunter:**
- Apollo.io: Free tier allows ~50 email reveals/month; paid Basic ($49/mo) yields ~150 exports. Search by person name + company domain. Best for direct email + phone of US contacts (Wayland, Eadie, McCullough, Collin, McLarand, Ronge, Howard).
- Hunter.io: Best for domain-level pattern confirmation + bulk verification. Use "Email Finder" with first/last + domain.
- Recommended workflow: confirm pattern via Hunter domain search first ($0 on free tier for pattern), then use Apollo for the actual verified address + direct dial.
- Priority order for KOR: (1) Wayland [NorCal], (2) McCullough/Eadie [Kennedy Wilson/Lindley], (3) McLarand [Jim's contact], (4) Collin [Amara Bay], (5) Ronge [SD], (6) Howard [DBRDS Sacramento], (7) Bosa [Vancouver relationship play].
