## PART 5 — KOR STRATEGIC CONTEXT

### Existing Threads
- **Onni Group (Vancouver):** KOR has a relationship with the Vancouver Onni team. Onni LA is a direct extension — same parent company. The critical question is whether Onni uses a consistent SE across both markets. Their current LA SE default is unconfirmed (not publicly disclosed on any LA project). Given that all Onni LA towers use either Chris Dikeakos (Vancouver AOR) or IBI Group (now Arcadis), a Vancouver-to-LA SE carry-over is plausible — if Englekirk (now WSP) was the historical LA SE, the WSP-integration friction creates an opening.
- **Intergulf Development Group (Vancouver → LA):** KOR relationship via Vancouver. LA contact: **Brian Buchanan, VP USA** is the primary US lead. The Hartford Apartments (218 units, Westlake, completed 2024) is their first LA delivery — SE not publicly disclosed but KOR should know this from the Vancouver side. Nabih Faris (CEO) is the principal relationship holder.

### Competitor SE Holding Dominance
- **Saiful Bouquet** — Gensler's default in LA; strong on Crescent Heights via One Beverly Hills
- **LFA (Labib Funk)** — Cityview + Jamison + KTGY pipeline default; mid-high-rise ceiling (~8-12fl most confirmed projects)
- **MKA** — Brookfield tier; not LA-resident firm
- **WSP/Englekirk** — Carmel Partners on Alloy; formerly Englekirk brand on several DTLA towers pre-2021
- **Nabih Youssef** — Johnson Fain default; office/commercial heavy

### WSP/Englekirk Loosening Note
The 2021 Englekirk integration into WSP was flagged as a strategic opportunity: Englekirk had deep personal relationships with LA developers that WSP's corporate structure may not sustain. Any developers or architects who worked with Englekirk principals specifically (not the WSP brand) are warm targets. The Carmel Partners/Alloy selection of WSP post-acquisition suggests at least one developer stayed. Others — particularly mid-market developers who prefer boutique SE relationships — may be available.

## PART 6 — TOP NEAR-TERM RELATIONSHIP TARGETS (4-LINE STRATEGIC READ)

**1. Onni Group LA (via Vancouver thread).** KOR already has the Vancouver relationship — this is the single highest-leverage activation. Onni LA's SE for the in-progress 60-story tower and the Mid-Wilshire 45-story is publicly unknown. The LA development office (1031 S Broadway) runs autonomously but Onni's founder/family makes SE selections influenced by trust and track record. Move: request a warm intro from the Vancouver Onni contact to the LA director of engineering/development — one conversation at the right level can unlock multiple towers.

**2. Intergulf LA (via Vancouver thread + Brian Buchanan).** Brian Buchanan (VP USA) is the designated US lead. Hartford Apartments (Westlake, 218 units) just delivered — Intergulf's LA pipeline will grow. SE for Hartford is unknown; if it wasn't KOR, the timing is right to get on the next project. Direct outreach to Buchanan, with Nabih Faris as the warm intro path, is the play. Target: be the SE of record on Intergulf's second and third LA buildings.

**3. Jamison Properties + KTGY (new/cold target).** Jamison is LA's Koreatown powerhouse (5,900+ units delivered, 2,000 under construction) with a tight repeating team: KTGY as AOR + LFA as SE. LFA is the incumbent. KOR's angle: Jamison is expanding beyond Koreatown (Sawtelle, Hollywood, Mid-Wilshire conversions) and doing larger buildings. On anything 10+ stories where LFA's mid-rise comfort zone becomes a constraint, KOR should position as the high-rise complement. Entry point: Keith McCloskey (KTGY LA Principal) is the architect relationship; Garrett Lee (President, Jamison Properties) is the developer call.

**4. Carmel Partners / Architect WPA+SCB (cold target, WSP incumbent).** Carmel Partners' Alloy used WSP (Englekirk legacy). Will Cipes (SVP Dev, LA) is the development contact. The natural moment to dislodge WSP is on Carmel's next LA project — they have no further confirmed LA pipeline publicly, but as LA's #1 Arts District developer post-Alloy they are actively sourcing sites. KOR's seismic credentials from Vancouver high-rise (same seismic zone character) + boutique-SE agility vs. WSP's corporate scale is the differentiator pitch.
