## Stage-Gate Rule Applied Throughout
An SE seat is "open" ONLY if: (1) project is EARLY-stage, AND (2) no SE is named. Design/permitting/construction = SE is locked even if unnamed in press. "Unnamed in press releases" is normal for structural sub-consultants on developer-led projects — it does NOT mean vacant.

## CLAIM 1: Hanover / KTGY "Coyote Creek Village" — "Biggest Open NorCal Seat"
*(San Jose, ~1,140 units, 3 buildings — claimed SE unnamed, open)*

**VERDICT: REFUTED (stage-gate fails for Buildings A & B; Building C ambiguous but SE almost certainly locked across all three)**

**Evidence:**
- **Building A ("Hanover Parkside," 397u):** Broke ground October 28, 2025. Actively under construction, completion targeted Q4 2027. SE was contracted at design-development in 2024, well before this groundbreaking.
- **Building B ("Hanover Coyote Creek," 372u):** Scheduled groundbreaking Q2 2026 — at or past today's date. Even if not yet started, SE selection on a market-rate mid-rise happens 12–18 months before groundbreaking (SD/DD phase ~2024–2025). Seat is locked.
- **Building C (371u):** No announced groundbreaking timeline. However, this is a 22-acre master plan where Hanover almost certainly retained one SE across all three buildings for continuity.
- **SE named publicly:** No. Press credits consistently stop at Hanover (developer) + KTGY (architect) + SummerHill/Pacific Cos (housing partners).

**Conclusion:** The "open seat" claim is not credible for any building. The SE is locked but simply not credited in press, which is industry norm. Drop this claim from pursuit planning.
