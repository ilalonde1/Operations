## Executive Summary

The NorCal multifamily/mixed-use SE market is bifurcated: (1) a Bay Area institutional tier dominated by KPFF and Forell|Elsesser (civic/HE/high-rise — not KOR's lane) and (2) a mid-rise podium/wood-frame developer-residential tier with fragmented incumbency and genuine open seats. The pipeline is real: 17,400 units under construction Bay Area-wide, a Sacramento decade-high delivery pace (4,662 units through Oct 2025), and a 2026-on project wave from Holland, Westbank, Greystar, Hanover/KTGY, Jay Paul/SCB, and Sand Hill. KOR's #1 NorCal SE target remains Nelson Structural (Irvine-based, 11 staff, incumbent on Holland/MVE at Gateway Crossing), but the broader ecosystem reveals 8–10 additional attack vectors beyond the Holland/Stevens Creek immediate priority. The most actionable near-term targets beyond Stevens Creek are: (A) Westbank/San Jose tower pipeline (James Cheng + Steinberg Hart + SCB as architects — Glotman Simpson is incumbent, a BC firm KOR knows), (B) KTGY/Hanover's Coyote Creek Village (1,140 units, SE unnamed), (C) Jay Paul/SCB CityView Tower (360 units, 27-story, SE unnamed), and (D) Greystar Sacramento's Marlowe Natomas/Elk Grove pipeline (SE unnamed). All four have unconfirmed SE assignments as of June 2026.
