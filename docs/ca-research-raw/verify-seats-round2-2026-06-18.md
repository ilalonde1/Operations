## 8. Midway Rising — San Diego Sports Arena (Safdie Rabines, ~4,000u)

**VERDICT: [Unverifiable — legally blocked / delayed; ground lease NOT signed; SE not named in any public source]**

- Midway Rising is in active legal limbo. Two consecutive court rulings struck down the city's height limit exemption (30-foot coastal height limit applies). The development team is seeking California Supreme Court review and exploring density bonus law as an alternative path.
- As of December 2025, *"the timeline has now been extended even further"* — KPBS. A proposed lease agreement is still in negotiations and requires City Council + committee approval before signing.
- Construction was originally targeted for winter 2026; now the soonest is *"end of 2026"* even under an optimistic scenario. Given the legal cloud, 2027+ is more realistic.
- Project team: Chelsea Investment Corporation, Legends, Zephyr, Safdie Rabines Architects. **No structural engineer is named** on the official team page (midwayrising.info) or in any news source. Bowman provides civil/survey services only.
- Stage gate: ground lease not signed, height limit unresolved, no permits, no construction = genuinely early-stage. SE selection is premature at this stage of legal uncertainty.
- Sources:
  - KPBS Dec 2025: https://www.kpbs.org/news/local/2025/12/05/after-multiple-delays-state-housing-law-could-clear-a-path-forward-for-midway-rising-development
  - Axios Sept 2025: https://www.axios.com/local/san-diego/2025/09/25/san-diego-midway-rising-development-plan-vote
  - Safdie Rabines portfolio: https://www.safdierabines.com/portfolio/midway-rising/
  - OB Rag height limit analysis Dec 2025: https://obrag.org/2025/12/how-midway-rising-developers-plan-to-sidestep-requirements-and-avoid-the-30-foot-height-limit/

**KOR assessment:** Legal uncertainty is so severe that this is not a near-term prospect regardless of SE status. Ground lease not signed. Flag as watch list only; revisit if lease executes in 2026.

## Final 2-Line Read

**Seats that survive scrutiny as genuinely open:** Fox Hills (5757 Uplander, Q1 2027 start, SE unnamed) and Calle De Luna (preliminary plans, SE unnamed) are the two most defensible "open" candidates — but both carry unconfirmed SE-selection risk during recent entitlement/DD. Amara Bay and Bosa 1st & Island are plausibly open but lack positive confirmation. Midway Rising is legally blocked and should not be treated as an active pursuit until the ground lease signs.

**Seats that were previously claimed as open but are actually locked:** Ritz-Carlton Newport Beach (Glotman Simpson confirmed via their own portfolio) and Gateway Crossing (Nelson Structural, Phase 1 occupied Sept 2025) are fully locked. The Glotman Simpson claim for 300 S First St is a probable misattribution — Glotman holds the adjacent BIG-designed Westbank tower at 35 S 2nd, not the Steinberg Hart project at 300 S First.
