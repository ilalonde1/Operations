## NO BC-MPI EQUIVALENT
There is NO single CA government database of all major private+public projects above a threshold. Best sources:
- **ConstructConnect CA** (projects.constructconnect.com) — 99,000+ active CA records; closest private-sector BC-MPI analog. ~$150/mo subscription. **The single biggest fidelity lever for a true project-by-project funnel.**
- Dodge Construction Network (Momentum Index, planning-stage) — subscription.
- ENR West Top Starts — top projects/yr, partial free.
- build.ca.gov Project Tracker — 29,000+ infra records (not buildings), free.
- SF Planning Pipeline (74,121 units Q4 2025, free); San Diego permit dashboard (free); BuildLA portal (free, launched Oct 2024); San Jose Major Projects (free); SANDAG (free).
- TAKEAWAY: SANDAG + BuildLA best free SD/LA sources; ConstructConnect = best paid analog.

## SEISMIC / REGULATORY BARRIERS (critical for entry strategy)
- Codes: CBC 2022 (ASCE 7-16) current; **CBC 2025 (ASCE 7-22) effective Jan 1 2026** — first CA code on ASCE 7-22.
- Review authority by type:
  - Private commercial/MF/mixed-use → local building dept (CBC); CA PE/SE. **MEDIUM barrier.**
  - Acute-care hospitals/SNF (OSHPD 1/2) → **HCAI** full review; Alquist Act seismic. **VERY HIGH.**
  - K-12 + community colleges (Field Act) → **DSA**; **SE license required (Civil PE excluded)**. **VERY HIGH.**
  - Tall buildings >160 ft (PBD) → local + **Seismic Peer Review Panel** (LA: LADBS SPRP; SF: AB-083). **HIGH.**
- **NO CA-Canada PE reciprocity** (BPELSG confirmed). Path to CA SE: FE exam (or waiver: BS+17yr / MS+13yr / PhD+1yr) → NCEES PE-Civil → CA Seismic (CSP) exam → CA Surveying (CES) exam → Civil PE → NCEES PE Structural → CA SE. Practical timeline 2-4 yrs for senior staff. FE waiver plausible for KOR seniors.
- SEAOC (SEAOSC=SoCal/SD+LA, SEAONC=Bay Area, SEAOCC=Central) — membership doesn't require CA license; the network entry path.
