Standard Details — Drafter

How to start a job from the standards, use a detail, and what to do when one is wrong or missing.

For drafters

1Start a new job

  1. Open I:\KOR-Standards\template\MASTER\
  2. Open KOR-Standards-Master-R25.rvt.
  3. Save As into the job folder straight away.
  4. Delete the disciplines the job doesn't need.

Never start from an old job. That is how somebody else's project name ends up on your sheet.

2Use a detail

  1. It is already in your file — you copied the master.
  2. Find it in the project browser and drag it onto a sheet.
  3. Every one is numbered KOR-D-##### on the view.

If you have the KOR toolbar, the Details button pulls one straight in instead.

3Find a detail's number

  1. Click the view in the project browser.
  2. Look at View Prefix in the properties panel.
  3. That is its number. Quote it in any email about it.

4A detail is wrong

  1. Do not fix it in your job.
  2. Email the gatekeeper the number and what is wrong.
  3. They fix it once in the standards model and publish.
  4. You pick the fix up on your next job.

Fixing it locally means the next person repeats the mistake.

5The detail you need isn't there

  1. Greyed out — it exists but isn't approved yet. Quote its number and ask.
  2. Not there at all — it is not a standard. Draw it in your job and carry on.
  3. Send what you drew to the gatekeeper.
  4. They add it, it gets a number, and after they publish everyone has it.

What the words mean

The master the template every job starts fromKOR-D-##### a detail’s permanent numberView Prefix where that number is writtenGatekeeper the one person who changes the master

The one habit

Everything comes from the master. Every fix goes back through the gatekeeper. Nothing gets patched in a job.
