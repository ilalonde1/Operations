## HOW TO RUN IT
**The one line to open and close with:** *"My job is to make sure you spend more time engineering and less time hunting for work — the hunting is now done for you."*

- **Frame yourself as support, not oversight.** Set it up as a working session, not a status update — *"we'll end with specific calls owned by name."*
- **§1 The problem:** land the hook — *"What if every engineer started from a verified call sheet instead of a blank page?"* Pause there before moving on.
- **§2 The Brain:** the strongest line is *"a bought database knows the market; this one knows KOR."* Emphasize it answers questions on demand — it's a brain, not a list. The integrity-gate line is your credibility anchor for when numbers get questioned.
- **§3 The proof:** lead with the honesty of the funnel (1,154 ≠ 1,154 opportunities). **Show the "do NOT chase" list deliberately** — *"as valuable for what it tells us to skip as what to chase"* — it pre-empts the "is this a wish list?" jab. Hold up the call sheet: *"this used to be a week of research; it's now a phone number."*
- **Never quote $950M as real** — it's the upper bound. The real numbers are **$340M warm / $183M expected / 247 pursuable.**
- **§4 Lifecycle:** draw the four boxes on the board (Surfaced → Triaged → Rejected/CRM → Tracked).
- **Close:** assign the **3 named owners** before anyone leaves. Decide beforehand whether you assign them or let people claim them live — claiming builds buy-in but runs slower.

## ANTICIPATED PUSHBACK & YOUR ANSWERS
The two skeptics will likely come from one of these angles. Say it, then move on — don't over-explain.

**"This is just more admin/overhead for us."**
→ *"The opposite. You're not researching anymore — you're handed a verified call sheet. The admin is the Brain's job; the relationship is yours. If anything it deletes a chore."*

**"I'm an engineer, not a salesperson — I don't have time for this."**
→ *"Agreed — that's exactly why I built it. It does the finding and monitoring so you don't. The whole point is to give your billable time back. ~20 warm calls beats the hours you currently lose scanning bid sites."*

**"How do I know these numbers are right? The data's been wrong before."**
→ *"Fair — and that's why every figure reconciles to a live query and the report literally won't ship if a built or cancelled project sneaks onto a call list. I'd rather show you 247 honest opportunities than 1,100 inflated ones. The 'do-not-chase' list is the proof we cut the junk."*

**"Why not just buy a BD database off the shelf?"**
→ *"Because a bought database knows the market. This one knows KOR — our win/loss record, our 78 projects with Wesgroup, which architects send work our way and which send it to Fast+Epp. You can't buy our own history."*

**"Who decides what we chase?"**
→ *"The funnel rules, in the open — in-lane, early-stage, warm path. Not my gut. Anything that fails triage is rejected with a reason everyone can see. This is about making the call transparent, not centralizing it."*

**If pushed on your own role / credibility:**
→ Stay in the servant-leader frame. *"I'm not here to manage your client relationships — those are yours. I'm here to make sure you never start from zero again. Judge it on whether your next pursuit is easier than your last one."*
