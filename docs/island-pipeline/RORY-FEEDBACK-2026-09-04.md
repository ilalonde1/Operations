v4 was generated by a wrong website anchor on our own client row.** The dossier told the
Principal Vancouver Island that the man filing 350 Douglas St was not our client. He is.

**Fixed:** row 80 re-anchored to `starlightinvest.com`; canonical 927773 *Starlight
