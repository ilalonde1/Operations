Part one · your points

Starlight — we had this wrong, and you caught it

You said David Woo works for Starlight and that invest and dev are the same or linked. You are right. The document told you the opposite three versions running, and the cause was our own data.

▸ What is actually true

  • The projects we have billed that client for are 2740 Spencer Rd Langford, 1701 Cedar Hill Rd Saanich and Quadra East and West in Victoria — urban multi-family, nothing like the retreat fund we had attached to the record.
  • Every contact we hold on that client is @starlightinvest.com, including Daniel Drimmer, the CEO of Starlight Investments.
  • Starlight Investments' own announcements name "The District" in Langford — that is our project at 2740 Spencer Rd.

So 350 Douglas St is being filed by our own client's VP of Development, and we described him as an unrelated party.

▸ How it happened, and why the correction is worth more than the error cost

Every firm in our system carries a website, and that website is how the system decides two records are the same company. Our Starlight client record had been given the wrong website — a similarly-named American retreat fund. Everything downstream then reasoned correctly from a bad premise: it compared two web addresses, saw two different companies, and told us so with confidence. Nothing errored, which is exactly why it survived three drafts.

Your flag fixed more than the line. The website is now right, the two duplicate Starlight records are merged into one with 118 contacts, and the reasoning that produced the error is recorded against that firm so it cannot recur. Nine other Island firms were found fragmented the same way in the same pass. One correction fixes the instance and usually a class of them.
