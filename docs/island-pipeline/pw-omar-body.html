▸ The one inference worth carrying into the room

Their growth is American. Dallas alone has 18 open roles, San Francisco 14, Atlanta and Minneapolis 10 each. Vancouver has one and Calgary one. A firm hiring like that is not expanding in Canada — it is consolidating here and pushing hard in the US south and west.

So this is not a "grow with them into new markets" conversation. It is a conversation about the Canadian work that already exists — and the single most active Canadian thing they have going is mass timber housing, where a competitor already holds the structural seat. That is section 04, and it is the whole reason to talk to them tonight.

They also own Portland Design (UK), Schmidt Hammer Lassen (Denmark, 170 people), Penoyre & Prasad, lauckgroup, Sink Combs Dethlefs and Pfau Long — so the "five retail principals hired from an Amsterdam firm" in January were, in effect, an internal move: Portland Design is theirs.

▸ Harry Jerome is a question, not a credential

Phases 2 and 3 for Darwin carry $430,000 of signed contract between them and have billed nothing — no invoices, no time. Phase 1 billed $9,840 against a $97,000 fee.

So do not say "we did Harry Jerome". Ask what happened to it. It is the largest piece of unstarted work we hold with them, and either answer — stalled, or still ahead — is worth having.

✕ Two things not to say

They have never been our paying client. All seven were invoiced to the developer. The one record under Perkins&Will's own account — ICBC Head Office — has no fee, no time, no invoice. Say "we've worked alongside you".

The opening

The mass timber opening — and who holds that seat

This is the reason to have the conversation.

In February 2026 they published a study proposing a prefabricated modular mass-timber system for Canadian housing, backed by $250,000 from DIGITAL's Housing Growth Innovation programme.

✕ The structural seat went to a competitor

Named partners: ASPECT Structural Engineers, Introba, and SFU's School of Interactive Arts & Technology. A funded Canadian mass-timber housing programme with someone else in the structural chair.

It is not peripheral to them. When Derek Newby was elevated to the RAIC College of Fellows on 27 May 2026, the citation was for "advancing lower-carbon construction, mass timber innovation, and climate leadership." He is quoted on the study — "We can do much better. We can start today." — with Kathy Wardle. They are also designing BCIT's zero-carbon tall timber student housing, announced in January. Mass timber appears three separate times in the firm's own keyword profile.

The question to ask: who carries structure on the timber work, and whether that is settled. Two of the three people quoted are already our contacts, and Peter Busby — still a Principal in the Vancouver studio — is the name most associated with tall wood in this city.
