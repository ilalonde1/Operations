CAD vs Revit — what the books say

How much CAD we still do, and whether it makes money. Measured from Deltek, weighted by hours and dollars, not by counting folders.

As of 14 October 2026
Billings posted through October 2026
2,328 jobs with production hours
The short version
  • CAD is 23% of the firm's drafting hours this year, down from 54% in 2019. Residential alone is 24%. The shift to Revit happened in 2020–2021 and has held since.
  • Counting jobs overstates CAD. In residential, CAD is 63% of jobs with hours but 39% of billings. The median CAD job is $50K of fee; the median Revit job is $175K.
  • Billed to completion, residential margins are level overall: 23% CAD against 25% Revit, fee-weighted. On the typical job Revit is ahead, 33% against 10%.
  • The level figure is the old book. Jobs started 2019 or earlier: CAD 31%, Revit 37%. Jobs started 2020 on and billed to completion: CAD -14% on 44 jobs, Revit 13% on 39.
  • Year by year it is plainer. Calendar 2020–2025, residential CAD jobs billed $9.4M against $11.6M of labour (-23%), below zero in 5 of 6 years; Revit billed $21.6M against $19.9M (8%), below zero in 2. In 2025: CAD -24%, Revit 8%.
  • Size, not tool, explains part of it. In the $100K–$300K band across all cohorts CAD runs 38% at $220 billed per production hour and Revit 33% at $159; the CAD losses sit in the under-$100K band, where most recent CAD jobs are.
  • Coverage: 1,281 of 2,328 jobs with hours carry Deltek's drafting-type tag. Untagged residential jobs billed $3.8M; nothing here can split them.

Where every number comes from
  • Deltek Vantagepoint over the same ODBC route the Financials module uses. Top-level regular projects only.
  • Hours: tkDetail. Engineering = labour codes 10 and 30, Drafting = 20. Rejected lines excluded.
  • Billed: PRSummaryMain, posted through October 2026. Fee: PR.Fee. US jobs converted at 1.36.
  • Cost: Deltek's posted labour amounts (tkDetail RegAmt + OvtAmt + SpecialOvtAmt), the rate in effect when the hours were posted, the same figure Firm Health uses. Partner hours at the app's imputed $250/hr, which is 75% of all labour cost here. With partners at their posted payroll amount instead, completed residential margins would read 70% CAD and 63% Revit.
  • Drafting type: the Deltek custom field CustDraftingType (RV, CAD, General). Sector: PR.ProjectType.
  • Reproduced the app's cost and margin to the dollar on six projects before anything was written. Two independent pulls of drafting hours agree to the hour on every year and type. The Historical screen, filtered to Has Hours + Fee, shows 1,152 projects and $99.1M of fee; 1,146 of them are this report's population and the rest are 6 hourly-billed jobs with no fixed fee, which the screen counts through their hourly revenue. On the screen's fee measure (fixed fee plus hourly revenue) those 1,146 jobs are $99.0M; this report counts the fixed fee alone, $98.2M.
What this does not say
  • Only 1,281 of 2,328 jobs with hours carry a drafting-type tag. The untagged line is real work that cannot be split.
  • Partner hours are a third of all billable hours and carry a policy rate, not a payroll rate. It moves CAD and Revit margins by about the same amount; the workbook carries the posted-payroll figure beside it.
  • A "CAD job" is the tag on the job, not every hour. A job that moved to Revit mid-way counts where Deltek put it.
  • In-flight jobs are excluded from margin: cost lands before billing does. Closed jobs that never billed 90% of their fee are excluded too; including them changes no comparison by more than two points.
  • Assumptions, stated so they can be challenged: US jobs at a flat 1.36, not the year's rate; margin is before subconsultants and anything not in Deltek's cost rate; 4,037 of 457,850 billable hours (1%) carry no posted amount and cost nothing here, all of them 2019–2021; "completed" means billed at least 90% of fee.
  • Counting folders with a .dwg or .rvt in them counts jobs, not work. Of 31 jobs on one such sheet, Deltek tagged 18 and the sheet agreed with 15 of them; on 13 Deltek had no tag, and the folder check found CAD files on 5.
