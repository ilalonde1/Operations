### About this document

This is a factual record of MVE + Partners, Inc. — its history, ownership, leadership, offices,
portfolio, collaborators, practice and published standing — assembled entirely from sources that are
open to anyone: MVE's own website and blog, MVE's own project pages preserved in the Internet Archive,
MVE's press releases, AIA Orange County award entries, the annual lists published by the *Orange County
Business Journal* and the *Los Angeles Business Journal*, dated trade press, municipal records, and
MVE's own job postings.

It contains no assessment and no comparison. Where two published sources give different figures, both
are shown and neither is chosen — the difference is reported as it stands.

**Evidence tiers**, used where they earn their place and omitted where a fact is plainly established:

| Tier | Meaning |

## 1. MVE + Partners at a glance

MVE + Partners, Inc. is an architecture, planning and interiors practice headquartered at **1900 Main
Street, Suite 800, Irvine, California 92614**. It was founded in **Santa Ana in February 1974** by
**Carl McLarand** as *Carl McLarand & Associates*, became *McLarand Vasquez Emsiek & Partners, Inc.*
after 1979, and practises today as **MVE + Partners**. It is in its **fifty-second year**.

The firm is **employee-owned**. Ownership was transferred over five years, 2019 to 2024, to a group of
**more than fifteen internal shareholders** (§3). **Matthew McLarand, AIA, NCARB** is **President +
Director of Design**; **Carl McLarand, AIA, NCARB** is **Chairman Emeritus**.

## 2. Fifty years

### 2.1 February 1974 — the founding

Carl McLarand opened *Carl McLarand & Associates* in the Brookhollow Office Park in Santa Ana in
**February 1974**, aged 26, with one employee: a drafter. He had completed USC's five-year architecture
programme in four years, served a six-month tour with the Coast Guard, and spent eight years at AC
Martin and Associates in Los Angeles, where he designed the 27-storey **1900 Avenue of the Stars** and
developed conceptual designs for **Atlantic Richfield Plaza**. (**VERIFIED** — Sonia Chung, "The 50-Year
History of MVE + Partners," *Orange County Business Journal*, 2024-03-11.)

The founding principle, in the firm's own phrase, was to be **"exceptional by design"** — carried
unchanged into the mission statement it publishes today (**VERIFIED** — MVE 50th-anniversary release,
2024-02-28; MVE release via GlobeNewswire, 2024-03-18).

The founder's account of the first three years, verbatim:

> "Sales in my first year were $5,000, then jumped to $75,000 in the second year and then doubled in the
> third year. We grew to 140 people."
> — Carl McLarand (**VERIFIED** — *OCBJ*, 2024-03-11)

*The sentence about growing to 140 people follows the three-year revenue sequence in the published text
and is not dated to a specific year there. It is reproduced as published.*

The 1970s work includes a **118-unit townhome development in Orange** that won a Gold Nugget award and
sold out within thirty days (**VERIFIED** — *OCBJ*, 2024-03-11).

### 2.2 1979 — the partners, and the name

In 1979 the firm moved to a Class-A high-rise beside South Coast Plaza and the then-new Orange County
cultural centre, and two partners joined:

> "In 1979, we moved into a Class-A high-rise office building located adjacent to both the South Coast
> Plaza and the Orange County cultural center that was in its infancy."
> — Carl McLarand (**VERIFIED** — *OCBJ* OC500 profile, 2024-11-17)

> "At this location, I brought in two new partners: Ernie Vasquez and Rick Emsiek and the name eventually
> morphed to MVE + Partners."
> — Carl McLarand (**VERIFIED** — *OCBJ*, 2024-03-11)

The initials are literal — **M** for McLarand, **V** for Vasquez, **E** for Emsiek (**REPORTED** —
*OCBJ*, 2019-03-13). **Ernesto "Ernie" Vasquez** was described by *OCBJ* in March 2019 as the architect
who "first became well-known as the 'V' in Irvine's MVE + Partners Inc." (**VERIFIED**); his work has
centred on urban and affordable housing, transit-oriented and mixed-use development, institutional
facilities and community planning (**REPORTED** — Cal Poly alumni materials and *OCBJ* profiles).
**Richard "Rick" Emsiek**, AIA, NCARB, is the named third partner.

The founder's stated reason for taking partners is worth recording in his own words, because it explains
the client-side orientation the practice has kept:

> "clients were risking tens of millions of dollars in each of their developments. I believed that they
> would feel more comfortable working with an architect who understood business issues."
> — Carl McLarand (**VERIFIED** — *OCBJ*, 2024-03-11)

## 3. Ownership and leadership

### 3.1 The 2019–2024 ownership transfer, in MVE's own published words

MVE's account of its succession is a broad internal transfer, not a single transaction. It is quoted here
in full because paraphrase changes it:

> "In 2019, MVE + Partners successfully transitioned its management to a new generation of leaders who
> were drawn from the existing organization. Matthew McLarand assumed the role of president and chief
> executive. In addition, over 15 existing members were selected as future leaders of the firm and were
> offered the right to purchase designated allocations of the company stock. Over the ensuing five years,
> the ownership of the firm has been completely transferred to these new shareholders in a manner that
> rewards and incentivizes them for their efforts and success."
>
> (**VERIFIED** — *Orange County Business Journal*, "The 50-Year History of MVE + Partners", 2024-03-11)

Three things follow from that passage, and only those three:

1. **The transition ran 2019 → 2024** — a five-year staged transfer completing at or about the firm's
   fiftieth anniversary.
2. **Ownership went to a group of more than fifteen internal shareholders.** The firm is internally and
   broadly held.
3. **The leadership step-up came in stages** — President and Director of Design in **December 2017**,
   with Carl McLarand continuing as chief executive and chairman (**VERIFIED** — *OCBJ*, 2017-12-06),
   and the fiftieth-anniversary history placing the assumption of "president and chief executive" in
   **2019** (**VERIFIED** — *OCBJ*, 2024-03-11).

> **One published detail is left standing rather than smoothed.** In **August 2021**, *OCBJ* described
> Carl McLarand as one "who still continues as chairman and chief executive," while quoting "MVE
> President Matt McLarand" in the same article (**VERIFIED** — *OCBJ*, 2021-08-30). That sits beside the
> 2019 date in the fiftieth-anniversary account. Both are published; **no date is chosen here for the
> chief-executive title.** What is not in question is the shape of the transition: President in 2017, a
> five-year ownership transfer running 2019–2024, and Carl McLarand as Chairman Emeritus today.

Carl McLarand's own bio describes "more than 45 years of nearly continuous growth" and his insistence on
what he calls **"evocative architecture" — "design that engages clients, users, and society"**
(**VERIFIED** — his MVE bio). Matthew McLarand's title on the site is **President + Director of Design**.

The visible expression of the transfer inside the roster is the **2022 promotion cohort of ten** (§3.3).

thirty-five include 1984, 1998, 2003, 2004, 2010, 2013 (twice), 2014, 2016, 2020, 2021 and 2026. Luis
Arambula's bio records four distinct promotions across twenty-eight years; Mark S. Kim's and Sherwin
Pineda's both trace Senior Associate Partner (2016) → Principal (2018).

transition described above.

Matthew McLarand firm-wide, Pieter Berger for Irvine, Sherwin Pineda for Los Angeles.

**Education clusters at USC and the California publics.** USC appears in eight bios — Carl McLarand,
Matthew McLarand, Craig Smith, Mark S. Kim, Sherwin Pineda, Hakop Musayelyan, Charles Pigg, and David
Arnold at masters level. Cal Poly Pomona appears in four (Ceron, Rivas, Ta, Wan), Cal Poly San Luis
Obispo in three (Nilmeier, Torres, Beuchat), UC Berkeley in three (Rongé, Berger, Gura).

**Credentials across the published leadership** (**VERIFIED** — the individual bios): **FAIA** — Kap
Malik. **AIA** — Carl McLarand, Matthew McLarand, Pieter Berger, Sherwin Pineda, Chase Rongé, Mark S.
Kim, Raymond Albanesi, Luis Estrada, Jinfan Chen, Hong Bae. **NCARB** — most of the above plus Luis

### 11.2 The rules this document was written under

1. **Roles are reproduced, never inferred.** Master planner, design architect, architect of record,
   executive architect and interior designer are distinct, and where MVE's own page assigns part of a
   design to another firm, that assignment is reproduced exactly.
2. **Where two sources disagree, both are shown and neither is chosen.** This applies to the
   chief-executive date (§3.1), the two San Diego dates (§4.1), the Riverwalk acreage (§5.6), the several
   projects carrying two entries or two figures (§5.14), and the local billings and headcount series
   (§9).
3. **No firm-wide revenue or headcount figure is stated**, because none is filed or currently published.
   What is published locally is presented as exactly what it is.
4. **Arithmetic is labelled.** The ~10,700 Irvine Company units and the ~9,000 student beds are sums of
   MVE's own published figures, marked **INFERRED**, and are not figures MVE publishes.
5. **Anything that could not be established is left out rather than softened.** Where a claim could not be
   supported by a named source with a date, it does not appear.
6. **Two disambiguations were applied throughout.** **MVE, Inc.** of Modesto — a civil engineering and land
   surveying firm sharing the initials — is unrelated and nothing from it appears here. **The Canyon** at
   1023 3rd Street, San Francisco, is by **MVRDV** and has been excluded.
7. **Access limits are stated plainly.** Several sources returned HTTP 403 or 404 to direct fetch;
   findings resting on those are search-index renderings, tagged **REPORTED**, and were not upgraded.
