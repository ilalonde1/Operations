✕ One rule before the meeting, and one before the room

“Not publicly named” does not mean the seat is open. It means no public record names a structural engineer. Every line here is a reason to ask a question, never a reason to assert one.

And this document is yours, not theirs. Section 5 names the engineers who already hold MVE’s work. That is preparation, so you never ask about a filled seat — it is not something to say aloud, and one of those names must not be said at all. See section 6.

Four California studios and one in Mexico — no US office outside California, which is what makes Dallas interesting. ✕ The exception — San Diego is not busy yet

Rongé’s studio opened with 10 staff, and the only two projects he has ever publicly named as its work are 4002 Park Blvd and The Becker — one in framing, one in plan check. Not one of the eight open roles is in San Diego. His 15–20 target lands around January 2027. Congratulating them on San Diego’s momentum would be a misread.

Question three

What to enquire about — announced work with no architect yet named

▸ AvalonBay is being taken over

It signed a merger agreement with Equity Residential on 20 May 2026 — 2.793 EQR shares per AvalonBay share, former AvalonBay holders 51% of the combined company, closing expected in the second half of 2026, a new company name and dual headquarters in Chicago and Arlington. AvalonBay is a confirmed MVE client. What happens to a development pipeline through a merger of that size is the most useful thing to ask them about, and it is public and uncontroversial.

  • AvalonBay’s forward pipeline is 31 communities and 9,997 apartment homes — that is the entire public disclosure. Their quarterly supplemental names every community under construction and none of the ones that are not. The four Californian ones are all already started, so all locked.
  • AVA Pacific Beach — AvalonBay, 138 apartments plus two parking structures on their own Ingraham Street site, approved by San Diego City Council 23 February 2026, not started, no architect publicly named.
  • The Plaza — The Irvine Company, 552 units, San Diego.
  • Jamison’s four Koreatown / Westlake conversions — ~1,086 units, no architect named on any.
  • Holland Partner Group’s 380 Inverness (Denver) and Ballard (Seattle) — past site plan, no architect reported. Holland is a repeat MVE client.
  • Riverwalk San Diego’s remaining ~240 affordable units — no developer or architect announced.
  • Riverwalk Phase 2+ — 17 of 20 parcels still undesigned, and still not sequenced anywhere public: the site’s own news feed has published nothing since the Q2 2026 update. MVE holds master planning and entitlements for the whole thing.
  • Hines’ 276 for-sale townhomes at Riverwalk — at grading, architect not publicly named, so do not assume it is MVE.

Kennedy Wilson / Toll Brothers — asked and answered, as far as the record goes. The “29 sites, $3.6B” figure is the September 2025 announcement. Kennedy Wilson’s own filings restate what actually closed: 24 land-position purchase agreements with potential capitalisation of ~$2.9B, in staggered closings from December 2025 to January 2026. Their 10‑K and 10‑Q name only two development projects anywhere, both in the Mountain West. The site list is not published and there is no sign it will be — which makes it a good question to ask rather than a gap to keep researching.

▸ If the software demo happens, these five change what you show

  • “You’re on Deltek — is that Vantagepoint or Ajera? And have you switched Ask Dela on?” Dela is off by default. If it is on, they will benchmark you against it in real time.
  • “What are you filing project email into today — and do you run Deltek PIM?” This reshapes the first two segments.
  • “Who looks after your IT and your Revit deployment — in-house or someone you bring in?” The highest-value unknown. Zero internal IT staff and no provider identified. That person is the most likely source of technical pushback, and asking tells you who is missing from the room.
  • “What Revit version are you standardised on, and do you publish a BIM execution plan or CAD standard?” ⚠ Directly load-bearing for DXF→ETABS, whose layer contract has never been run on a foreign set.
  • “Are you on Bluebeam — and are you on Max?” If yes, they have Smart Overlay in preview. Pitch rebar on the steel-weight delta, never on the change list.
