# MVE + Partners — Forward Pipeline Outside Southern California

**Compiled 2026-08-25 · Utah · Bay Area · Denver · adjacent Mountain West and Pacific Northwest**

Forward pipeline only: announced, filed, in entitlement, or approved but **not yet under construction**.
Anything already breaking ground is listed separately in §5 so it does not get pitched by mistake.

Every structural-engineer field reads either a named firm or **not publicly named**. It never reads "no SE" —
absence of a public name is absence of evidence, not evidence that the seat is open. It does, however, mean
the seat is not visibly closed, which is the whole point of the exercise.

**Tags.** **VERIFIED** = stated on a primary or official source (the firm's own page, a city record, a
developer's own project page). **REPORTED** = trade press. **INFERRED** = reasoning from established facts,
labelled as such.

**Prior art this builds on** — read before the meeting, not re-derived here:
`docs/audit-2026-08/10-MVE-COMPANY-DOSSIER.md` (§5.13 current work, §7.2 structural engineering, §6 clients)
and `docs/audit-2026-08/mve-research/B-portfolio.md` (§15 pipeline). Those cover the **built** Utah portfolio —
twelve projects, ~4,100 units — and a SoCal-weighted current-work list. This document is the layer they do not
have: what has **not** been built yet, outside Southern California.

## 4. Denver, and the Bay Area studio

### Denver — what the record shows, and what it does not

MVE opened a **Denver studio in September 2022**, led by principal **Richard Flierl**, addressing Colorado,
Utah and Arizona (**VERIFIED** — Katie Murar, *OCBJ*, 2022-09-19).

MVE's **live contact page, fetched 2026-08-25, lists five offices: Irvine, Los Angeles, San Diego,
San Francisco, Guadalajara.** Denver is not among them.

**That is the entire finding. Do not assert the studio closed.** No closure announcement, no lease filing,
no trade-press item was found. Searches of Denverite and BusinessDen for "MVE" returned no matches at all —
neither positive nor negative. **Richard Flierl still shows as Principal, MVE + Partners** on his own LinkedIn
and on TheOrg, based in Littleton, CO, with activity as recently as September 2025 (**REPORTED** — self-reported
plus an org-chart aggregator; not re-confirmed on MVE's own leadership page).

**No MVE-named Colorado project was found at any stage.** The Denver-metro forward work located here
(380 Inverness, Holland Partner Group) is not MVE's. Treat Denver as a question to ask Matthew McLarand
directly, not as a market with a known MVE pipeline.

### San Francisco — active and led

Opened **2023-08-08** at **150 Post Street, Suite 750**, still the address on the live contact page, led by
**Terrence Seah, NCARB**, a principal since 2016, described as *"overseeing several thousand new units in the
firm's northern California portfolio."* **VERIFIED.**

1,792 units, for The Irvine Company. Seah is the name attached to that portfolio.
