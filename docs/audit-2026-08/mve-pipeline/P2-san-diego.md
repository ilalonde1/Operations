## 2. Projects with no publicly named structural engineer

**All of them.** Not one MVE-connected San Diego project — pipeline or under construction — names a structural
engineer in any public source reached in this session. That includes The Becker, where the GC (Level 10),
the developer (Wakeland), the architect (MVE) and the completion date are all published and the SE is not.

This matches the finding already banked in `C-ecosystem-practice.md`: *"A structural engineer on Riverwalk,
The Becker, or OC Vibe — not publicly named on any of the three. Searched; not found."* Two independent
search passes, three months apart, returning the same absence, is a pattern worth stating plainly at the
meeting — **but it is an absence of publication, not evidence that seats are open.** The Becker is in framing;
its SE was engaged years ago.

The seats that are genuinely plausibly open, ranked:

1. **Pali / 4002 Park Blvd** — a 21-storey concrete tower on a half-acre TPA site that has been "preparing for
   groundbreaking" for twelve months while its developer sought an investment partner. A capital re-set of that
   length very often re-opens the consultant team, and value-engineering a high-rise podium is where a
   structural engineer earns the seat. **This is the single highest-value target in the county.**
2. **Townhomes at Riverwalk** — 276 for-sale homes, grading now, vertical design landing across late 2026 for a
   2028 delivery, and **no architect publicly named**. Whoever wins the architecture will bring an SE.
3. **Riverwalk's remaining ~240 affordable units** — Wakeland's next Riverwalk building has not been announced.
   MVE is the incumbent architect on Wakeland's Riverwalk work.
4. **Riverwalk Phase 2 parcels** — 17 of 20 parcels undesigned. MVE holds the master plan and entitlements, which
   is exactly the position from which a firm gets asked "who should we use for structure?"

## 3. The San Diego studio — what it is actually working on

**Founding, twice.** The studio was **established October 2024**; the **first full-service downtown premises**
opened **July 2025** at **655 West Broadway, Suite 1170** — 11th floor of a 23-storey tower, about **1,600 sf**,
with room to expand into adjacent suites. Anyone citing one date should say which event. (**VERIFIED** — MVE's
Becker release 2025-08-18 for the October 2024 date; SDBJ 2025-07-22, reprinted OCBJ 2025-08-25, for the
premises. Address re-confirmed on mve-architects.com/connect, read 2026-08-26.)

**Headcount.** Opened with **10 staff**. Rongé's stated trajectory: **15–20 within 18 months** — that window
closes around **January 2027**, so the studio should be mid-climb right now — **then 25–30**. No independent
2026 headcount figure was found. MVE has **live San Diego job postings for Senior Project Manager and Senior
Designer**, indexed August 2026; both detail pages returned 404 on direct fetch, so treat the postings as
**REPORTED** evidence of active hiring rather than proof of specific open seats.

**Workload, in Rongé's own words.** *"The Becker is the first project that our new San Diego office will be
taking through construction"* (**VERIFIED** — MVE release, 2025-08-18). The two projects he named to SDBJ as
the studio's current work are **exactly the two in this document**: the 190-unit Riverwalk affordable building
and the 206-unit Hillcrest tower. A studio of ten with two named projects, one now in framing and one stalled
on capital, is a studio with capacity — and with every reason to be building its bench.

**Sectors the studio is pointed at.** Multifamily and mixed-use above all; affordable housing as a real
practice, not an add-on; transit-oriented density (Riverwalk is built around a new trolley stop, Pali sits in
a Transit Priority Area); master planning and **entitlements as a named service line**. Rongé's second front is
**surf-anchored and resort development** — he and Pieter Berger gave a joint interview on it in August 2026.

Council on **2024-07-30**, with implementing ordinances finalised **2024-09-10**. It covers ~380 acres, lifts

## 4. One thing this session resolved, and one it did not

**RESOLVED — "Pali" and "4002 Park" are the same project, not two.** `B-portfolio.md` carried them as
separate rows with a warning not to present them as two without asking. They are one. The MVE release of
2025-08-18 names *"4002 Park, a new 21-story apartment building"* in Hillcrest; MVE's live **Pali** page gives
21 storeys, Hillcrest, client REDA. The unit count moved 195 → 206 and the height 20 → 21 as the design
developed between the May 2024 press description and the current page. **Update `B-portfolio.md` accordingly.**

**NOT RESOLVED — whether Pali has broken ground.** SDBJ (2025-07-22) said construction would start "late
2025." The MVE release (2025-08-18) said "preparing for the groundbreaking." The Registry's headline —
**"REDA Seeks Investment Partner for 206-Unit Luxury High-Rise in San Diego's Hillcrest"**, dated
**circa 2025-06-05** from search metadata, page itself 403 — says the equity was still open two months before
that. Nothing published since records a groundbreaking, and REDA's own page still lists entitlements and
construction management as forward services. **INFERRED: not started as of 2026-08-26.** The clean way to
settle it is to ask Rongé directly — which is a good opening question, not a research failure.
