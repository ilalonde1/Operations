**Prior art read before starting** (repo rule 2): `docs/audit-2026-08/mve-research/B-portfolio.md`
(2026-08-25, 642 lines, §15 "Current pipeline") and `A-firm-history-people.md`. That document

## How to read the tags

- **VERIFIED** — a filing, a city page, a CEQA record, a developer or consultant's own page, or
  MVE's own site/blog.
- **REPORTED** — trade press or aggregator.
- **INFERRED** — labelled reasoning, nothing more.
- **UNRESOLVED** — could not be established, and is not guessed at.

**On the SE field.** Where this document says *not publicly named*, that means no public record was
found — a filing, a press release, a consultant's project page. It does **not** mean no engineer is
engaged. On a project already in construction an engineer is certainly engaged and simply
unpublished. On a project in entitlement the seat may genuinely be open.

## 4. Is MVE getting busier?

**Short answer: yes, on the evidence available, and the strongest single proof is their own office
list — but the growth is regional expansion inside California, not the six-city national footprint
some profiles claim.**

**VERIFIED — MVE's own contact page, fetched 2026-08-26.** MVE lists **four offices: Irvine, Los
Angeles, San Diego, San Francisco.** All four are California.

**VERIFIED — MVE's own blog, 2025-07-22 and 2025-07-30.** MVE opened its **first full-service San
Diego office in July 2025**, led by principal Chase Rongé. That is a new office fourteen months ago,
and it is the firm's own announcement.

**REPORTED — OCBJ, 2022-09-19.** MVE opened a **Denver studio in September 2022** under principal
Richard Flierl, to serve Colorado, Utah and Arizona. ⚠ **Denver is not on the current contact page.**
Aggregator profiles claiming five or six offices including Denver, Salt Lake City and Washington DC
are **not supported by MVE's own site today**. Do not repeat the six-office claim.

**REPORTED — OCBJ, 2022.** MVE was **Orange County's seventh-largest architecture firm at nearly $21
million in annual OC billings with 61 local employees**. This figure is **four years stale** — it is
the most recent published billings number found, and it should be stated with its date or not at all.
For context, OC's 26 largest architecture firms reported **$461.7M in OC billings, up 4.3% year on
year** (OCBJ, date not established in the retrieved result — **UNRESOLVED**).

**REPORTED — ZipRecruiter, August 2026.** Roughly **20 current MVE openings**, $83k–$145k, including
Job Captain, Project Manager, Project Lead and Designer roles in Irvine. MVE's own careers page
routes to an ADP portal that renders only in a browser, so the count could not be confirmed at
source. **MVE's own job listings live at `mve-architects.com/jobs/{slug}` — individual posts fetch,
the index does not.**

**INFERRED — new-project cadence in the LA development press.** Counting first-surfacing of MVE
projects in LA YIMBY and Urbanize LA:

| Year | New MVE projects surfaced | Which |
|---|---|---|
| 2024 | 1 | 920 S. La Brea (Aug) |
| 2025 | 2 | 3313 Sunset (Oct), 6201 Bristol Pkwy (Oct) |
| **2026, through August** | **4** | 6052 W. Olympic (Apr), 626 S. St. Andrews (Jun), Riverwalk Studio City approval (Jun), 5350 Wilshire DEIR (Jul) |

Direction is up, and 2026 has eight months on the board rather than twelve. **This measures press
coverage, not filings.** A filings count would need city permit systems that were not reachable in

**The strongest non-numeric corroboration:** MVE currently has **three LA multifamily projects in
active construction** (626 S. St. Andrews, 6052 W. Olympic, 3313 Sunset), **one OC for-sale community
in site work** (The Landing at Tustin Legacy), and **nine forward-pipeline projects** across OC and
LA. That is a firm with work in every stage at once.

## 7. What to do with this in the meeting

Ordered by how open the seat looks.

1. **Riverwalk at Studio City** — approved June 2026, construction targeted late 2027, no SE
   anywhere in the record. The longest runway of anything in the table.
2. **5350 Wilshire** — Draft EIR circulating right now. 46 storeys and 419 units is the largest
   single structural scope in the forward pipeline, and MVE has eight LA towers of 20+ storeys
   behind it, so this is a repeatable lane rather than a one-off.
3. **6201 Residences, Culver City** — approved, construction targeted this year. If the SE is not
   already appointed, this is the shortest fuse; if it is, ask who, and the answer maps the incumbent.
4. **Mosaic, Long Beach** — 628 entitled units with no announced start, held by the original
   partnership rather than sold on. An overlooked block.
5. **OCVIBE Phase I** — ask directly whether John A. Martin holds the residential buildings. The
   answer is worth more than the pursuit.
6. **Snug Harbor** — do not congratulate anyone. The General Plan Amendment was rescinded 6–0 in
   February 2026 after a residents' referendum. MVE was still publicly discussing the design in
   August 2026, so it is alive in some form, but the entitlement is back to a 20,000 sf envelope.

**One question that reframes the whole conversation:** MVE runs several projects as *executive
architect* to another firm's design (Landry Design Group at Santa Monica & Barrington and at
Robertson Park; AO as retail design consultant at Studio City). On those, the consultant team is
often assembled by the executive architect. Ask which of the forward projects MVE holds that role on.
