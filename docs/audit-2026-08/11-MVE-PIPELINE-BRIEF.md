# Four questions, four answers

Prepared 25 August 2026, revised 26 August. Everything below is sourced; the detail sits in
`mve-pipeline/P1-oc-la.md`, `P2-san-diego.md`, `P3-utah-bay-developers.md`.

**One rule before the meeting.** Where this says a structural engineer is *"not publicly named,"* it
means exactly that — no public record names one. **It does not mean the seat is open.** Every line
below is a reason to ask, never a reason to assert.

---

## 1 · Are they getting busy? Yes — and here is the number

The clearest measure is how often a new MVE project surfaces in the Los Angeles development press:

| year | new MVE projects surfacing |
|---|---|
| 2024 | 1 |
| 2025 | 2 |
| **2026 (to August)** | **4** |

Four in eight months against one in all of 2024, plus three LA projects in active construction.
That is what Dan means.

Corroborating, all dated: **~20 open roles** advertised as of August 2026 — designers, senior
designers, project managers, job captains across Irvine and LA. **Orange County billings rose 25%
to \$28.1M in 2025** (OCBJ), and they hold **#8 in Orange County** and **#14 in Los Angeles County**
(\$24.5M LA billings, 2025 — LABJ 2026). Two senior non-design hires this year: a Director of
Marketing & Strategy in March and their General Counsel in July.

**Five offices, and now settled from their own contact page** (read 26 August): Irvine, Los Angeles,
San Diego, San Francisco, and **Guadalajara, Mexico**. Four California studios and one in Mexico —
**no US office outside California.** Denver, opened 2022, is not listed; there is no closure
announcement and none should be assumed.

**The exception worth knowing: San Diego is not busy yet.** Rongé's studio opened with 10 staff and
the only two projects he has ever publicly named as its work are 4002 Park Blvd and The Becker —
one in framing, one in plan check. His 15–20 target lands around January 2027. Congratulating them
on San Diego's momentum would be a misread.

**Why OCVIBE is now first.** The entitlement set MVE's client filed with Anaheim in October 2025
(Final Site Plan DEV2025-00019, Attachment 10) carries a full project directory. It names the
applicant, the agent, **smith-clementi** as master plan architect, **MVE + Partners as architect**,

structural engineer listed. John A. Martin & Associates does not appear.** A final site plan does
not always require the structural consultant to be named, so this is not proof of an open seat — but
it is the closest thing in this brief to one, and it also confirms MVE's role from a city record
rather than from MVE's own website.

## 3 · What to enquire about — announced work with no architect yet named

- **AvalonBay is being taken over.** It signed a merger agreement with **Equity Residential on 20
  May 2026** — 2.793 EQR shares per AVB share, former AvalonBay holders 51% of the combined company,
  closing expected in the second half of 2026, a new company name and dual headquarters in Chicago
  and Arlington. AvalonBay is a confirmed MVE client. **What happens to a development pipeline
  through a merger of that size is the single most useful thing to ask them about**, and it is public
  and uncontroversial.
- **AvalonBay's forward pipeline is 31 communities and 9,997 apartment homes** — that is the entire
  public disclosure. Their quarterly supplemental names every community *under construction* and
  none of the ones that are not. The four Californian ones are all already started, so all locked.
- **AVA Pacific Beach** — AvalonBay, **138 apartments** plus two parking structures on their own
  Ingraham Street site, **approved by San Diego City Council 23 February 2026**, not started, no
  architect publicly named.
- **The Plaza** — The Irvine Company, 552 units, San Diego.
- **Jamison's four Koreatown/Westlake conversions** — ~1,086 units, no architect named on any.
- **Holland Partner Group's 380 Inverness (Denver) and Ballard (Seattle)** — past site plan, no
  architect reported. Holland is a repeat MVE client.
- **Riverwalk San Diego's remaining ~240 affordable units** — no developer or architect announced.
- **Riverwalk Phase 2+** — **17 of 20 parcels still undesigned**, and still not sequenced anywhere
  public: the site's own news feed has published nothing since the Q2 2026 update. MVE holds master
  planning and entitlements for the whole thing.
- **Hines' 276 for-sale townhomes at Riverwalk** — at grading, **architect not publicly named, so do
  not assume it is MVE.**

**Kennedy Wilson / Toll Brothers — asked and answered, as far as the record goes.** The "29 sites,
\$3.6B" figure is the September 2025 announcement. Kennedy Wilson's own filings restate what actually
closed: **24 land-position purchase agreements with potential capitalisation of ~\$2.9B**, in
staggered closings from December 2025 to January 2026. Their 10-K and 10-Q name only two development
projects anywhere, both in the Mountain West. **The site list is not published and there is no sign
it will be** — which makes it a good question to ask rather than a gap to keep researching.

## 5 · Do not raise these

- **Discovery Park is not MVE's.** The Irvine Company's 1,858-unit Irvine entitlement — the one this
  brief previously led with — is **BDE Architecture's**. Every architectural sheet in the plan set
  filed with the City of Irvine (Case 00935798-PMP, 10 April 2025) carries BDE's title block; civil
  is Urban Resource and landscape is burton. BDE is a San Francisco multifamily firm, in practice
  since 1988, **with a San Diego office** — so on their largest new entitlement, MVE's oldest client
  chose someone else, and that someone else is now also a competitor in San Diego.
- **4002 Park Blvd (Pali) — the structural seat is not open.** San Diego's permit record, read from
  the city's daily feed on 26 August, shows a building permit in plan check (\$47.1M valuation, 21
  storeys, 212,821 sf, 206 units) and **an ACI 318-19 code-deviation request issued in May 2024** —
  a structural submittal. An engineer has been engaged for over two years. The project is also not
  stalled: gas services were cut in June 2025, the temporary-power and undergrounding permit has
  been in inspection since October 2025, and grading agreements were issued on 30 June and 9 July
  2026. **It has not started vertical construction. It is not dormant either.**
- **Park Summit is a different building.** A 21-storey tower on Balboa Park's northwest corner,
  opened July 2026 — JWDA architect, Floit Properties, at 555 Upas Street. Easy to confuse with
  4002 Park Blvd. It is not MVE's.
- **Snug Harbor, Newport Beach** — the General Plan Amendment was **rescinded 6–0 on 9 February
  2026** after a referendum. It is dead.
- **Reserve at Silo Park** — **broke ground 14 October 2025.** Two developer websites still describe
  it as pre-development.
- **The Village at Riverwalk** (721 units) and **Riverwalk Central Village** — **Gensler**, not MVE.
- **9th & Broadway / Celadon** — completed 2015, and two other firms also claim it.
- **Uptown Newport** — not an Irvine Company project. Shopoff Management.
- **350 Fig** and **920 S. La Brea** — entitled but dormant since 2020 and 2024 respectively.

## 6 · The five questions, in order

1. **"Who is doing structure on the OCVIBE residential buildings?"** — 530 units, MVE as architect
   on the filed drawings, thirteen consultants named including geotechnical, and no structural
   engineer among them. Asked neutrally, this is the best question in the brief.
2. **"What has to happen before Santa Clara Park starts?"** — 1,792 units approved eighteen months
   ago. The public answer is that the Irvine Company is waiting out the leases on the offices it
   replaces. Landscape and civil are engaged; structural is not on record.
3. **"What does the Equity Residential merger do to AvalonBay's pipeline?"** — a client of theirs is
   being absorbed into a company half again its size, closing this half of the year. They will have
   a view, and it is a question about their world rather than about their consultants.
4. **"How are you covering structural in San Diego and Salt Lake?"** — no structural engineer is
   publicly named on any MVE San Diego project across three passes, and Salt Lake is locally held.
5. **"What came out of the Toll Brothers transition to Kennedy Wilson?"** — 24 land positions,
   ~\$2.9B, unpublished. They will know what they kept.

## What is not established

**No revenue or headcount figure is given here.** Published sources disagree and none is a filing.
The local billings above are exactly that — local.

**Still blocked:** Culver City's project pages and Santa Clara's planning directory refuse even a
browser request, so 6201 Residences and Santa Clara Park could not be read from their own city
records. CoStar, SDBJ and The Registry SoCal remain paywalled or closed.

**Now open, and worth not rediscovering:** BuildSD, aiaoc.org and the Hillcrest BIA all answer
normally to a browser user-agent. The decisive San Diego source turned out not to be the Accela
portal at all but **`data.sandiego.gov`, whose development-approvals extract is republished daily**
with status, valuation, storey count and affordable-unit mix per address. And the artefact that
answered two of this brief's questions was the same in both cases: **a city entitlement plan set,
which carries the full consultant directory.** That is how Discovery Park's architect and OCVIBE's
missing structural line were read.
