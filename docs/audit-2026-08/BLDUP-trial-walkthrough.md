# BLDUP free account — walkthrough and the test that decides it

**Why bother:** BLDUP is the only source found that advertises the exact schema this work
reconstructs by hand — developer, architect, general contractor, **structural engineer**,
landscape — on one record. No public permit feed anywhere carries an architect; fourteen were
tested and every field that looks like one holds the contractor. If BLDUP's design-team fields
are genuinely populated in MVE's markets, it replaces weeks of document-by-document recovery.

⚠ **If they are populated on a fifth of records, it is a lead list, not a design-team layer** —
and that is precisely the trap every public feed set. **Measure the fill rate, do not read the
feature list.**

---

## Signing up — 2 minutes, no card

1. Go to **`https://www.bldup.com/users/sign_up`**
2. It states **"No credit card required"** and runs in three steps:
   **1 Account → 2 Account Profile → 3 Business Profile**
3. Step 1 asks **email + password**. Steps 2–3 ask first name, last name, phone,
   **company name** and **org type**.
   - For org type pick whatever is closest to *engineering / AEC services* — it likely shapes
     which markets and sectors they suggest, so it is worth being accurate rather than generic.
4. The free tier gives **up to 3 custom feeds**, each filtered by market, sector and status.
   Pro adds Opportunity Boards and CRM export — **do not upgrade yet**.

⚠ Their site 403s automated requests, so I cannot create or drive the account. Once you are in,
either paste what you see or grant access and I will take it from there.

## The three feeds to create — pick the markets that decide the question

The free tier caps at three, so spend them where the answer matters most. **Use MVE's markets,
not ours:**

| Feed | Market | Filter | Why this one |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Phoenix / Maricopa AZ** | Multifamily, pre-construction | We have an independent Arizona answer already (PUD narratives). **This feed can be scored against known truth** — the only market where we can grade BLDUP rather than trust it |
| 2 | **Raleigh–Durham NC** | Multifamily, pre-construction | Same: we recovered 11 architects from plan sets. A second gradeable market |
| 3 | **Las Vegas / Clark County NV** | Multifamily or mixed-use | Our weakest region. If BLDUP is strong here it is worth money on that alone |

⭐ **Feeds 1 and 2 are the real experiment.** We know the right answers in Phoenix and Raleigh,
so we can measure BLDUP instead of believing it. Feed 3 is the commercial question.

## What to look at once you are in — five minutes

Open **any ten multifamily project records** in the Phoenix feed and count:

1. **How many name an ARCHITECT?** (not "designer", not the contractor — the architect)
2. **How many name a STRUCTURAL ENGINEER?**
3. **At what stage does the record first appear** — pre-design, entitlement, permit, or under
   construction? Earlier is worth far more; by permit stage the design team is long chosen.
4. **Is there an export?** Without CSV it cannot be joined to the permit and plan-set work
   already built, and every lookup stays manual.

### How to grade the answer

- **Architect populated on 7+ of 10** → it is a real design-team layer. Worth paying for, and it
  closes the one gap this whole exercise has.
- **3–6 of 10** → useful supplement, not a replacement. Worth a Pro seat only if it exports.
- **Under 3 of 10** → it is a project-lead product wearing a design-team label. **Do not buy it**;
  the PUD narratives and plan sets already beat it, free.

⭐ **Cross-check two records against what we already hold.** In Phoenix: *Solis at Norterra*
(architect **KTGY**, developer Pulte) and the *3014 W Deer Valley Rd* scheme (architect
**Kass Wilson Architects**, developer Roers Companies). Both came from the city's own PUD
narratives. **If BLDUP names those correctly it is reading real sources; if it names a contractor
or leaves them blank, we know exactly what we are buying.**
