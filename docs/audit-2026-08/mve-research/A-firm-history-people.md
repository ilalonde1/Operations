## How to read this document

Every claim below carries a tier and a source. The tiers are not promoted and are not blended.

**VERIFIED** — a primary source: MVE's own live pages, a corporate filing, or a named publication
reporting a fact directly. **REPORTED** — a secondary source: press or an aggregator, accurate to the
best of that source's knowledge but not primary. **INFERRED** — reasoning, labelled as such, drawn only
from evidence stated on the same page.

Where a fact could not be established it is named in §11 and left out of the body. Nothing in this
document is estimated, rounded up, or filled in from pattern.

Two conventions matter. First, **where sources disagree, both are shown and neither is chosen** — the
disagreement is the finding. Second, **the absence of a name from a current list is never reported as a
departure.** People move between roles, titles change, and web pages are rebuilt; this document records
what a page says on the date it was read and nothing further.

## 1. The firm in one paragraph

MVE + Partners, Inc. is an architecture, planning and interiors practice headquartered at 1900 Main
Street, Suite 800, Irvine, California, founded in Santa Ana in February 1974 by Carl McLarand as *Carl
McLarand & Associates* and known for most of its life as *McLarand Vasquez Emsiek & Partners*. It is in
its fifty-second year. Its own live "About" page states **51 years, 205,000 residential units, 300
million-plus square feet built, 325 Gold Nugget Awards, 27 Builders Choice Awards and 24 Urban Land
Institute Awards** (**VERIFIED** — [mve-architects.com/about](https://mve-architects.com/about), read
2026-08-21; figures taken from the page's own counter values). The firm lists **five offices** — Irvine,
Los Angeles, San Diego, San Francisco and Guadalajara — and **thirty-five people** on its leadership
page. Ownership passed out of the founder's hands over a five-year transition completed in 2024, into the
hands of a group of more than fifteen internal shareholders led by Matthew McLarand, the founder's son,
who is President and Director of Design. It ranks **No. 8 among Orange County architecture firms by local
billings (2026)** and **No. 14 in Los Angeles County (2026)**, and in 2025 it won its **87th Gold Nugget
Grand Award in 51 years**.

## 2. Origin and history

### 2.1 The founding, February 1974

Carl McLarand opened *Carl McLarand & Associates* in the Brookhollow Office Park in Santa Ana in
**February 1974**, at the age of 26, with a single employee — a drafter (**VERIFIED** — Sonia Chung,
"The 50-Year History of MVE + Partners," *Orange County Business Journal*, 2026 archive, published
**2024-03-11**,
[ocbj.com](https://www.ocbj.com/leader-board/the-50-year-history-of-mve-partners/)). He had completed
USC's five-year architecture programme in four years, served a six-month tour with the Coast Guard, and
then spent eight years at AC Martin and Associates in Los Angeles, where he designed the 27-storey 1900
Avenue of the Stars and developed conceptual designs for Atlantic Richfield Plaza (**VERIFIED** — same
source).

The firm's founding principle, as MVE states it, was to be **"exceptional by design"** (**VERIFIED** —
MVE 50th-anniversary release, **2024-02-28**,
[zweiglist.com](https://zweiglist.com/mve-partners-celebrates-its-50th-anniversary/)).

McLarand's own account of the first three years, quoted verbatim:

> "Sales in my first year were $5,000, then jumped to $75,000 in the second year and then doubled in the
> third year. We grew to 140 people."
> — Carl McLarand (**VERIFIED** — *OCBJ*, 2024-03-11)

*(Note on reading that quote: the sentence about growing to 140 people follows the three-year revenue
sequence in the published text but is not explicitly dated to the third year. It is reproduced here as
published, without interpretation.)*

### 2.2 1979 — the partners, and the name

In **1979** the firm moved to a Class-A high-rise adjacent to South Coast Plaza and the then-nascent
Orange County cultural centre, and McLarand brought in two partners:

> "At this location, I brought in two new partners: Ernie Vasquez and Rick Emsiek and the name eventually
> morphed to MVE + Partners."
> — Carl McLarand (**VERIFIED** — *OCBJ*, 2024-03-11)

> "In 1979, we moved into a Class-A high-rise office building located adjacent to both the South Coast
> Plaza and the Orange County cultural center that was in its infancy."
> — Carl McLarand (**VERIFIED** — *OCBJ* OC500 2024 profile, published **2024-11-17**,
> [ocbj.com](https://www.ocbj.com/oc-500/oc500-2024-carl-mclarand-matthew-mclarand/))

The initials are literal: **M** for McLarand, **V** for Vasquez, **E** for Emsiek (**REPORTED** — *OCBJ*,
"Architectural Firm SVA Rides Public Sector Boom," **2019-03-13**,
[ocbj.com](https://www.ocbj.com/news/weekly-news/architectural-firm-sva-rides-public-sector-boom/)).

**Who they were.** *Ernesto "Ernie" Vasquez* — described by *OCBJ* in March 2019, then aged 69, as the
architect who "first became well-known as the 'V' in Irvine's MVE + Partners Inc." (**VERIFIED** — *OCBJ*,
2019-03-13). His work has centred on urban and affordable housing, transit-oriented and mixed-use
development, institutional facilities and community planning (**REPORTED** — Cal Poly alumni materials and
*OCBJ* profiles). He went on to co-own **SVA Architects, Inc.** in Santa Ana — the firm MVE's public-sector
unit became when it was spun off (§2.4). *Richard "Rick" Emsiek*, AIA, NCARB, is publicly associated with
the firm as President and Partner in professional listings (**REPORTED** — LinkedIn and business
directories; **not** listed on MVE's current leadership page, and this document draws no conclusion from
that — see §11).

### 2.5 What Carl McLarand says the 1979 partnership was for

The founder's stated rationale for taking on partners is unusual enough to record verbatim, because it
explains the firm's client-side orientation:

> "clients were risking tens of millions of dollars in each of their developments. I believed that they
> would feel more comfortable working with an architect who understood business issues."
> — Carl McLarand (**VERIFIED** — *OCBJ*, 2024-03-11)

Over fifty years the firm reports having designed buildings on **six continents** and won **more than
1,000 architectural awards**, navigating multiple recessionary cycles through diversification
(**VERIFIED** — *OCBJ*, 2024-03-11; corroborated by MVE's own 50th-anniversary release, 2024-02-28).

## 3. Ownership and succession — the most misread fact about MVE

The common shorthand is that Matthew McLarand "bought the firm from his father." **MVE's own published
account describes something broader**, and the distinction is worth getting right:

> "In 2019, MVE + Partners successfully transitioned its management to a new generation of leaders who
> were drawn from the existing organization. Matthew McLarand assumed the role of president and chief
> executive. In addition, over 15 existing members were selected as future leaders of the firm and were
> offered the right to purchase designated allocations of the company stock. Over the ensuing five years,
> the ownership of the firm has been completely transferred to these new shareholders in a manner that
> rewards and incentivizes them for their efforts and success."
>
> (**VERIFIED** — *OCBJ*, "The 50-Year History of MVE + Partners," 2024-03-11)

Three things follow from that passage, and only those three:

1. **The transition ran 2019 → 2024**, a five-year staged transfer, completing at or about the firm's
   fiftieth anniversary.
2. **Ownership went to a group of more than fifteen internal shareholders**, not to one person. The firm
   is internally, broadly held.
3. **Matthew McLarand's step up came in stages** — President and Director of Design in December 2017,
   with Carl remaining chief executive and chairman (**VERIFIED** — *OCBJ*, 2017-12-06), and the
   fifty-year history placing the assumption of "president and chief executive" in 2019 (**VERIFIED** —
   *OCBJ*, 2024-03-11).

> **One unresolved detail, stated rather than smoothed over.** In **August 2021**, *OCBJ* described
> founder Carl McLarand as one "who still continues as chairman and chief executive," while in the same
> article quoting "MVE President Matt McLarand" (**VERIFIED** — *OCBJ*, 2021-08-30). That sits awkwardly
> beside the 2019 date in the fiftieth-anniversary account. Both are published; **this document reports
> the discrepancy rather than choosing a date for the CEO title.** What is not in doubt is the shape of
> the transition: 2017 President, a five-year ownership transfer running 2019–2024, and Carl McLarand as
> Chairman Emeritus today.

Carl McLarand is today **Chairman Emeritus** (**VERIFIED** — MVE leadership page, read 2026-08-21). His
own bio describes "more than 45 years of nearly continuous growth" and his insistence on what he calls
**"evocative architecture" — "design that engages clients, users, and society"** (**VERIFIED** —
[his MVE bio](https://mve-architects.com/about/leadership/carl-mclarand)).

Matthew McLarand's current title on MVE's site is **President + Director of Design** — the site does not
use "CEO" (**VERIFIED** — MVE leadership page, read 2026-08-21).

### 4.5 What the roster shows

**Tenure is long and internal promotion is the norm.** Of the thirty-five, publicly stated joining years
include 1984, 1998, 2003, 2004, 2010, 2013 (×2), 2014, 2016, 2020, 2021 and 2026. Luis Arambula's bio
records four distinct promotions across twenty-eight years; Mark S. Kim's and Sherwin Pineda's both trace
Senior Associate Partner (2016) → Principal (2018). **A cohort of ten was elevated in 2022** — Pieter
Berger to Principal; Paul Kearney and Tim Beuchat to Senior Associate Partner; and Anuradha Chaukkar,
Jinfan Chen, Joe McClyde III, Hakop Musayelyan, Erick Rivas, Steven Rosier and Hannah Wan to Associate
Partner — which sits squarely inside the 2019–2024 ownership transition described in §3.

**Design leadership is deliberately plural.** Three people carry a "Director of Design" title
simultaneously — Matthew McLarand (firm-wide), Pieter Berger (Irvine) and Sherwin Pineda (Los Angeles).

**Education clusters at USC and the California publics.** USC appears in **eight** bios — Carl McLarand,
Matthew McLarand, Craig Smith, Mark S. Kim, Sherwin Pineda, Hakop Musayelyan, Charles Pigg, and David
Arnold (masters). Cal Poly Pomona appears in four (Ceron, Rivas, Ta, Wan), Cal Poly San Luis Obispo in
three (Nilmeier, Torres, Beuchat), and UC Berkeley in three (Rongé, Berger, Gura).

**Two senior non-design seats were filled in 2026** — marketing/strategy in March and legal in July —
alongside the finance-and-operations seat. All three sit outside the design hierarchy.

### 9.5 Design philosophy in the principals' own words

## 11. What could not be verified

Recorded here so that nothing in the body above has to carry weight it cannot bear.

1. **Firm-wide revenue.** No filing exists. Three secondary figures circulate (~$22.9M, ~$22–23M, $39.1M)
   and disagree by roughly 70%. The only verified financial series is **Orange County billings** — a
   different and smaller measure. §6.3.
2. **Current firm-wide headcount.** MVE's last self-published figure is "approximately 100" from February
   2024. The published *local* counts — 61 in Orange County (2022) and 80 in Los Angeles County (2026) —
   cannot both sit inside a firm-wide total of about 100, so at least one field is measured on a different
   basis. Aggregator figures between ~85 and ~120 conflict with each other and none is primary. §6.1.
3. **MVE's 2026 Orange County billings figure.** *OCBJ* published the No. 8 rank on 2026-08-24 but did
   not print MVE's dollar figure in the article text; the figure appears only in the printed list.
4. **The status of the Denver studio.** Opened September 2022, named as an office through March 2024, and
   absent from the `/connect` page on 2026-08-21. No announcement of a closure, consolidation or
   relocation was found. **No conclusion is drawn.** §5.1.
5. **A San Francisco studio director.** The 150 Post Street office is listed; no San Francisco director
   appears on the current leadership page. Whether the role is unfilled, held by someone not listed, or
   covered from another office is not publicly stated.
6. **Whether Janet Dennerline is MVE's first General Counsel.** She is verifiably the current one, from
   July 2026. No source establishing that the role is new to the firm was found, so the claim is not made.
7. **Richard Emsiek's and Richard Flierl's current roles.** Both appear in sourced material connected to
   MVE; neither appears on the current leadership page. Directory listings are not evidence of present
   employment, and their absence from a rebuilt page is not evidence of departure. §4.6.
8. **The exact date the name became "MVE + Partners."** Carl McLarand's own account says the name
   "eventually morphed" after 1979; no source gives a date for the change from *McLarand Vasquez Emsiek &
   Partners* to *MVE + Partners*.
9. **AIA 2030 Commitment status, a published sustainability report, or a carbon-reduction target.** None
   located. §9.4.
10. **The "140 people" in Carl McLarand's first-three-years quote.** Reproduced verbatim; its placement in
    the published text does not make clear which year it refers to. §2.1.
11. **The precise reconciliation of the two San Diego dates** (October 2024 studio, July 2025 first
    full-service downtown office) is an **inference**, clearly labelled as such. §5.2.
12. **The date Matthew McLarand took the chief-executive title.** The fiftieth-anniversary history says
    2019; *OCBJ* in August 2021 still described Carl McLarand as chairman and chief executive. Both are
    published and the discrepancy is left standing. §3.
13. **Gold Nugget Awards for 2022, 2024 and 2026.** No MVE win found for those years; the 2026 honoree
    list could not be retrieved at all. **No negative claim is made.** §8.3.
14. **The year of the AIA Orange County 25 Year Award for Bayview Center.** The award and the credit are
    confirmed; the year is not. §7.3.
15. **ENR California Top Design Firms.** Only the top 20 is published without a subscription. MVE is not
    in any published preview, but its presence or absence below rank 20 is **unverified in both
    directions** — this is not a finding that MVE is absent from the list. §7.4.
16. **Whether MVE participates in the opt-in national surveys at all.** Its absence from *Architectural
    Record*'s Top 300, BD+C's *Giants 400* and Zweig's *Hot Firm* list is a verified absence from those
    published tables; whether the firm submits to them is not publicly stated. §7.4.
