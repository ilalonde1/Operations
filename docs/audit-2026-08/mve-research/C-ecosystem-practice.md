# MVE + Partners — Professional Ecosystem and Practice Profile

**Subject:** MVE + Partners, Inc. · 1900 Main Street, Suite 800, Irvine, California · mve-architects.com
**Compiled:** August 2026. All live sources fetched **2026-08-25** unless a different date is given.

This is a record of the professional world MVE works inside: the clients whose names recur across its
portfolio, the consultants and builders credited alongside it on named projects, the tools its own job
postings require, the roles it is hiring, the awards and public hearings its work has passed through, and
where its work is published. Firms are named as collaborators on the public record. Nothing here assesses,
ranks or compares any firm.

## How to read the evidence tags

| Tag | Means |
|---|---|
| **VERIFIED** | A primary source: MVE's own live page, a named consultant's or contractor's own project page, a design-award programme entry, or a municipal record. URL and fetch date given. |
| **REPORTED** | Trade press, a distributed press release, an aggregator, or third-party firmographic detection. |
| **INFERRED** | Labelled reasoning from the evidence directly above it, and nothing else. |
| **NOT ESTABLISHED** | Searched and not found. Recorded as a gap, never as an absence. |

Two disambiguations, applied throughout. **MVE, Inc.** of Modesto — civil engineering, land surveying — is
an unrelated firm that shares the initials; nothing from it appears here. And **The Canyon** at 1023 3rd
Street, San Francisco is by **MVRDV**, not MVE; it surfaced repeatedly in search and has been excluded.

**Architectural Job Captain (All Levels)** posting for the Los Angeles studio requires:

> Proficiency in **Revit**, **AutoCAD**, **SketchUp** and **Microsoft Office** — and states plainly that a
> **"Revit test will be given prior to hire."**

It also asks for knowledge of building systems and codes including **CBC** and **ADA**, recent experience
in high-density, multi-family or high-rise work, and lists the role as full-time with **no remote work**.
Salary range shown: **$72,000–$108,000**. **VERIFIED** — MVE posting, retrieved via the talents.vaia.com
mirror, fetched 2026-08-25.

The **Designer** posting for the Irvine studio requires **Revit**, **SketchUp**, **Photoshop**, **Adobe
Illustrator** and **Microsoft Office**, and describes producing designs, working drawings, schedules and
details on Revit and SketchUp. **REPORTED** — search-index rendering of the MVE posting; the live posting
URL returned 404 at fetch time.

### What the hiring standard says

business success,"* with career development *"achieved through challenging assignments and professional
development plans"* and training and professional-development reimbursement offered. **VERIFIED** —
careers page.

the typology the portfolio is built on. **Licensure expected or in progress** on a job-captain role, and
**15+ years** on Senior Designer, indicate depth is being bought at the delivery end. And the roles are
**studio-resident, not remote**, in a practice now spread across five offices.
