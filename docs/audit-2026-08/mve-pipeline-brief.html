✕ Three rules before the call

“Not publicly named” does not mean the seat is open. It means no public record names a structural engineer. Every line here is a reason to ask a question, never a reason to assert one.

This document does not go to MVE. It ranks their projects by how little is locked down and names the engineers who already hold their work. It is a hunting sheet and it reads like one. The agreed leave-behind position from the prep call was nothing — tools discussed verbally, no code, no screens.

The tools are bait, not the offer. Jim’s own words: “the only reason I brought it up was to spark interest, and he bit right away.” KOR is not selling software on this call; the goal is to get back on the proposal list and to learn what is coming. Do not raise the virtual-engineer or virtual-drafter work.

Orange County billings rose 25% to $28.1M in 2025 (OCBJ), and they hold #8 in Orange County and #14 in Los Angeles County ($24.5M LA billings, 2025 — LABJ 2026). Two senior non-design hires this year: a Director of Marketing & Strategy in March, and their first General Counsel in July. The HR Manager opening is the third — a firm putting its scaffolding in.

Five offices, settled from their own contact page (read 26 August): Irvine, Los Angeles, San Diego, San Francisco, and Guadalajara, Mexico. Four California studios and one in Mexico — no US office outside California, which is what makes Dallas interesting. Guadalajara is a real studio, not a back office: it has a named director on the leadership page (Liliana Bernardis Valenzuela) and is currently hiring a BIM Coordinator. Denver, opened 2022, is not listed; there is no closure announcement and none should be assumed.

✕ The exception — San Diego is not busy yet

Rongé’s studio opened with 10 staff, and the only two projects he has ever publicly named as its work are 4002 Park Blvd and The Becker — one in framing, one in plan check. Not one of the eight open roles is in San Diego. His 15–20 target lands around January 2027. Congratulating them on San Diego’s momentum would be a misread.

▲ Why OCVIBE is now first

The entitlement set MVE’s client filed with Anaheim in October 2025 carries a full project directory. It names the applicant, the agent, smith‑clementi as master plan architect, MVE + Partners as architect, Fletcher Studio for landscape, Fuscoe for civil, Pirzadeh for traffic, Hunsaker for master planning, Holmes for the fire master plan, 3d‑identity for signage, Moran for dry utilities, Water Concern for irrigation — and NMG Geotechnical. A final site plan does not always require the structural consultant to be named, so this is not proof of an open seat. But it is the closest thing in this brief to one, and it also confirms MVE’s role from a city record rather than from their own website.

▸ AvalonBay is being taken over

It signed a merger agreement with Equity Residential on 20 May 2026 — 2.793 EQR shares per AvalonBay share, former AvalonBay holders 51% of the combined company, closing expected in the second half of 2026, a new company name and dual headquarters in Chicago and Arlington. AvalonBay is a confirmed MVE client. What happens to a development pipeline through a merger of that size is the most useful thing to ask them about, and it is public and uncontroversial.

  • AVA Pacific Beach — AvalonBay, 138 apartments plus two parking structures on their own Ingraham Street site, approved by San Diego City Council 23 February 2026, not started, no architect publicly named.
  • The Plaza — The Irvine Company, 552 units, San Diego.

  • Riverwalk San Diego’s remaining ~240 affordable units — no developer or architect announced.

Kennedy Wilson / Toll Brothers — asked and answered, as far as the record goes. The “29 sites, $3.6B” figure is the September 2025 announcement.
