**A different question from** `docs/bd-dossier-mve-mclarand-2026-06-17.md`. That dossier answers *how do we

**Evidence tags, used on every claim and never promoted.**
**VERIFIED** — a primary source: MVE's own live page, a vendor's own page, or KOR's own system, with a URL
and a date. **REPORTED** — press, aggregator, or a third-party firmographic database.
**INFERRED** — labelled reasoning from the evidence above it, and nothing else.
**UNKNOWN** — could not be determined. Every one of these is converted into a question in §7.

> **The rule that matters most here.** Where the technology stack could not be established, this document

> wrong four times by calling a capability absent when it had merely not been found.

## 1. The one-page read

**MVE runs Deltek and Microsoft 365, designs in Revit, and — on the evidence — has no IT department at
all.** A departmental breakdown of ~120 staff returns **0 in information technology, 0 in data science, 0
in product management** (**REPORTED**, firmographic enrichment on the verified org record, 2026-08-21), and
MVE's own live leadership page carries **no IT, BIM, technology or digital-practice title anywhere on it**
(**VERIFIED**, 2026-08-21). **The technical lead this demo was written to survive may not exist.**

**That inverts three assumptions in the audit.** The *"we'd just build it in Power BI"* objection is far
less credible from a firm with nobody to build it. *"What happens when you leave?"* stops being KOR's
weakness and becomes a constraint both firms share. And **anything requiring MVE to build, integrate, host
or maintain software is a non-starter** — which means the demo's honest goal is credibility and a shared
workflow, not a sale.

**The single most important name is one the June dossier never had: David Arnold, Director of Finance and
Operations** (**VERIFIED** on MVE's live leadership page; TheOrg and RocketReach independently call him
CFO). In a firm with no IT function, finance-and-operations *is* the software-decision seat — **and he owns
Deltek.** **This reverses the run-sheet.** `06-DEMO-RUN-SHEET.md` places Financials *"last and short — the
least relevant thing you own to them."* That was written when MVE's ERP was unknown. **They run Deltek. If
Arnold is in the room, the financial layer is the most relevant thing KOR owns** — and two things the audit
told KOR to hide, `get_wip` and the February ledger boundary, become **BEFORE-DEMO must-fixes**, because
they are the first questions a finance director asks. See §5.

**Their live pressures are real and they are structural.** The 2025 CBC took effect **2026-01-01** and caps
the Sa multiplier for Group R at 2, down from 4 — *"can significantly reduce feasible floor plates on
larger residential projects"* (**REPORTED**, ISE Engineers, 2026-02-09). Group R is MVE's entire business.
They are growing across five listed offices plus Denver and Salt Lake coverage, with a San Diego studio
Chase Rongé has said publicly should reach 15–20 then 25–30 people — **a standards-and-coordination problem
with no internal owner.** They hired their **first General Counsel in July 2026.**

**Three corrections to carry in before you speak.** MVE's own site lists **five** offices, not six — do not
say "your six offices." **MVE is Riverwalk's master-plan architect; Gensler is the architect on Central
Village** — do not call the towers theirs. And **Glotman Simpson holds at least two MVE seats, not one** —
Ritz-Carlton Newport *and* 3rd & Fairfax, both **VERIFIED on Glotman's own project pages**. That last one is
§8's first entry and it must never be said aloud.

### The three cases

**Ask who is coming before you build the running order (§7 Q1).** Nobody has asked, and it determines more
than anything else in the audit.

#### Case A — Matthew McLarand attends, Arnold does not

He is President **and Director of Design**. Play design and delivery.

- **Open:** Segment 3, **DXF → ETABS**, paired with the **Kor Tools ETABS plugin** — the geometry generator
  plus the half that does materials, sections, cracking modifiers, load combinations, pier labels and Excel
  export. **Paired, that is drawing in → model defined and ready to analyse**, and it is the only complete
  version of this story KOR has.
- **Then:** the **Revit ribbon** and **detail-sheet composition** — the only live click-path that needs no
  VPN, no SQL and no share.
- **Keep Financials to five minutes** or drop it. The run-sheet's original advice holds in this case.
- ⚠ **Risk:** he is not the person who will ask a hard architectural question, so a smooth demo here proves
  less than it feels like it proves. **Get a commitment to a next conversation with a technical person.**

#### Case B — David Arnold attends

Play the Deltek reporting layer and the conversational CFO.

- **Open:** Segment 1, **transmittals**, read-only, to establish that nothing here writes.
- **Then go long on Financials** — Executive Summary, AR, DSO, collections exposure, utilisation, and the
  **drill-through down to the invoice row**, which is the impressive part. Run the **`DeltekSchemaValidator`
  — clean across all 34 expected columns against 676 live columns** — if durability comes up.
- **Prerequisites, not optional:** the MCP redeploy has shipped and been artifact-verified; every
  February-sourced tile carries an as-of label; the FX and Org-scope traps are closed (the app uses `CAD`
  and 1.378457, the deployed service uses `""` and 1.36 — **you get two different numbers on stage**).
- ⚠ **Risk:** he is the one person who can benchmark this against Deltek in real time. **Ask whether Ask
  Dela is switched on (§7 Q2) before you claim anything about its limits** — and per
  `03-COMPETITIVE-BATTLECARD.md` §7.1, test all four Dela questions in KOR's own tenant first.

#### Case C — both attend (the likeliest, and the one to plan for)

- **Open with Segment 1, client transmittals and download telemetry.** It is the one segment that speaks to
  **design delivery** and **operational control** at the same time — a drawing set going out, and evidence
  of who received it. It is read-only, it is the best-evidenced thing KOR owns, and it gives both people
