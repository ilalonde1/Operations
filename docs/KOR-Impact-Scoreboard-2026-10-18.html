Targets to 31 March 2027

The firm invoiced $5.10M in the last twelve months, about $14,000 a day. Every day taken off DSO is that much cash in the bank instead of in clients' hands.

  • DSO from 159 to 120 days. 39 days × $14,000 ≈ $545,000 of cash, without billing a dollar more.
  • Over 90 days from $1.42M to under $700k. A partner owns each invoice over 90 days and reports at the quickie.
  • 31103-01's $502k settled one way or the other: collected, settled, or written off with JM's decision recorded.
  • Invoicing back above $400k a month. September's $289k is the number that matters most for next year.
  • Every fee older than 90 days closed: won, or who won and why.
  • The cadence kept: 2 monthlies and 6 quickies every two weeks, and the action list moving (closed > opened).

How each number is measured

  • Invoiced and collected: Deltek LedgerAR. Invoiced = invoice lines on the revenue accounts (the ones the Financials screen's DSO uses); collected = cash receipts applied to the AR account. US jobs converted at 1.36. Check: September's $289,348 matches the Financials engine's own billing figure exactly.
  • AR at month end: today's AR walked back through each month's change on the AR account. DSO = AR ÷ twelve months' invoicing × 365, the Financials screen's formula. Today's 159 days is that screen's figure.
  • Aging, critical and over-budget projects: the Financials engine's own drilldowns, recorded once a day.
  • CRM wins and losses: running totals, so a month's change is that month's decisions. Older wins and losses carry no close date, so there is no reliable BD history before today.
