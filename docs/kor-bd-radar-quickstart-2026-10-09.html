The one distinction that matters

A SIGNAL is somebody applying to build

Months or years before a tender. The structural engineer is usually NOT chosen yet. This is where a relationship gets made — find who filed it and phone them.

A BID is somebody asking for prices

The engineer has almost always been chosen already. Decide whether to bid, by the deadline. You are competing on price, not position.

Signals is sorted newest first

A signal has no deadline but it goes cold. Something filed last week is a phone call; something from 2017 is history. Work down from the top.

The score is a hint, not a verdict

It reads the words in the application, so a project described badly scores badly. Open anything that looks interesting regardless of the number.
