## 4. Points for the app build

Distilled from what the room asked for or bumped into, not from what I would like to build.

1. **Partners cannot see it.** Jim is on an old build with no working access, and still enters BD into the Teams spreadsheet. Until every partner is on the current build the spreadsheet will not die. A push-update or a hosted view is the fix, not another walkthrough.
2. **A gatekeeper needs a queue.** Omar as (co-)gatekeeper implies a triage view: new inbound, owned, next action due, stale. The June "lock each lead to one owner" rule is still a rule in prose; the coordination problem Ian described (three people calling one architect) is exactly what an owner lock prevents.
3. **Events are a first-class thing.** The way in on this call was a party three times over (P&W 10 Sept, Graham 1 Oct, LA Tall Building in Nov, CDA lunch 11 Sept). The app has no events entity, no "who is invited", no reminder. Ian promised an events list by hand; that should be a feature.
4. **Qualification rosters with deadlines.** Fraser Health (closes before Nov), SMP seismic (course first, then NDA), Stantec qualified supplier (done), MVE insurance requalification, Port Authority lists. These are the doors; the app should hold each roster with status, owner and date, and nag.
5. **Per-person digests.** Ian will "distil and send pertinent information to people" by hand again. The CRM knows the owner of each engagement; it should produce each person's action list so the next synopsis is generated, not written.
6. **Surface what the app already knows.** The room discussed the BC Cancer Vancouver expansion as a future; the prime-consultant RFP was already ingested (opportunity 23643). A "you talked about X, here is what we hold" step, even a manual one before each BD meeting, closes that gap.
7. **Person moves are a signal.** Ben Smith (Starlight → BAM), Luciano (→ Perkins&Will), Stephanie (left Mission Group), Brian Kapuscinski (Continuum → BJK, already recorded). Each move opened or closed a door on this call. The app needs a "moved to" action on a person, taken from a meeting note, and Starlight's 112-person list should stop showing Ben Smith as current.
8. **Never show the wrong Continuum again.** In the meeting Ian read "Peter Fair" from Continuum *Partners* (Denver, #74300) while the room meant Continuum *Architecture* (Victoria, #927758). The two rows were split that same day. Search results and dossier headers must carry city and Kind so a same-name firm cannot be read for another.
9. **Change-of-use and building-sale signals.** The new service line needs its own feed: Vancouver change-of-use permits and S3 triggers, commercial listings, brokers (Colliers). Owner's-rep RFPs are already coming in through the tender sources; the conversion market is not.
10. **Service-line tags.** Seismic assessment, peer review, value engineering, TI seismic restraint, wood frame, steel stud. Tag opportunities and orgs by what we could sell them, so "who needs an S3 assessment" is a query.
