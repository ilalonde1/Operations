Sectors: HEALTHCARE (dominant), K-12 seismic retrofit, higher ed, civic/cultural, YVR transportation, mass timber, P3/design-build. Recent wins: Cowichan District Hospital (Gold 2026 Touchstone Award), New Surrey Hospital + BC Cancer Centre, Royal Columbian Jim Pattison Acute Care Tower, Science World seismic/carbon retrofit.

Key people: Clint Low (Managing Partner, late-career), Tim White (Managing Partner; co-authors BC School Seismic Retrofit Guidelines, EGBC Technical Review Board), Mike Mariotto (holds US S.E. - possible CA reach).

KOR competitive angle: Do NOT compete head-on in K-12 seismic schools - Tim White is the policy gatekeeper. Instead exploit (a) healthcare monoculture/bandwidth stress - four mega-hospitals active simultaneously at only ~31 staff; position KOR as overflow sub or via architects who find BB&P stretched; (b) single-office, no Interior/Island presence; (c) succession concentration in Low + White. Target private-sector seismic (heritage/commercial) where White's institutional relationships don't apply. Monitor Mariotto for CA activity since KOR is also pursuing CA.
