# Open Structural-Engineer Seats — Pursuit List

**What this is:** tracked major projects (Major Projects Inventory) at **early/capital-planning stage** where the **structural engineer is explicitly "not yet awarded"** — i.e. the SE seat is genuinely **open and winnable**, gated by the open-seat rule (early-stage only; later-stage "no SE" = locked, excluded). These are KOR's lane — Lower Mainland developer-residential / mixed-use / Indigenous / affordable — and **most proponents are already warm** (existing KOR relationships or active meeting pursuits).

**The play:** on an early-stage project with no SE chosen yet, get to the **proponent or architect before they pick the structural engineer.** Where the proponent is WARM, that's a phone call, not a cold pitch.

**Source:** MPI, SE = "Not yet awarded", stage = CapitalPlan, 2026-06-21. 16 seats, all Lower Mainland.
