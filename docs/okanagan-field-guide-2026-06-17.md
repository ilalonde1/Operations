## THE READ — what the Okanagan actually is
**The Okanagan is an emerging, public-sector + institutional market for KOR — a relationship-build play, not a warm pipeline.** KOR has almost no track record here (one civic tie to the City of Kelowna). But unlike the Island, the interior has **several genuinely-open, early-stage institutional seats** worth positioning for now — anchored by **UBC Okanagan** (three open buildings) and **City of Kelowna** civic work. The catch: every one is 18–24 months from an RFP, and **RJC Engineers runs a Kelowna office and owns the incumbent institutional relationships.** Play it as: get on the architect teams before RJC locks them.

## VERIFIED STATUS — what's *actually* open (researched 2026-06-17, sourced)

**GENUINELY OPEN — position now, RFP 18–24 months out:**
- **Okanagan Centre for the Performing Arts ($190–260M, City of Kelowna)** — site endorsed Dec 2025, $1M in the capital plan for feasibility; **no design RFP yet.** Revery is doing concept work only. → get on the eventual design team. **City of Kelowna is a KOR client — the warmest way in.**
- **UBCO Student Housing Expansion ($120M, 500 beds)** — UBC "Future Projects," no team on MERX/BC Bid yet. **The largest open prize on the list.** Will procure through UBC Properties Trust — monitor MERX.
- **Penticton Twin Pad Arena ($70M)** — council-endorsed Oct 2025, 2026 budget funds preliminary design; **no architect/SE RFP issued.**
- **UBCO FIRE — Wildfire Research & Education Facility ($32M)** — UBC "Future Projects," capital-plan stage. **High-complexity build** (combustion chamber, wind tunnel) → early SE engagement has real value.
- **UBC Medicine MPT-MOT Expansion, Kelowna ($33M)** — likely a commercial tenant-improvement (modest SE scope); no team yet.

**DORMANT — monitor, build the relationship quietly:**
- **New Monaco Mixed-Use, Peachland (~$1B, AMC Developments)** — highway access approved-in-principle Feb 2025 but **no permits, no design team**; SE engagement is 12–24 months out *if* it clears review. The region's marquee — worth a standing relationship with AMC now, while it's quiet, but not a live pursuit.

**LOCKED / built — do NOT chase:**
- **Caban / Gyro Beach Cityhomes (Cressey, Kelowna)** — **complete** (built 2024–25). (Our DB had it as "Proposed" — stale.)
- **Kelowna Community Theatre Upgrades ($15M)** — consulting team selected ~Dec 2025; SE almost certainly locked. Verify with the City before spending effort.
