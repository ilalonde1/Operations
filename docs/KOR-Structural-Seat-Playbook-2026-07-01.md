# The Structural Seat
## How structural engineering mandates are actually awarded in KOR's markets — and the play for each channel
**KOR Structural · 2026-07-01 · Synthesized from ~300 verified projects and firms researched June–July 2026 (every underlying claim primary-sourced and independently fact-checked; sources live in the project dossiers and the BD platform)**

---

## The one-page insight

**The structural seat is never awarded by "the market." It is held by one specific actor, and which actor depends entirely on the delivery model.** Six channels cover every project KOR pursues. Each has a different decision-maker, a different clock, and a different losing move — and almost all of them decide **before anything appears in a public tender**.

The meta-finding across every dossier this summer: **by the time a structural seat is publicly visible, it is usually already gone.** KOR's win record proves the corollary — our five Ledcor wins came from being *inside the owner's building program*, not from responding to anything. The playbook below is therefore a pre-positioning system, not a bid list.

| # | Channel | Who holds the seat | When it's decided | KOR's play | The losing move |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Owner / developer-direct** | The owner | Continuously, program-by-program | Be the incumbent; expand program-wide | Treating each building as a new sale |
| 2 | **Architect-led (no in-house SE)** | The architect | At/before funding | Warm the architect **before** the grant lands | Waiting for the RFP |
| 3 | **Architect-led (integrated firm)** | The firm — *outside* its in-house scope only | At overflow/specialty moments | Overflow + specialist positioning | Pitching what they self-perform |
| 4 | **GC design-build / ECI** | The GC's preconstruction team | At pursuit formation | Be on the precon call list before pursuits form | Chasing after award |
| 5 | **Alliance / IPD** | The alliance, at formation | When participants are named | Pair with architecture-only firms entering alliances | Assuming "no SE named" = open |
| 6 | **P3 / consortium** | The consortium, at bid | At consortium formation | Join teams early — or consciously skip | Bidding energy on locked programs |

## Channel 1 — Owner / developer-direct: where KOR wins today
**How it works:** the owner procures the structural engineer directly — no architect gatekeeper, no GC. Relationship and delivery record decide everything.
**Verified evidence:** Ledcor Property Investments / **Telus Living program** — 5 KOR wins (~$560k) since Apr 2024, 2 proposals pending, and **3 of the 4 program rezonings approved Nov 2025–May 2026** with no SE publicly named — the program is converting to design with KOR already inside. Beyond Ledcor: te'tuxwtun/sxwayxwum (Snuneymuxw/Petroglyph), Enoch Cree vertical builds, Christ Church Cathedral seismic, independent schools & faith capital programs, Troika, Morguard.
**Win conditions:** responsiveness, fee-proposal cadence, program-level (not project-level) coverage. **The play:** convert every project win into a program conversation; owner-direct is also where Indigenous, faith and institutional owners live — KOR's verified June call-sheet is largely this channel.

## Channel 2 — Architect-led, no in-house structural: the pre-funding channel
**How it works:** architecture-only firms hire the SE — and they decide **early**, often before the project is funded, from relationships.
**Verified evidence:** GEC (NorQuest CSC ~$240M — capital grant *not yet approved*, SE *not yet engaged*: the textbook pre-positioning window), Station One (SD62/SD23 schools), Tawaw (Enoch program — no in-house SE, verified), MCM (Broadway Plan towers), mcfarlane biggar (YYJ terminal program), KMBR, Meiklejohn.
**Win conditions:** be known to the architect before money exists; engineers-talking-to-architects beats BD-talking-to-procurement. **The play:** a standing warm-architect roster with a *funding-watch* on each firm's stalled projects — when the grant lands, the SE call happens within weeks.

## Channel 3 — Architect-led, integrated firm: the overflow channel
**How it works:** firms with in-house structural (DIALOG, Stantec, HDR/Arcadis) self-perform by default. The seat exists only at the edges: **mega-scale co-SE** (DIALOG took Yolles as *equal partner* on the $900M Kaye Clinic; DIALOG+Stantec co-SE'd the $1.4B Calgary Cancer Centre — both under design-build), **specialist gaps** (Fast+Epp on pioneer mass timber; Equilibrium on rammed earth), and **studio gaps** (DIALOG Vancouver has no healthcare structural track record).
**Win conditions:** peer credibility + a named specialty + patience. **The play:** the July 10 DIALOG posture — become the firm they think of at the overflow/specialty moment; never lecture their code people; never pitch what they self-perform (DIALOG: bridges, transit, sub-$800M home-market hospitals).

## Channel 4 — GC design-build / ECI: the precon channel
**How it works:** on design-build, P3-lite, and early-contractor-involvement work, the **GC's preconstruction function assembles the design team** — there is typically **no public roster** (verified at Ledcor: none exists; entry is relationship-based).
**Verified evidence:** Ledcor Design-Build → Fast+Epp on BC Children's Centre for Health Complexity; Ledcor ECI → Fast+Epp on BCIT Tall Timber; PCL's Alberta mega-DB pattern (Kaye, Calgary Cancer) forcing co-SE arrangements; Clark Builders (Turner) partnering external design firms; James Cowan Theatre (Ledcor DB — SE never publicly named, and the one rumour about it failed verification).
**Win conditions:** be on the precon call list *before* the pursuit forms. **The play:** named precon/BD relationships per GC — at Ledcor that is Aaron Grenier (Estimating & Precon) and Barry Murphy (BD); at Clark, the Lenarduzzi/Harding roster ask. One good design-build teaming per GC converts to a standing slot.

## Channel 5 — Alliance / IPD: the formation channel
**How it works:** BC health capital increasingly uses alliance contracting. Participants — including, sometimes, a structural firm — are **named at alliance formation**. The decisive verified pattern: **the structural participant gets named only when the design lead lacks in-house structural.** Cowichan District Hospital: architect Parkin (no in-house SE) → **Bush Bohlman named as the structural participant.** UHNBC: DIALOG BC (engineering in its legal name) → **no structural participant named** — the seat lives inside the design lead.
**Win conditions:** know which architecture-only firms are entering alliance pursuits, and team **before formation**. **The play:** the BC health-capital map (companion report, in production) exists precisely to list the upcoming alliance-model projects and their likely design leads. This is the highest-value repeatable class on KOR's home turf.

## Channel 6 — P3 / consortium: mostly locked — choose or skip
**How it works:** consortiums lock the full design team at bid. Verified locked examples: Surrey-Langley SkyTrain (Entuitive inside), Broadway Subway, Calgary Green Line, BC Ferries programs, **Alberta's school accelerator (Alberta Infrastructure consortiums pick the SE — not school boards)**.
**The play:** either join a consortium at formation (a deliberate, priced decision) or consciously spend zero energy. **The rare exception to watch:** a public owner running its own design competition — **Surrey City Centre Arena (award ~Aug 2026, design team genuinely open)** is the current live case, stamped and watched in the platform.

## Operating cadence (how this stays alive)
1. **Formation events, not tender ads:** the platform's research jobs watch rezoning approvals, capital-grant announcements, alliance formations, and preferred-proponent awards — the moments seats actually get decided.
2. **Every engineer meeting feeds the machine:** debriefs are ingested within a day; every named firm/person/project becomes a record. The next pursuit starts warmer than the last.
3. **Every claim stays challengeable:** all of the above traces to primary sources or KOR's own Deltek records; anything inferred is labeled. When a fact fails re-verification, it gets corrected in the source dossier — this document inherits those corrections.
