Nanaimo Fifth Street: the team is in place

201 rental homes at 564 Fifth Street, on land BC Housing keeps. The design is drawn and in for a development permit; the building permit and construction are 2027/28.

What
Two 5-storey buildings over one underground parkade: 34 income-based homes replacing the old Sanala Apartments, 167 near-market rentals for low and moderate incomes. 171 parking stalls.
Owner
BC Housing (Provincial Redevelopment Program).
Operator
M'akola Housing Society will manage the Phase 1 homes.
Developer
M'akola Development Services, named on the project team in BC Housing's September 2026 engagement report.
Architect
MacDonald Hagarty Architects, Comox: title block of the DP001426 drawings, issued for DP 30 June 2026.
Structural
We found none named on the drawings, in the City's file or in coverage.

CD12 rezoning adopted 23 March 2026. DP001426 submitted 8 July 2026 and active; Design Advisory Panel, then council in the new year. Later phases depend on provincial budget approval. It replaces a cancelled 358-home, four-building scheme (DP001361) by a different team (applicant Pooni Group, architect Formline Architecture).

The route in

How BC Housing builds

  • On most projects BC Housing owns the land and picks the design team; on some a partner develops it (Chard on Douglas Street, M'akola on Fifth Street and 355 Nicol). The architect is named on the permit file; the structural engineer rarely is before the building permit. On these 62 projects we found the engineer named in public on only two.
  • Construction managers come off a prequalified roster of at least 19 firms. Stratford Gardens' CM call closed 9 October with five invited: Metro Can, Ledcor, TL Housing Solutions, Buttcon West and Dawson Wallace.
  • Some buildings use BC Housing's standard designs (its DASH programme): Cranbrook 217 16th Ave N and Glenfair Phase 1 in Kamloops. Who engineers a DASH building is not public; we assume those seats are not open.
  • 1406 East King Edward is a modular package: a 14-storey steel modular tower designed by Stantec with Bird's Stack Modular, and Bird is construction manager.
  • Redevelopments are the prize. Skeena Terrace, Hall Towers, Evergreen Terrace, Rosewood Village, Skaha Assembly and Glenfair are planned in phases over years, and each phase is a new design commission.

18 seats

The open seats

No architect or structural engineer confirmed in public. Biggest first; the longer-range ones are last.

Large redevelopments

  • Vancouver · Skeena Terrace. About 1,900 homes in 15 buildings, 7 phases, 4 to 36 storeys. Approved at public hearing March 2024; final rezoning 2027; Phase 1 timing to be set. Architect Perkins&Will (Jason LeBlanc).
  • Burnaby · Stratford Gardens. 126 replacement homes plus 230 to 365 new (sources differ), up to 6 storeys. CM procurement closed 9 October, so construction has not started. No architect found. BC Housing development manager: Nicholas Hardy.
  • Burnaby · Hall Towers. 1,206 homes on BC Housing land, 331 of them replacements, in a 33–52 storey master plan. Held while Burnaby aligns it with its new OCP. Rezoning applicant Aplin & Martin; planner Urban Strategies.
  • Richmond · Rosewood Village. 850 rentals in 4 phases; Phase 1 is 138 homes. Rezoning and the Phase 1 DP are in review. Architect HUMAN Studio.
  • Campbell River · Robron Village. About 248 homes in three buildings up to 6 storeys; M'akola Housing Society operates the existing homes. Design development 2025–26, approvals 2026–27. No team public: the earliest seat in the set.
  • Kamloops · Glenfair, Phases 2–4. About 260 homes (340 across all four phases), buildings of up to 6 storeys. Phase 1 (80 seniors' homes at 1118 Glenfair, three readings 6 October) is a standard BC Housing design; the later phases are from 2028, subject to funding.

Mid-size, starting 2026–27

  • Squamish · 39900 Government Road. Two 6-storey buildings, 65 supportive plus 85–100 affordable (the second unfunded). DP in review; construction in 2027.
  • North Vancouver · 1234 East Keith Road. 65 homes, 6 storeys, wood frame, Lu'ma operating. Start estimated fall 2026; construction managers were prequalified in 2024.
  • North Vancouver · 1577 Lloyd Avenue. 73 homes, 5 storeys, wood frame, redesigned since 2021. Start winter/spring 2027. Station One drew the 2021 scheme.
  • Williams Lake · 845 Carson Drive. 51 homes, 4 storeys; a 51-home, 4-storey BC Housing building was approved by council in August 2026 with Altus Group as applicant.
  • Terrace · Tetrault Street. 35 homes. Variance at council 24 August; start estimated fall 2026.
  • Quesnel · 530 Carson Avenue. Up to 50 homes. Start spring 2027.
  • Powell River · 5010 Joyce Avenue. 44 homes plus 20 shelter spaces, 3 storeys. Lease endorsed September 2026; start summer 2027.
  • West Kelowna · 2570 Bartley Road. 60-bed permanent shelter, 2-storey wood frame. Rezoning in 2026; build 2027.
  • Prince George · 3177 McGill Crescent. 10 complex-care homes, single storey. DP fall 2026.

Longer range

  • Penticton · Skaha Assembly. About 600 homes, 6–14 storeys. Rezoning at third reading, adoption pending. Construction deferred to 2029–30.
  • Kelowna · Bertram. The 20-storey, 176-home tower was cancelled in July 2026; the mayor wants 6–8 storeys on the land.
  • Kamloops · Fortune Drive and Leigh Road. 54 homes, 4 storeys. Rezoned (applicant Tengri Architecture), then deferred in March 2026 for budget.

Before quoting

How to read this

  • "Open" means no structural engineer is named in public, not that none is engaged. Engineers are rarely named before a building permit. An open seat is a reason to call, not a promise.
  • Dates are BC Housing's where nothing newer exists. Its pages say they are current as of 22 September 2026; where a council decision, tender or news report is more recent, that is used instead.
  • Every firm and person named here appears in a council file, permit drawing, tender notice, trade brief or the firm's own listing. Where a fact rests on a single source, the text says so.
